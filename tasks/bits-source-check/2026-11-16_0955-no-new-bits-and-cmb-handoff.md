### F1 — The CMB Bit does not carry the September 6 conditional repeated-weight construction

**Priority:** medium; stale source handoff in a published Bit package.

The maintained source now contains a concrete intermediate result that the CMB Bit's article and NotebookLM dossier do not mention:

- Main §XVIII, “A conditional spectral construction,” specifies a grain comparison \(\rho\) inside anchor closure \(A\).
- At one selected \(\rho\), each repeat has the same local factor \(q_A(\rho)\) over the repeat index, with \(0<q<1\), and \(W_A(\rho)\) supplies the observable weighting.
- Under those assumptions, the exact sum is \(G_A(\rho)=W_A(\rho)q/(1-q)=W_A(\rho)/[\exp(\chi_A(\rho))-1]\), where \(\chi_A=-\ln q_A\).
- The local factor may vary across the grain sweep. If it changes between repeats at one selected grain, the terms use products of the actual factors and the simple denominator need not survive.
- The conventional Planck illustration supplies \(\chi=x=h\\nu/(k_BT)\) and \(W=x^3\); those physical identifications have not been derived from native relations.

The CMB package still says in \`02-notebooklm-source.md\`, “What is and is not currently derived,” that the required repeated-resolution / state-counting / occupancy rule is missing. Its article's “The familiar yellow curve is a clue, not a derivation” section likewise leaves the repeated-resolution rule wholly absent. Its \`06-revision-note-blackbody-audit.md\` predates the September 6 extension.

Those statements are too broad now. The conditional mathematics exists. The **native physical identification** remains missing.

The ownership split is already clear in the maintained source and in [Why Does Heat Draw a Curve?](../../bits/why-does-heat-draw-a-curve/00-manifest.md): Heat Draw a Curve owns the local curve-generation/repeat-weight question; the CMB Bit owns the relative-resolution cooling application.

**Proposed change:**

1. Add a short cross-reference in the CMB dossier and, if the published article is refreshed, a concise update paragraph linking to Why Does Heat Draw a Curve?
2. Replace “the repeated-resolution rule is missing” with: “a conditional local-repeat construction is available; the native physical identification of \(q_A(\\rho)\), \(W_A(\\rho)\), the frequency argument, and normalization remains Completion pending.”
3. Keep the \(x^2\\sqrt{1-x^2}\) closure hump as a distinct toy. Do not replace it with, or call it, the conditional Planck construction.
4. Preserve the remaining CMB completion list: native \(T(z)\) / characteristic-scale mapping, anisotropy and angular power spectrum, recombination/acoustic observables, and a discriminating prediction.

This is a package handoff correction, not a new CMB derivation and not a reason to rewrite the canonical source.

### F2 — The Clock Bit's composite description predates anchor closure and recursive local caps

**Priority:** medium; package wording should be brought into line with later maintained definitions.

The Clock article's “Start with persistence, not time,” “The clean case makes a triangle,” and “One composite, not two full costs” sections, together with Parts III–V of its NotebookLM dossier, define \(R_{all}\) as the stable coarse composite after \(R_a\), \(R_p\), and \(R_{ap}\) seat. They correctly limit the orthogonal construction to two independent one-dimensional projections with no additional consequential \(R_{ap}\) bias.

However, the package does not explicitly carry two later rules:

1. **Anchor closure:** every consequential anchor for the clock comparison must be included. A shared contribution may be omitted only when it creates no consequential distinction. The maintained clock source says this applies to clocks too.
2. **Recursive local caps:** \(R_{all}\) is the locally capped resolving participation for this anchor-closed comparison. Inner participants retain their own supported closures. It is not a universal allowance or one outer pool replacing/draining inner \(R_{all}\) participation.

The current canonical owners are explicit:

- Main §VIII, “Budget comparisons need anchor closure.”
- Main §XI, “Clocks do not measure total resolution” and “The simple projection construction.”
- Basics I, “Each relation keeps its own cap.”
- September 7 map, “Recursive compartments retain inner participation.”

The Clock package's “one composite” / “shared persistence work” wording is not a direct contradiction, and its low-bias formula is still properly conditional. The gap is that the later guardrail is absent, leaving room for the exact pooled-budget misreading that the September 7 clarification was meant to prevent.

**Proposed change:** add a short scope paragraph to the Clock dossier and article, or a v4 revision note:

- close the consequential anchors before comparing the two projections;
- treat common-anchor cancellation as conditional on no lost distinction;
- define \(R_{all}\) as the locally capped participation of this particular anchor-closed comparison;

## Fresh sources examined

### Xu et al., *Sizing the Universe with DESI Galaxy Sizes: Plain Fundamentals of Fundamental-Plane Lensing*
arXiv:2609.09151, submitted 8 September 2026.

This introduces a spectroscopic galaxy-galaxy lensing magnification estimator based on Fundamental-Plane size residuals and demonstrates a direct convergence response with calibration for magnification bias. It is potentially useful as a complementary weak-lensing observable, but the paper is not a merging-cluster overlap analysis and does not perform a `halo A + halo B + baryons` residual decomposition.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** No, not for the target discriminator.

**Classification:** Not yet a material methodological advance for this specific watch. It is a general lensing-method development, not a demonstrated improvement to overlap-residual isolation.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09151

### Tersenov et al., *Mitigating baryonic effects in weak lensing with higher-order statistics*
arXiv:2609.09131, submitted 8 September 2026.

The paper studies baryonic-feedback bias in weak-lensing summary statistics and scale-cut strategies, including BNT transforms. This is relevant to the broad problem of baryonic contamination in lensing inference, but it addresses cosmological statistics rather than spatial residual decomposition in merging clusters.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** Baryonic feedback is modeled statistically in convergence maps, but not as the ordinary baryonic contribution in a specific merger overlap region after fitted halo subtraction.

**Classification:** General methodological background only; not a material advance for the discriminator.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09131

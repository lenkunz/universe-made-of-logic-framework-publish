## New/current results examined

### Valdarnini — *Hydrodynamical simulations of the merging cluster El Gordo in a two-state self-interacting dark matter scenario* (ApJ 1008; published 18 September 2026)

A large suite of N-body/hydrodynamical simulations fits El Gordo under a two-state self-interacting-dark-matter model. The comparison uses observational constraints from the measured weak-lensing profiles of the two colliding clusters and emphasizes sensitivity to uncertainties in the primary mass and projected dark-matter separation.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** No, not in the form required by this discriminator. The work fits global merger/halo observables with simulations; it does not report an observed inter-halo convergence residual remaining after subtraction of fitted halo A, fitted halo B, and ordinary baryons.

**Classification:** Relevant merger-dark-matter modeling context, but not a Direct match, Partial/suggestive correspondence, Constraint on magnitude of this residual, or a sufficiently specific methodological advance for this discriminator. It therefore does not materially change the test.

### Cho et al. — *Joint JWST–DECam Lensing Reveals that the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger* (ApJ 1005, July 2026)

Current searches again surfaced this high-fidelity joint weak+strong-lensing analysis, which resolves three halos and revises the main/subcluster mass ratio to about 10:1.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** The individual halo mass modeling is substantially improved, but the paper does not perform the requested overlap-region residual decomposition after subtracting the fitted halos plus baryons.

**Classification:** Previously known methodological/contextual improvement; not new since the previous check and not itself a discriminator-level result.

### Cha et al. — *Weak-lensing Analysis of Intracluster Filaments in Abell 2744: Matched-filter Scans and Stepwise 2D Tracing* (submitted/revised August 2026)

Current indexing continues to surface the matched-filter and locally adaptive stepwise tracing method for filamentary weak-lensing structures in the merging cluster Abell 2744.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** No complete two-halo-plus-baryon residual decomposition. This method was already identified in earlier watch activity and is not a new development in the current window.

**Classification:** Previously known methodological advance; no new state change today.

### Perseus reference case

HyeongHan et al., *Direct evidence of a major merger in the Perseus cluster* (Nature Astronomy 2025), remains the closest known observational analogue: a >3σ mass bridge between the main and subcluster, with merger simulations able to reproduce the bridge through repeated core passages.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** Only partially. It is not a complete `halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons` residual decomposition, and the bridge is traced by member galaxies with a conventional merger-history explanation.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence plus Conventional explanation; not new.

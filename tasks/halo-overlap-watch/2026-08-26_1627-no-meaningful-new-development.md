## Sources / papers examined

### HyeongHan et al., *Direct evidence of a major merger in the Perseus cluster* — Nature Astronomy (2025)
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-025-02530-w

Still the closest observational case located. The weak-lensing analysis reports a >3σ mass bridge between the Perseus main halo and the NGC 1264 subcluster. In the published extended-data line-profile test, subtracting the two halo radial profiles reduces the midpoint significance from about 3σ to about 2σ, while the integrated bridge significance remains 3.2σ.

**Additive components accounted for?** Partially. Two halo profiles are explicitly subtracted, but this is not a complete `halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons` residual decomposition. The bridge is traced by member galaxies, and the authors' idealized merger simulations can generate the bridge through ordinary merger dynamics.

**Classification:** Partial / suggestive correspondence plus a strong conventional explanation. Not new in this run.

### Rihtaršič et al., *Mapping dark matter in the Bullet Cluster using JWST imaging and spectroscopy* — arXiv:2601.22245 (2026)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22245

Updated JWST gravitational-lens reconstruction with 135 secure multiple images from 27 spectroscopic background galaxies.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multi-component conventional mass modeling is detailed, but no dedicated positive overlap residual after subtracting fitted main/subcluster halos plus baryons was newly reported.

**Classification:** Methodologically relevant background, not a new direct test.

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST-DECam Lensing Reveals That the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger* — arXiv:2512.03150 (2025)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03150

Joint weak+strong-lensing modeling resolves three halos and revises the Bullet Cluster mass ratio to roughly 10:1.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multiple halos are modeled, but the paper does not report the required non-additive overlap residual statistic.

**Classification:** Context/model refinement, not a new discriminator result.

### Famaey, *On the residual missing mass of the Bullet Cluster* — arXiv:2605.10022 (2026)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10022

Discusses residual missing mass in the Bullet Cluster in the MOND context using the updated JWST-based lens model.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. This is a different residual concept and is not an overlap residual after subtracting the two cluster halos and ordinary baryons.

**Classification:** Not a direct test of this discriminator.

### Heinrich et al., *The major cluster merger in Abell 2034 as seen by XRISM: Strong turbulence and spectral anomalies?* — arXiv:2604.27161, revised 20 Aug 2026

Recent revision concerns X-ray spectroscopy and gas turbulence in a merger.

**Additive components accounted for?** No lensing residual decomposition relevant to this test.

**Classification:** Merger-dynamics context only; not a meaningful advance for the discriminator.

## Sources and papers examined

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST–DECam Lensing Reveals that the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger*
The Astrophysical Journal 1005, 28 (July 2026); arXiv:2512.03150.

The joint JWST+DECam strong/weak-lensing analysis robustly separates three halos and revises the main-to-subcluster mass ratio to roughly 10:1. This materially improves component modeling for future residual tests.

**Additive components accounted for?** The work models the individual lensing halos, but it does not report the required `observed overlap − halo A − halo B − baryons` positive residual test.

**Classification:** Methodological advance/background for future testing, but **not new in this run** and not a direct match.

### Cha et al., *A High-Caliber View of the Bullet Cluster Through JWST Strong and Weak Lensing Analyses*
arXiv:2503.21870 (2025).

Reports a possible mass and intracluster-light trail extending from the Bullet subcluster toward the main cluster in a high-resolution JWST reconstruction.

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated residual after fitted main/subcluster halo subtraction plus baryonic modeling is demonstrated for the trail.

**Classification:** Partial / suggestive correspondence at most, with explicit projection/systematic caveats. **Not new in this run.**

### HyeongHan et al. / subsequent Perseus discussion
The known Perseus weak-lensing result remains one of the closest cases to the discriminator because a bridge signal is reported between the main halo and the NGC 1264 subhalo, with part of the analysis examining the bridge after subtracting two halo profiles.

**Additive components accounted for?** Partially, not in the full discriminator form. Member galaxies trace the bridge and ordinary merger-history simulations can reproduce bridge-like structure.

**Classification:** Partial / suggestive correspondence plus conventional explanation. **No newly published residual decomposition located in this run.**

### Scofield et al., *An Active Galaxy Cluster Merger at Cosmic Noon Revealed by JWST Weak Lensing and Multiwavelength Probes*
ApJ Letters 999 L1 (2026).

Provides a JWST weak-lensing reconstruction of XLSSC 122 and merger-related structure, but no dedicated overlap residual matching the discriminator was identified.

**Additive components accounted for?** No discriminator-matching subtraction located.

**Classification:** Context/methodological relevance only. Not a new direct test.

### Sweeney & Rozo, *Cluster infall for mass calibration in the stage-IV era*
Phys. Rev. D 114, 023562 (27 July 2026).

Develops cluster-outskirts/infall mass calibration with spectroscopic velocities and simulations. Useful to reduce mass-model uncertainty generally, but not an overlap-residual analysis of merging halos.

**Classification:** General methodological context, not a direct test.

### Recent large weak-lensing map work
Recent COSMOS-Web and DECADE+DES Y3 mass-map papers substantially improve weak-lensing mapping resolution/area and filament detection, but the searches did not reveal a newly published merger-specific subtraction analysis that isolates a non-additive positive overlap residual.

**Classification:** General methodological advance, not a material state change for this discriminator today.

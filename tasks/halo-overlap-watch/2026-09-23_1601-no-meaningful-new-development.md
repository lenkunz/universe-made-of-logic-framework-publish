### HyeongHan et al. (2025), *Direct evidence of a major merger in the Perseus cluster*
Nature Astronomy 9, 925–931.

Still the closest known observational case in this watch: a >3σ mass bridge between the main Perseus halo and the NGC 1264 subcluster. However, this is not new. The bridge is also traced by cluster member galaxies, and idealized merger simulations reproduce it through repeated core crossings.

**Additive components accounted for?** Partially in the published analysis, but not as a complete `halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons` residual decomposition sufficient for the framework discriminator.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence plus a strong conventional merger explanation. No new state change.

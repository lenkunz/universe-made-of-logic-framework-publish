## Sources and papers examined

### Leung et al., *Nulling baryonic feedback in weak lensing surveys using cross-correlations with fast radio bursts*
Physical Review Letters, accepted 2026-09-04.

This is genuinely fresh methodological work. It proposes using cross-correlations between weak-lensing shear maps and fast-radio-burst dispersion measures to reduce sensitivity to baryonic-feedback uncertainty by a factor of a few around k≈1 Mpc^-1 in simulations.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for for this discriminator?** No. The method suppresses baryonic-feedback contamination statistically in cosmic-shear analyses. It does not perform a local merger-overlap decomposition of fitted halo A + fitted halo B + ordinary baryonic structure, nor does it measure an overlap-region positive residual.

**Classification:** Methodologically relevant background, but **not** a material Methodological advance for this specific discriminator yet. It could become useful if adapted to cluster-scale/local residual inference.

Source: https://journals.aps.org/prl/accepted/10.1103/ms7q-gw8d

### Cha et al., *Weak-lensing Analysis of Intracluster Filaments in Abell 2744: Matched-filter Scans and Stepwise 2D Tracing*
arXiv:2604.07445; current search indexing notes a 2026-08-27 submission/update.

Uses matched-filter scans plus a stepwise 2D tracing method to recover curved filamentary weak-lensing structures in the merging cluster Abell 2744.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** No. The method traces filamentary structure and is not a residual test after subtracting the fitted merger halos and baryons.

**Classification:** Already-known methodological context; no new state change today.

Source: https://www.alphaxiv.org/hi/abs/2604.07445

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST–DECam Lensing Reveals that the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger*
The Astrophysical Journal 1005, 28 (2026).

Combines JWST strong/weak lensing with wide-field DECam weak lensing to resolve the Bullet Cluster into three halos and obtain robust virial masses. This materially improves component-halo modeling, but the paper does not report the required positive overlap residual after subtracting both halos and baryons.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for for this discriminator?** No dedicated overlap-residual decomposition.

**Classification:** Important existing merger-lensing context, not a new discriminator-level result in this run.

Source: https://scixplorer.org/abs/2026ApJ..1005...28C/abstract

## Fixed discriminator

`observed overlap − fitted halo A − fitted halo B − ordinary baryonic contribution → positive residual`

The current stable framework wording still treats this as a **Candidate Correspondence** and requires a positive overlap-correlated residual beyond simple additive superposition. The empirical target was not changed in this run.

## Sources / papers examined

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST–DECam Lensing Reveals that the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger*
The Astrophysical Journal 1005, 28 (July 2026); arXiv:2512.03150.

This joint weak+strong-lensing analysis resolves three halos and obtains robust virial masses for the Bullet Cluster components, strongly favoring a roughly 10:1 main-to-subcluster mass ratio. It improves the physical initial conditions for future merger modeling.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** Multiple halos are explicitly modeled for mass estimation, but the paper does not report the target quantity as an overlap-region positive residual after subtraction of fitted halos plus ordinary baryons.

**Classification:** Methodologically relevant background, but not a new test of the discriminator in this run.

### Cha et al., *A High-Caliber View of the Bullet Cluster Through JWST Strong and Weak Lensing Analyses*
arXiv:2503.21870.

Reports a possible mass/intracluster-light trail extending from the Bullet subcluster toward the main cluster. This remains interesting but is already known from prior runs.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** No dedicated `halo A + halo B + baryons` residual decomposition establishing the trail as a non-additive excess.

**Classification:** Partial / suggestive correspondence at most, already known; no new state change.

### HyeongHan et al., *Direct Evidence of a Major Merger in the Perseus Cluster*
Nature Astronomy (2025), plus follow-up discussion in 2026.

The Perseus weak-lensing bridge remains the closest previously identified case because an integrated bridge signal survives subtraction of two halo radial profiles. However, the feature is also associated with cluster-member galaxies and can arise in ordinary merger simulations.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** Two halo profiles are subtracted in the published line-profile analysis, but this is not a full fitted-halo-plus-baryon residual decomposition.

**Classification:** Partial / suggestive correspondence with a substantial conventional explanation; already known.

### Zhang et al., *Baryonic mass budgets in the central regions of the Bullet Cluster and their consistency with strong lensing in MOND*
arXiv:2606.19454.

Re-estimates baryonic mass budgets in Bullet Cluster core regions under an IGIMF scenario and argues that more baryonic mass may be present than canonical stellar-mass assumptions imply.

**Relevance to discriminator:** This is useful as a reminder that baryonic subtraction is itself model-dependent and must be treated carefully. It does not provide an overlap residual measurement.

**Classification:** Conventional/baryonic-modeling context, already available before this run; no new state change.

### September 2026 MNRAS / recent arXiv searches

Fresh searches of newly indexed September 2026 material did not surface a new merging-cluster lensing paper reporting a statistically meaningful positive overlap residual after fitted halos and ordinary baryons were modeled/subtracted. Newly surfaced cluster-merger papers were either unrelated to lensing residuals (for example radio-relic/shock studies) or did not perform the required decomposition.

## Assessment

No genuinely new result found in this run materially changes the state of the discriminator.

The best existing observationally relevant cases remain:

1. **Perseus:** closest to a residual-style test, but incomplete and conventionally reproducible.
2. **Bullet Cluster JWST trail:** visually and structurally suggestive, but not demonstrated as a non-additive residual after fitted halos plus baryons.
3. **Improved Bullet Cluster mass models:** valuable for future forward modeling, but not themselves overlap-residual tests.

No new direct match, new partial correspondence, new bound on the effect, new dedicated residual method, new weakening result, or new conventional explanation was identified since the prior check.

**Final status: No meaningful new development.**

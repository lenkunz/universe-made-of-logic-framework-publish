## Sources and papers examined

The current category batches were title- and abstract-screened. No newly listed paper performed a two-halo-plus-baryon overlap residual analysis.

The closest or potentially adjacent entries examined were:

- **Madangarli et al., “A Brush with Turbulence: Probing the ICM Dynamics in the Merging Toothbrush Cluster using XRISM,” arXiv:2610.05351, submitted 2026-10-04 and listed in the 2026-10-06 intake.** A 167 ks XRISM/Resolve observation finds multiple line-of-sight ICM velocity components, modest turbulent pressure support of about 3–6%, and evidence for at least three subclusters. This improves knowledge of the merger geometry and gas kinematics in a relevant system, but it does not reconstruct a lensing mass map, fit and subtract two halo profiles, model a complete baryonic mass contribution for a convergence residual, or measure the overlap-region residual. It is useful contextual control, but not a material advance of the discriminator itself. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05351)
- **Ohashi, Saha & McCleary, “Optimizing Color Selection for Cluster-Member Identification with SuperBIT,” arXiv:2610.06635, submitted 2026-10-05.** The study benchmarks three SuperBIT colors for red-sequence cluster-member selection and finds `(b-g)` gives the smallest scatter and highest purity. Better member identification can help trace stellar mass, but this short method paper does not target merging-cluster residual mass maps or the required component subtraction. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06635)
- **Alarcon & Sánchez, “Weak lensing, Big Magnification: Recovering hidden lensing information from galaxy positions,” arXiv:2610.03913.** This develops magnification-response-selected cosmological lensing statistics using DES Y6 photometry. It was submitted 2026-10-02 and is not a merging-cluster residual method; it does not isolate overlap regions or subtract halo and baryonic components. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03913)
- **Paine et al., Roman WFI brighter-fatter detector characterization, arXiv:2610.03882.** The correction reduces a detector nonlinearity relevant to future weak-lensing PSF control by about a factor of 25, but it is a general detector-calibration result and does not materially change the specific cluster-overlap test in its present form.
- **Gao & Anbajagane, “Baryonic Imprints on DM Halos: characterizing the full concentration-mass probability distribution with CAMELS,” arXiv:2610.03946.** This concerns halo concentration statistics across simulations, not merging-cluster overlap residuals or an observed additive-component subtraction.

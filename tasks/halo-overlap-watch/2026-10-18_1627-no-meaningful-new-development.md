## Sources/results examined

### Chen et al. (2026), *Mapping Dark Matter in Merging Clusters with Weak Lensing for CSST: Morphology and Filament Recovery*

arXiv:2610.17857, submitted 2026-10-13. This was already identified and archived by the 2026-10-15 watch, so it is **not new in this run**.

The study uses CSST-like mock reduced-shear maps from cosmological N-body simulations and tests MIU2Net on filament-connected halo pairs and elongated halos. It reports halo-center localization around 0.1 h^-1 Mpc and improved preservation of low-contrast filamentary morphology relative to Kaiser-Squires reconstruction.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. These are total dark-matter convergence maps from simulations. The work does not independently fit/subtract halo A and halo B, does not subtract baryons, and does not measure a post-subtraction overlap residual.

**Classification:** Previously logged methodological advance / conventional-filament control, not a new development today.

### Previously closest observational case: Perseus

HyeongHan et al., *Direct Evidence of a Major Merger in the Perseus Cluster* remains the closest known observational example because a statistically significant mass bridge is detected between the main and subcluster halos. However, the bridge is also traced by member galaxies and ordinary merger simulations can generate it through repeated core passages.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** Only partially in prior analysis; this is not a complete `halo A + halo B + baryons` residual decomposition.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence with a strong conventional merger explanation. Not new in this run.

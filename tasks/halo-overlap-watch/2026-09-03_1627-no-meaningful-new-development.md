## Sources/papers examined

### Valdarnini, *Hydrodynamical simulations of the merging cluster El Gordo in a two-state self-interacting dark matter scenario*
arXiv:2608.21844, submitted 22 August 2026.

A large N-body/hydrodynamical simulation suite fits observed properties of El Gordo using a two-state self-interacting dark-matter scenario. The paper compares simulated post-collision halo properties to measured weak-lensing profiles of the two colliding clusters and highlights the importance of uncertainties in the primary mass and projected dark-matter separation.

**Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for for this discriminator?** No. The analysis constrains component halo profiles and merger dynamics, but it does not report a dedicated overlap-region convergence residual after subtracting fitted halo A, fitted halo B, and ordinary baryons.

**Classification:** Relevant merger-modeling background, but **not a material Methodological advance for this specific discriminator**. It does not change the state of the overlap-residual test.

### Cha et al., *A High-Caliber View of the Bullet Cluster Through JWST Strong and Weak Lensing Analyses*
arXiv:2503.21870 (2025).

Still reports a possible mass and intracluster-light trail extending from the Bullet subcluster toward the main cluster.

**Additive components accounted for?** Not in the required residual form. The feature is not established as what remains after subtracting both fitted halos and baryonic contributions.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence at most; not new in this run.

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST-DECam Lensing Reveals That the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger*
arXiv:2512.03150 (2025).

Improves individual halo mass estimates using joint weak+strong lensing and resolves three components, which is useful for merger initial-condition modeling.

**Additive components accounted for?** Component masses are modeled, but the work does not provide the overlap residual required by the discriminator.

**Classification:** Previously known methodological/contextual work; no new overlap-residual result.

### Zhang et al., *Baryonic mass budgets in the central regions of the Bullet Cluster and their consistency with strong lensing in MOND*
arXiv:2606.19454 (June 2026).

Re-estimates baryonic masses in Bullet Cluster core regions under an IGIMF scenario and argues that inferred baryonic budgets may be larger than canonical estimates.

**Additive components accounted for?** This is relevant to the baryonic-subtraction term conceptually, but it concerns central core baryon budgets, not an overlap-region residual test.

**Classification:** Context for baryonic-systematic uncertainty; not a new material update to the overlap discriminator.

### Madangarli et al. — *A Brush with Turbulence: Probing the ICM Dynamics in the Merging Toothbrush Cluster using XRISM*
Dated 2026-10-06 and surfaced in the fresh search window. This is an XRISM/Resolve study of ICM velocity structure in the Toothbrush Cluster. It finds a complex multi-component gas velocity field and discusses multiple subclusters, including structure previously identified in weak-lensing/optical work.

**Relevance to discriminator:** useful merger/baryonic-dynamics context, but not a new lensing residual analysis. It does not subtract fitted lensing halos and ordinary baryonic mass from an observed convergence map to measure an overlap residual.

**Additive components accounted for?** No, not in the sense required by the discriminator. The work decomposes X-ray spectral/gas components rather than performing the required mass/convergence subtraction.

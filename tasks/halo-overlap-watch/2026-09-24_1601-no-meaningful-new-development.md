## Fixed discriminator

`observed overlap − fitted halo A − fitted halo B − ordinary baryonic contribution → positive residual`

The current GitHub source continues to state the halo-overlap prediction as a positive overlap-associated excess beyond simple additive superposition, with magnitude and detectability pending. Framework status remains **Candidate Correspondence**.

## Sources and papers examined

### Cha et al. — Weak-lensing Analysis of Intracluster Filaments in Abell 2744: Matched-filter Scans and Stepwise 2D Tracing
arXiv:2604.07445; revised/indexed in August 2026.

Previously identified methodological advance. The method subtracts an azimuthally averaged host-cluster shear contribution and adaptively traces curved filamentary shear. It does **not** subtract two separately fitted merger halos plus ordinary baryons and therefore does not implement the target discriminator.

**Classification:** Methodological advance, already known from the prior watch state; no new change this run.

**Additive components accounted for?** No, not in the required two-halo-plus-baryon form.

### Valdarnini — Hydrodynamical simulations of the merging cluster El Gordo in a two-state self-interacting dark matter scenario
arXiv:2608.21844; journal publication reported September 2026, ApJ 1008, DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae9300.

A simulation suite models El Gordo under a two-state self-interacting dark-matter scenario and compares against measured weak-lensing profiles of the two colliding clusters. It constrains a different dark-matter-interaction hypothesis and emphasizes sensitivity to uncertainties in primary mass and projected DM separation.

**Classification for this discriminator:** Relevant merger-DM context, but not a material category 1–6 change to the halo-overlap residual test. It neither reports nor constrains a positive inter-halo residual after additive halo+baryon subtraction.

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated overlap residual decomposition matching the discriminator.

### SuperBIT / Saha et al. — Lensing in the Blue III: Weak Lensing Shape Catalogs of 30 Merging Galaxy Clusters
arXiv:2603.18376 (March 2026).

SuperBIT provides calibrated weak-lensing shape catalogs for ~30 merging clusters, with realistic simulation-based shear calibration. This remains potentially valuable for future sample-level residual tests.

A Brookhaven seminar scheduled for 24 September 2026 advertises preliminary convergence maps of several clusters, described as the first weak-lensing mass reconstructions from the stratosphere. At the time of this check the seminar had not yet occurred in its local US/Eastern time, so no new presented result can be assessed or counted.

**Classification:** Existing methodological/data resource; no new assessable result yet.

**Additive components accounted for?** No published two-halo-plus-baryon residual analysis identified in this run.

### Perseus cluster — HyeongHan et al. (Nature Astronomy 2025)
Retained as the closest known comparator. The >3σ mass bridge and prior profile-subtraction analysis remain relevant, but this is not new in the present run and conventional merger simulations can generate the bridge.

**Classification:** Previously known partial/suggestive correspondence with conventional explanation; no status change.

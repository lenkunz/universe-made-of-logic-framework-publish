### Chen et al. — *Mapping Dark Matter in Merging Clusters with Weak Lensing for CSST: Morphology and Filament Recovery* (arXiv:2610.17857; submitted 2026-10-13)

Fresh searches continue to surface this as the closest recent halo-pair methodological result. It uses CSST-like mock reduced-shear maps from cosmological N-body simulations and reconstructs filament-connected halo pairs with MIU2Net, recovering low-contrast connecting morphology better than conventional Kaiser-Squires reconstruction.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. The work reconstructs total dark-matter convergence in simulations. It does not fit and subtract halo A, halo B, and ordinary baryonic mass, and the connecting filaments are present in the conventional simulation truth.

**Classification:** Previously reported **Methodological advance**; not new this run.

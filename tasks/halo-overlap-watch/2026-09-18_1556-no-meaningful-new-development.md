### Joint JWST–DECam Bullet Cluster lensing

Cho et al., *Joint JWST–DECam Lensing Reveals that the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger*, ApJ 1005, 28 (July 2026), DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae6a90; arXiv:2512.03150.

The joint weak+strong-lensing analysis resolves three halos and obtains robust virial masses, with a main/subcluster mass ratio near 10:1. This is useful for future collision modeling and reduces halo-model uncertainty.

**Additive components accounted for?** Individual halos are modeled, but the paper does not report the required inter-halo residual after subtracting fitted halo A + fitted halo B + ordinary baryons.

**Classification:** Useful established background, but not a new discriminator-level result in this run and not newly surfaced relative to recent checks.

### Abell 2744 intracluster-filament weak-lensing method

Cha et al., *Weak-lensing Analysis of Intracluster Filaments in Abell 2744: Matched-filter Scans and Stepwise 2D Tracing*, arXiv:2604.07445 (April 2026).

The method traces curved/complex weak-lensing filamentary structures using matched-filter scans plus locally varying stepwise 2D orientations. It remains relevant as a possible technique for detecting non-straight residual morphology.

**Additive components accounted for?** Not in the form required by the discriminator; it is a filament-characterization analysis rather than a two-halo-plus-baryon residual test.

**Classification:** Previously identified methodological background, not a new development today.

### Current September conference item: split infall in cluster-pair filaments

A 18 September 2026 conference contribution reports >5σ spectroscopic evidence for split velocity flow in galaxy filaments connecting cluster pairs after subtracting rigid-body background motion and Hubble flow.

Source: https://web.gravity.sjtu.edu.cn/event/11/contributions/278/

**Relevance:** informs conventional dynamics of matter between cluster pairs and may eventually help constrain ordinary bridge/filament contributions.

**Additive components accounted for?** This is a galaxy-velocity/filament analysis, not a lensing mass residual analysis and not a merging-halo subtraction test.

**Classification:** Contextual conventional-structure information, not a material category-6 explanation of an existing discriminator-level residual.

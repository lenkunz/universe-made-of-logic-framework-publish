## Sources/papers examined

### Chen et al. — *Mapping Dark Matter in Merging Clusters with Weak Lensing for CSST: Morphology and Filament Recovery* (arXiv:2610.17857; submitted 2026-10-13)

This remains relevant methodological background but was already identified and archived in the 2026-10-15 watch run. It reconstructs filament-connected halo pairs in CSST-like mock weak-lensing data and shows that MIU2Net preserves low-contrast connecting morphology better than Kaiser-Squires reconstruction.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. The input is cosmological N-body dark-matter convergence, and the analysis reconstructs total convergence rather than fitting/subtracting halo A, halo B, and ordinary baryons. The recovered connecting structures are ordinary filaments present in the simulation truth.

**Classification:** Previously reported Methodological advance; not new this run.

### RXC J0032.1+1808 merger revision/indexing

Recent indexing/revision material describes a rich bimodal cluster merger with X-ray bimodality, prior lensing elongation, a radio relic, galaxy velocity constraints, and hydrodynamical-simulation comparisons favoring an off-axis post-pericenter merger geometry.

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated `halo A + halo B + baryons` residual decomposition is reported. The work constrains merger geometry/history rather than an overlap mass residual.

**Classification:** Useful merger-state context, but not a new test of the discriminator and not a material state change for this watch.

### Valdarnini — *Hydrodynamical simulations of the merging cluster El Gordo in a two-state self-interacting dark matter scenario* (arXiv:2608.21844; ApJ 2026)

Current web indexing continues to surface this work. It compares simulated merger models with El Gordo weak-lensing constraints and stresses sensitivity to primary mass and projected dark-matter separation.

**Additive components accounted for?** No overlap-residual subtraction matching the fixed discriminator.

**Classification:** Merger-simulation/model context; not new in the present window and not a direct/partial overlap-residual result.

### Perseus mass bridge (HyeongHan et al.)

Still one of the closest older observational analogues: a >3σ bridge was reported between the main and subcluster and merger simulations can generate the bridge through repeated core passages. It is not new in this run and remains subject to the previously recorded conventional merger explanation.

**Additive components accounted for?** Only partially in the published bridge/profile analyses; not the complete fixed discriminator with ordinary baryons independently removed.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial/suggestive correspondence plus conventional explanation; no new state change.

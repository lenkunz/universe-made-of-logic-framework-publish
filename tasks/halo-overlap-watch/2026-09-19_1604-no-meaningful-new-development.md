## New/recent material checked

### Valdarnini — *Hydrodynamical simulations of the merging cluster El Gordo in a two-state self-interacting dark matter scenario*

- arXiv:2608.21844, submitted 2026-08-22; journal publication surfaced 2026-09-18 in *The Astrophysical Journal*.
- Uses N-body/hydrodynamical simulations and measured weak-lensing profiles of the two colliding El Gordo components to test a two-state self-interacting-dark-matter scenario.
- **Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for in the discriminator's required residual sense? No.** The work compares simulated merger properties and halo lensing profiles with observations; it does not report `observed overlap − halo A − halo B − baryons` and test the remainder for a positive convergence excess.
- **Classification:** not a new discriminator result. Relevant merger-DM context, but neither supportive nor weakening evidence for the specific non-additive overlap prediction.
- Strongest relevance: it illustrates that merger dynamics and alternative DM microphysics can change post-collision halo profiles, which would need to be controlled in any future residual test.

- Submitted 2026-09-17, updated 2026-09-18.

- **Additive components accounted for? Not applicable.** This is cluster detection/method validation rather than merger-overlap residual decomposition.
- **Classification:** no material change to the discriminator. Potentially useful general weak-lensing infrastructure, but not specific enough to qualify as a methodological advance for this test.

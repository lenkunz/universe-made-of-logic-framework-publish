### Wilde et al. — *A VLA Study of the Disturbed Massive Cluster PLCK G165.7 + 67.0 and Its Two Narrow Angle Tail Galaxies*

- arXiv:2609.30649
- Submitted 2026-09-25; appeared in the 2026-09-28 `astro-ph.GA` recent listing
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30649

**What was measured:** VLA 15 GHz and 6 GHz radio-continuum observations resolve two narrow-angle-tail radio galaxies in the disturbed massive cluster PLCK G165.7+67.0. Jet bending, spectral steepening, and two dynamical models were used to infer roughly 2,000 km/s space velocities and a possible near-core-passage interpretation for the brightest cluster galaxy.

**Were additive halo and baryonic components accounted for?** **No.** The paper does not reconstruct a convergence or projected mass-density map, fit halo A plus halo B, subtract ordinary baryons, or inspect an inter-halo residual.

**Why it does not change the test:** The inferred galaxy motions may help constrain merger phase in future joint modeling, but the current work supplies no overlap-region mass measurement and no non-additive residual test.

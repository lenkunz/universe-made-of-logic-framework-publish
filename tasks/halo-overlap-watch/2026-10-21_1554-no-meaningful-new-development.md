## Sources/papers examined

### Chen et al., *Mapping Dark Matter in Merging Clusters with Weak Lensing for CSST: Morphology and Filament Recovery* — arXiv:2610.17857, submitted 13 Oct 2026

This remains the newest closely relevant result returned by the searches. It uses CSST-like mock weak-lensing data for filament-connected halo pairs and elongated halos and shows that MIU2Net can recover low-contrast connecting structures more faithfully than conventional Kaiser–Squires reconstruction.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. The work reconstructs total convergence from N-body mock maps. It does not independently fit/subtract halo A, halo B, and ordinary baryons and then measure an overlap residual.

**Classification:** Methodological advance, already surfaced in the previous watch cycle; not a new development for this run.

### Finner et al., *Weak-Lensing Characterization of the Dark Matter in 29 Merging Clusters that Exhibit Radio Relics* — arXiv:2407.02557

Large merger sample with multi-component NFW fitting and weak-lensing mass maps. It remains useful as a possible sample base for future residual tests but is not newly published and does not report the required post-subtraction overlap residual.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multi-component halos are fitted, but no dedicated `observed overlap − halo A − halo B − baryons` residual statistic is reported.

**Classification:** Background/sample resource; no new state change.

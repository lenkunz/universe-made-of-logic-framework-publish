## Fixed discriminator

`observed overlap − fitted halo A − fitted halo B − ordinary baryonic contribution → positive residual`

Framework status remains **Candidate Correspondence**.

## Newly surfaced/recent work checked

### Manjón-García et al. — *Euclid: Galaxy cluster detection through the weak lensing effect — algorithm assessment and selection* (arXiv:2609.20571; submitted 2026-09-17)

Blind challenge of nine weak-lensing cluster-detection algorithms on 1200 deg² of Euclid-like simulations. Four complementary methods were selected (optimal filtering, Gaussian filtering, aperture mass, and multiscale wavelets), with combined detection performance substantially better than individual methods.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. This is cluster detection, not a merger overlap residual decomposition. It does not subtract two fitted halos plus baryons or measure an inter-halo excess.

**Classification:** General methodological background, but not sufficiently specific to the discriminator to count as a meaningful Methodological Advance for this watch.

### Luo et al. — *ELUCID. IX. Recovering the Substructures and History of the Coma Cluster* (arXiv:2609.20442; submitted 2026-09-17)

Constrained hydrodynamic simulations reproduce Coma's observed intracluster filaments and connect them to assembly history.

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated lensing residual subtraction matching the discriminator. The work provides a conventional assembly-history explanation for filamentary structure but does not analyze a candidate non-additive overlap excess.

**Classification:** Contextual conventional modeling; not a new test of the discriminator.

### Valdarnini — *Hydrodynamical simulations of the merging cluster El Gordo in a two-state self-interacting dark matter scenario* (ApJ 1008; surfaced 2026-09-18)

A suite of N-body/hydrodynamical simulations tests a two-state self-interacting-dark-matter scenario against El Gordo weak-lensing profiles and merger observables.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. The analysis fits merger properties and dark-matter interaction scenarios rather than computing `observed overlap − halo A − halo B − baryons` in the inter-halo region.

**Classification:** Relevant merging-cluster dark-matter modeling, but not a Direct Match, Partial Correspondence, Constraint on the overlap residual, or sufficiently targeted methodological advance for this discriminator.

### Roche et al. — *Host Dependence and Line-of-Sight Effects on Galaxy-Galaxy Strong Lensing in Clusters* (arXiv:2608.30079; 2026-08-30, updated 2026-09-01)

Shows that correlated line-of-sight structure, baryonic physics, host Einstein radius, field-of-view definition, and matched selection methodology materially affect galaxy-galaxy strong-lensing comparisons between observations and simulations.

**Additive components accounted for?** The paper explicitly studies baryonic and line-of-sight contributions to a strong-lensing statistic, but not a two-halo merger overlap residual.

**Classification:** Useful systematics warning for any future residual-lensing test, but it does not materially change the state of this specific discriminator.

### SuperBIT upcoming presentation / program material (2026-09-24)

SuperBIT targets roughly 30 merging clusters and is producing preliminary weak-lensing convergence maps from stratospheric imaging. This remains potentially valuable for a future ensemble overlap-residual analysis.

**Additive components accounted for?** No published result located in this run demonstrating two-halo-plus-baryon subtraction or a surviving overlap residual.

**Classification:** Watchlist / future methodological opportunity, not a current meaningful development.

## Previously relevant benchmark retained

The 2025 Perseus weak-lensing result remains the closest known observational case: a >3σ mass bridge is reported between the main cluster and subcluster, and prior detailed analysis found an integrated bridge signal after subtracting two halo profiles. However, it is not new in this run, baryonic/member-galaxy structure is associated with the bridge, and merger simulations provide a conventional route to generating it. It therefore remains suggestive rather than a clean direct match.

## Assessment

No newly surfaced paper in this check performs the required decomposition or reports a statistically meaningful positive inter-halo residual after separately modeling/subtracting both halo components and ordinary baryons.

The newest Euclid algorithm paper improves broad weak-lensing cluster detection, and recent Coma/El Gordo/strong-lensing-systematics work improves surrounding methodology and conventional modeling, but none materially advances the specific non-additive overlap test enough to trigger a user notification.

arXiv:2603.18376 (2026).

The SuperBIT program provides calibrated weak-lensing shape catalogs for 30 merging clusters, with systematic diagnostics and simulated-image bias tests. This remains useful infrastructure for future residual and stacking analyses, but the paper itself does not report a new overlap-region residual beyond fitted halo superposition.

**Additive components accounted for?** Not in the specific discriminator form.

### Shi & Cui, *Nonlinear weak lensing reconstruction for galaxy clusters*
Phys. Rev. D 113, 103514, published 7 May 2026.

The method improves nonlinear weak-lensing mass reconstruction near cluster cores and reaches very small reconstruction residuals in noiseless simulations. It is relevant because cleaner reconstruction could reduce one source of false residual structure in future merger tests, but it is not new in the present watch window and does not itself test an overlap excess.

**Additive components accounted for?** Not applicable; this is a reconstruction-method study.

**Classification:** Methodological background, no new state change.

### HyeongHan et al., *Direct Evidence of a Major Merger in the Perseus Cluster*
Nature Astronomy 9, 925–931 (2025); arXiv:2405.00115.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00115

Still one of the closest observational analogues. It reports a >3σ mass bridge between the Perseus main cluster and subcluster. Previous detailed tracking found that a two-halo profile subtraction leaves an integrated bridge signal, but the paper also shows ordinary merger simulations can generate the bridge through repeated core passages.

**Additive components accounted for?** Partially. Two halo profiles are treated in the bridge analysis, but this is not a complete `halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons` residual decomposition.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence, with a conventional merger explanation. No new state change today.

### Cha et al., *A High-Caliber View of the Bullet Cluster Through JWST Strong and Weak Lensing Analyses*
arXiv:2503.21870 (2025).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21870

Reports a possible mass / intracluster-light trail extending from the Bullet subcluster toward the main cluster, but the feature is not demonstrated as a residual after subtraction of both fitted halos plus ordinary baryons.

**Additive components accounted for?** No, not in the discriminator form.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence at most. No new state change.

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST-DECam Lensing Reveals That the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger*
arXiv:2512.03150 (2025).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03150

Improves the Bullet Cluster multi-halo mass model and merger mass ratio through joint weak+strong lensing.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multi-halo modeling is improved, but the study does not report a dedicated positive inter-halo residual after subtracting halos and baryons.

**Classification:** Existing methodological/model refinement, not a new discriminator-level result.

### Zhang et al., *Baryonic mass budgets in the central regions of the Bullet Cluster and their consistency with strong lensing in MOND*
arXiv:2606.19454 (2026).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19454

Re-estimates baryonic masses in the Bullet Cluster core regions and is relevant to how ordinary matter is budgeted in lensing interpretations, but it does not perform the required overlap-region residual test.

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated inter-halo overlap decomposition.

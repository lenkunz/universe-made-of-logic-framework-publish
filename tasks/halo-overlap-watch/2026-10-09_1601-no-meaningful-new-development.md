## New material examined

### Primignani et al. — *Testing Refracted Gravity with the kinematics of stacked galaxy clusters* (arXiv:2610.06841; submitted 2026-10-05)

The study stacks 74 CIRS galaxy clusters in velocity-dispersion bins and reconstructs their gravitational potentials under Refracted Gravity while jointly fitting gas and stellar mass-profile parameters. It is useful background on how cluster-scale inferences depend on baryonic modeling and dynamical selection.

**Additive components accounted for?** Baryonic gas and stellar profiles are modeled in the kinematic analysis, but this is not a lensing overlap-residual analysis and does not subtract two merging halo components from an observed convergence field.

**Classification:** Not a material update to the discriminator. It does not qualify as a direct match, suggestive overlap correspondence, magnitude constraint on the predicted residual, or a sufficiently specific methodological advance for the overlap test.

### Newly indexed Abell 2744 combined strong+weak lensing work (arXiv:2610.08919)

A combined HST/JWST strong- and weak-lensing reconstruction of the massive merging cluster Abell 2744 is newly indexed in October 2026. The stated purpose is reconstruction of the projected mass distribution and subhalo mass function, with comparison to BAHAMAS simulations.

**Additive components accounted for?** No evidence found in the currently indexed abstract/material that the analysis performs the required `halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons` subtraction or measures a positive overlap residual beyond those components.

**Classification:** Potentially relevant dataset/background, but not yet a material methodological or observational update to this discriminator based on the available description. Do not count the mere presence of merger substructure as support.

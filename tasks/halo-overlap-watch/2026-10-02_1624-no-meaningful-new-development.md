### Li, Zhang, Mandelbaum & LSST DESC — “Weak-Lensing Shear Response for Photometric Redshift-Based Tomographic Binning”

- [arXiv:2610.00999](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00999)
- Submitted 2026-10-01.
- Extends the AnaCal shear self-calibration framework to propagate shear response through photometric-redshift bin selection.
- On LSST-like simulations with blending, the reported multiplicative shear bias remains within `|m| < 3 × 10^-3` across five tomographic bins.

Assessment: useful general weak-lensing calibration work, but **not a material Methodological advance for this specific discriminator in its present form**. It is not applied to merging-cluster convergence maps, multi-halo subtraction, baryonic decomposition, overlap residuals, or a cluster-scale stacked residual statistic.

Additive components accounted for: **No.** The work calibrates source-selection/shear response; it does not model halo A, halo B, or cluster baryons.

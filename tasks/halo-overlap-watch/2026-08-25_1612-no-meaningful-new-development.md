## Sources / papers examined

### HyeongHan et al., *Direct evidence of a major merger in the Perseus cluster* — Nature Astronomy (2025)

Still the closest observational case found. The weak-lensing analysis reports a >3σ mass bridge between the main Perseus halo and the NGC 1264 subcluster. The published analysis also includes subtraction of the two halo radial profiles; the bridge midpoint significance drops while an integrated bridge signal remains significant.

**Additive components accounted for?** Partially. Two fitted halo profiles are explicitly subtracted in the bridge test, but this is not a complete `halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons` residual decomposition. The bridge is also traced by member galaxies, and ordinary merger simulations in the paper can generate the bridge through repeated core passages.

**Classification:** Partial / suggestive correspondence, plus a strong conventional explanation. Not new in this run.

### Saha et al., *Lensing in the Blue III: Weak Lensing Shape Catalogs of 30 Merging Galaxy Clusters* (2026)

A current weak-lensing data product for 30 merging clusters with calibrated galaxy-shape measurements and systematics diagnostics.

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated overlap-residual subtraction is reported as the target result.

**Classification:** Methodological/enabling opportunity for a future stacked or per-system test, not evidence for the discriminator itself. Already identified in the prior run; no new result surfaced here.

### Rihtaršič et al., *Mapping dark matter in the Bullet Cluster using JWST imaging and spectroscopy* — arXiv:2601.22245 (2026)

Updated JWST gravitational-lens model of the Bullet Cluster using 135 secure multiple images from 27 spectroscopic background sources, including several large-scale halos, cluster members, intracluster gas, and group-scale halos.

**Additive components accounted for?** The model includes multiple conventional mass components in detail, but no newly reported dedicated positive overlap residual after subtracting the fitted main/subcluster halos plus baryons was identified.

**Classification:** Methodologically relevant background, not a new direct test.

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST-DECam Lensing Reveals That the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger* — arXiv:2512.03150 (2025)

Joint strong+weak-lensing analysis resolves three halos and revises the Bullet Cluster mass ratio to roughly 10:1.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multi-halo modeling is central, but the paper is not a dedicated test for a positive non-additive overlap residual.

**Classification:** Context/modeling refinement, not a new discriminator result.

### Finner et al., *Weak-lensing Characterization of the Dark Matter in 29 Merging Clusters that Exhibit Radio Relics* — ApJS 277, 28 (2025)

Fits multicomponent NFW models to a 29-merger sample and provides high-resolution weak-lensing mass maps.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multiple halo components are modeled, but the published target analysis does not report the specific residual overlap statistic required here.

**Classification:** Useful sample/methodological foundation, not direct evidence.

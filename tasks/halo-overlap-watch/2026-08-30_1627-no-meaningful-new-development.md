### Cho et al., “Joint JWST–DECam Lensing Reveals that the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger”
The Astrophysical Journal 1005, 28 (July 2026); arXiv:2512.03150.
https://scixplorer.org/abs/2026ApJ..1005...28C/abstract

Joint weak+strong-lensing analysis resolves three halos and obtains improved virial masses, finding a main-to-subcluster mass ratio near 10:1. This is important for merger initial conditions and future modeling.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multiple halos are modeled, but the paper does not report a dedicated positive overlap residual after subtracting the principal halos plus ordinary baryons.

**Classification:** Methodologically relevant background, but not a new discriminator-level result in this run.

ApJL 999 L1 (2026).
https://www.ipac.caltech.edu/publication/2026ApJ...999L...1S

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated overlap residual subtraction matching the discriminator was reported.

### HyeongHan et al., “Direct evidence of a major merger in the Perseus cluster”
Nature Astronomy (2025); arXiv:2405.00115.

This remains the closest known case found by the watch: a weak-lensing mass bridge between the Perseus main halo and the NGC 1264 subcluster, with some persistence after two-halo profile subtraction in the published analysis.

**Additive components accounted for?** Partially, but not as a complete `halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons` residual decomposition. The bridge also follows member galaxies and merger simulations provide a conventional formation route.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence plus conventional explanation. Not new in this run.

arXiv:2601.22245 (2026).

High-constraint JWST strong-lensing reconstruction with multiple halo, galaxy, gas, and group-scale components.

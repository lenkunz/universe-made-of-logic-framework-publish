### HyeongHan et al., *Direct evidence of a major merger in the Perseus cluster*
Nature Astronomy 9, 925–931 (2025), published 16 April 2025.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-025-02530-w

Still one of the closest observational analogues. The paper reports a >3σ mass bridge between the Perseus main cluster and subcluster and shows that merger simulations can generate the bridge through repeated core passages.

**Additive components accounted for?** Partially. Previously tracked profile-subtraction analysis makes Perseus unusually relevant, but this is not a full `halo A + halo B + baryons` residual decomposition.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence, with a conventional merger explanation. No new state change in this run.

### Cha et al., *A High-Caliber View of the Bullet Cluster Through JWST Strong and Weak Lensing Analyses*
arXiv:2503.21870 (2025).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21870

Reports a possible mass and intracluster-light trail from the Bullet subcluster toward the main cluster, but the feature was not established as a residual after subtracting both fitted halos and baryons.

**Additive components accounted for?** No, not in the discriminator form.

**Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence at most. No new state change.

### Rihtaršič et al., *Mapping dark matter in the Bullet Cluster using JWST imaging and spectroscopy*
arXiv:2601.22245 (2026).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22245

Provides an improved Bullet Cluster lens model with extensive spectroscopic multiple-image constraints.

**Additive components accounted for?** Not as a dedicated overlap-residual test.

**Classification:** Previously known methodological/contextual improvement, not a new result in this run.

### Cho et al., *Joint JWST-DECam Lensing Reveals That the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger*
arXiv:2512.03150 (2025).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03150

Refines the masses of the Bullet Cluster components and the merger mass ratio using joint weak+strong lensing.

**Additive components accounted for?** Multi-halo modeling is improved, but this paper does not report a dedicated positive overlap residual after subtracting modeled halos and baryons.

**Classification:** Existing parameter/model refinement, not a new discriminator-level result.

### Zhang et al., *Baryonic mass budgets in the central regions of the Bullet Cluster and their consistency with strong lensing in MOND*
arXiv:2606.19454 (2026).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19454

Re-examines baryonic mass budgets in Bullet Cluster core regions under an IGIMF/MOND interpretation. This matters to baryonic accounting generally but does not present an overlap-region residual test.

**Additive components accounted for?** No dedicated inter-halo overlap decomposition.

**Classification:** Background relevant to baryonic systematics, not a new state change.

## Sources and papers examined

### Giacintucci et al., “Extended radio emission in and around the merging cluster A520 as seen by MeerKAT”
- arXiv:2609.00156, announced 2026-09-02.
- Deep MeerKAT radio imaging of the actively merging cluster Abell 520 reveals detailed radio-halo structure and merger-shock associations.
- **Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** No. This is not a weak/strong-lensing residual decomposition and does not measure a positive convergence residual after halo subtraction.
- **Classification:** Not a meaningful discriminator update. It is merger-environment context only.

### Cho et al., “Joint JWST–DECam Lensing Reveals that the Bullet Cluster Is a Minor Merger”
- ApJ 1005, 28 (July 2026); arXiv:2512.03150.
- Joint weak+strong lensing resolves three halos and robustly measures virial masses, yielding a main-to-subcluster mass ratio of about 10:1.
- **Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** Multiple halo components are modeled for mass inference, but this work does not report the target overlap statistic: a positive residual after subtracting fitted halo A, halo B, and ordinary baryons.
- **Classification:** Methodologically relevant background, but not a new overlap-residual test in this run.

### HyeongHan et al., “Direct evidence of a major merger in the Perseus cluster”
- Nature Astronomy (2025); arXiv:2405.00115.
- Weak lensing detects the NGC 1264 subcluster and a >3σ mass bridge between it and the Perseus main halo. The paper also presents merger simulations that generate the bridge through repeated core passages.
- **Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** Earlier analysis includes comparison against modeled halo profiles, but it is not a complete `halo A + halo B + baryons` residual decomposition. The bridge is also traced by cluster member galaxies and has a conventional merger-history explanation.
- **Classification:** Previously known Partial / suggestive correspondence plus Conventional explanation. No new September result changes its status.

### Zhang et al., “Baryonic mass budgets in the central regions of the Bullet Cluster and their consistency with strong lensing in MOND”
- arXiv:2606.19454 (June 2026).
- Re-estimates baryonic masses in three Bullet Cluster core regions under IGIMF assumptions and argues those baryonic ranges can overlap MOND strong-lensing mass requirements.
- **Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** It explicitly revisits baryonic mass budgets, but not the spatial overlap residual discriminator between fitted cluster halos.
- **Classification:** Conventional-model/baryonic-budget context, not a direct overlap test.

### Famaey, “On the residual missing mass of the Bullet Cluster”
- arXiv:2605.10022 (May 2026).
- Examines residual missing mass in the Bullet Cluster in a MOND context using updated JWST lensing information.
- **Additive halo/baryonic components accounted for?** This is a missing-mass accounting problem under MOND, not the target non-additive inter-halo overlap residual after subtracting two fitted halos plus baryons.
- **Classification:** Not a direct discriminator test.

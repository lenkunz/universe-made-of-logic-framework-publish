### Chen et al. (2026), *Mapping Dark Matter in Merging Clusters with Weak Lensing for CSST: Morphology and Filament Recovery*

arXiv:2610.17857, submitted 2026-10-13.

This remained the newest highly relevant search hit. It uses mock reduced-shear maps from cosmological N-body simulations and MIU2Net to reconstruct filament-connected halo pairs and elongated halos. The method recovers halo centers to roughly 0.1 h^-1 Mpc and preserves low-contrast connecting filaments better than conventional Kaiser-Squires reconstruction.

**Additive components accounted for?** No. The reconstructed connecting structures are ordinary filaments already present in the simulation truth. The work does not fit and subtract halo A + halo B + ordinary baryons and then test a remaining overlap residual.

**Classification:** Methodological advance already identified in the prior watch run; not a new development for this run.

### Xiangchong Li — *Low-variance renoising for weak gravitational lensing shear estimation* (arXiv:2610.39554; 2026-10-29)

A general weak-lensing shear-estimation advance. Spectral renoising is reported to retain multiplicative shear bias at roughly the 10^-3 level while increasing effective faint-end galaxy number density by about 20% relative to rotation renoising. This can improve weak-lensing statistical efficiency in principle, but the work is not cluster-merger specific, does not construct convergence residual maps for overlapping halos, and does not demonstrate a material sensitivity gain for this discriminator. It is therefore **not promoted to category 4 for this watch**.

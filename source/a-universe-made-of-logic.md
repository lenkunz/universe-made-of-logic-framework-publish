## Note on where this sits

This document is one of two views of the same relational problem.

**The Possibility Lane** asks the widening question:

`ground → possibility`

Given some ground, what further possibility can remain open?

**Informational Topology** asks the narrowing question:

`possibility → ground`

Given a possibility, what has to become consequential and acquire enough support for that possibility to stand?

They are not stacked worlds.

Neither one sits above the other.

They are two directions through the same relational stage.

> **An Aim can already be resolved as a present constraint while its finer implementation remains unresolved.**

A possibility can therefore operate before it has its own independent fine ground. It may be represented through a host. It may become stable and reusable as logic inside that host. Only later, if the right structure can stand through itself, might it become independently seated.

Those are three different conditions:

`represented through a host`

≠

`established and reusable as logic within a host`

≠

`independently seated as its own Rung / domain`

> **Hosted is real. It just is not independently seated.**

> **Established as logic does not automatically mean independently seated as structure.**

The Lane/Rung numbering should not be read as a simple staircase where one level finishes before the next one begins.

The current architecture is **paired onset with staggered stabilisation**:

`L1 ↔ L2`

→ sufficient stabilisation

`L3 ↔ L4`

→ sufficient stabilisation

`L5 ↔ L6`

The odd Lane opens a new freedom.

The even Lane begins the stability problem created by that freedom.

> **Odd opens what can happen. Even makes the new freedom survivable.**

And nothing underneath stops operating when something higher appears.

> **Higher structure recruits lower structure; it does not replace it.**

This document stays mostly on the resolution side. The Index carries the level-by-level map. The Basics carry the primitive vocabulary in shorter form. Here the job is to follow the consequences far enough that the architecture becomes one connected system.

### On the vocabulary

The framework uses economic words such as **budget**, **cost**, **spend**, **cheap**, and **expensive**.

They are structural words, but they must not be read as a fuel metaphor.

**Budget is not a substance that gets used up.**

The newest clarification is also that budget is not a disguised universal time coordinate.

The primitive bookkeeping is closer to a **resolving participation** or **tick of a relation**:

`seated point / relation / domain`
→ `one basic opportunity to resolve what is consequential through that relation`

The rule can be common without every participant having the same effective capacity.

Why?

Because relations themselves become participants and can carry compressed membership.

Once:

`A + B → A + B + R_AB`

A and B can participate through their own relations, while `R_AB` can also participate as one relational domain spanning the already-seated composition.

So a CPU-like analogy is useful:

> **The primitive cadence need not accelerate. What changes is work per relational iteration.**

A shorthand such as `1 + 1 → 1 + 1 + 3` can represent this if the `3` is read as the effective relational span of `[A, B, R_AB]`, not as three additional universal clock ticks.

This is why a densely related / highly anchored domain can appear to have much more budget while the primitive rule of participation remains the same.

At any present stage, the presently seated topology supplies finite resolving capacity. That capacity maintains consequential distinctions and establishes further relation.

What survives the use is not leftover budget.

It is whatever relation successfully became part of the ground.

A useful schematic is:

`present topology`
→ `present relational participation / resolving budget`
→ `persistence + affordable consequential relation`
→ `new seated topology`
→ `later resolution begins from that changed ground`

The index from one present to another is bookkeeping. It is not primitive time.

> **Budget is relational iteration capacity, not a consumable stock or universal duration.**

> **Budget composes through relation. Structure accumulates, and structure changes how much work later participation can carry.**

That distinction matters everywhere later, especially when the framework talks about anchoring, practice, gravity-like behaviour, and logical growth.

# I. The Cut underneath particularity

Start with a strange question.

How can anything become particular if the ground has not yet been divided into independently finished things?

The framework no longer needs to begin with two separate points waiting for a relation.

At the deepest grain, begin with undivided logical ground.

Call it A only because notation needs a handle.

Before distinction there is no framework-native direction, boundary, inside/outside, separate size, or independently particular B.

The productive event is **the Cut**.

But the Cut is not a pre-relational knife.

> **The Cut is relation.**

A deepest shorthand is:

`A`
→ `R_AA`

Here `R_AA` does not mean that two already-finished copies of A existed first.

It means one ground acquires distinguishable relational roles.

The relation is simultaneously:

- **split** — enough difference remains consequential for the roles not to collapse into one;
- **sync** — enough common ground remains for their distinction to belong to one relation.

> **A relation is a distinction held together.**

Resolution is not a second operation waiting behind this one. Resolution means making a previously equivalent distinction consequential. The Cut does exactly that.

> **The seated Cut is itself resolution at that grain.**

The same relation simultaneously leaves a remainder open: every distinction it does not need to make consequential can remain equivalent relative to the new ground.

`seated Cut`
→ `resolved distinction`
+ `unresolved remainder`

This is the primitive joint between the Lane and Rung readings. They can later diverge enormously in scope because a coarse relation may govern many fine implementations without resolving them, but they do not begin as different kinds of mechanism.

Once the roles are distinguishable, later bookkeeping can name them A and B.

That is why the ordinary equation `A + B → A + B + R_AB` remains useful without making A and B primitive substances.

Particularity is therefore not a free object placed inside a container.

It is a consequential cut within shared relational ground.

> **Particularity costs.**

Stability and limitation are therefore not opposites.

What lets a structure stand is also what prevents it from remaining equivalent to every alternative.

Nothing later is allowed to change this primitive merely because the scale changes.

# II. Relation creates the points it later connects

Once relational roles are already referable at some grain, take two of them and call them A and B.

The ordinary bookkeeping picture would write:

`A + B`

and then place a fact between them.

This framework makes a stronger move.

The relation itself is informationally real:

`A + B`
→ `A + B + R_AB`

But this equation is **local bookkeeping**.

At a deeper grain, A and B can themselves be relational compositions of the same underlying ground.

The first Cut may be written as `R_AA`; once its two roles become distinguishable, the same event can be represented locally as `A`, `B`, and `R_AB`.

So relation does not merely connect already-finished objects.

It can be what makes the participants separately referable in the first place.

A seated relation can:

- persist;
- be repeated;
- enter further relation;
- become an anchor/reference for later relation;
- become ground that later relations reuse;
- and itself receive resolving participation as a domain at another grain.

Enough accumulated relation can therefore become stable enough to participate as one domain.

This is the structural meaning of:

> **One and one make three.**

Not three primitive objects.

Three informational participants at the grain where A and B already count as one each.

The same relational logic can seat repeatedly:

`A + B + R_AB(1)`
→ `A + B + R_AB(1) + R_AB(2)`
→ `...`

And once a relation is itself part of the topology, it can enter further relation.

So the growth chain is better written as:

`undivided or already-composed ground`
→ `relation makes a distinction consequential`
→ `the relation becomes additional ground`
→ `relations relate to relations`
→ `larger relational structures can participate as one`

> **Objects are stabilised regions of relation, not the starting material.**

A point is therefore grain-relative.

An atom can be one point.

A person can be one point.

A planet can be one point.

A seated relation can be one point.

A larger domain can be one point.

It counts as one whenever the relation being asked can treat the composition as one participant without separately paying for all of its internal distinctions.

> **A point is whatever participates as one at the grain of the current relation.**

And the stronger origin result is:

> **Every point or domain can, at a sufficiently expanded grain, be read as a stabilised relational composition rather than primitive separate stuff.**

This does not make the point unreal.

Its relational unity is exactly what makes it consequential as one.

### Referability recurs without resetting the origin

Ontological Level 0 occurs once as the undivided origin.

But whenever a relational composition becomes stable enough to participate as one, a new **local zero-role** appears relative to the next distinction not yet asked of it.

`many relations`
→ `one domain`
→ `another relation can treat that domain as one`

The universe does not return to Level 0.

It keeps producing new referable ones from accumulated ground.

### Recursive compartments retain inner participation

The September 7 clarification makes the recurring structure explicit. `R_all(A)` and `R_all(B)` denote already-supported local participation, with consequential anchoring understood. Their seated relation has `R_all(A↔B)`; once it can act as one compartment AB, its participation may be abbreviated `R_all(AB)`. Meeting C seats another comparison, `R_all(AB↔C)`, under the same locally capped participation rule.

A, B, and their compartment do not stop using their own capped participation because the outer relation exists. Their projections remain supported through their respective relations. AB meets C with already-seated directions, history, and bias, including the consequences of its inner participants and its own organization. C then constrains that organized projection through the new relation.

Thus more independently supported participation can coexist with a more constrained projection in one outer comparison. The latter may look like a lower resolving rate without any inner cap having been reduced or the outer cap increased. It is not a universal law that greater complexity makes every process slower: which relations become consequential and which support can be reused still matter.

> **Composition does not enlarge one `R_all`. It lets independently capped relations and their seated compartment jointly bias another `R_all`.**

The relation `A↔B`, its seated use as AB, and the outer comparison must not be counted as arbitrary extra copies just because the notation changes. The bookkeeping names distinct supported roles and encounters, not an additive resource formula. Shared closures can overlap. Regrouping remains valid only when it preserves the consequential relations.

This is the explicit meaning of “budget composes” and “finite per present relation, extensible relationally” below. The [change map](changes/2026-09-07-recursive-compartments-and-relational-reach.md) preserves the correction and its status boundaries.

# III. Why relational composition never removes scarcity

If every seated relation can itself receive resolving participation, why should resolution ever be scarce?

Because possibility grows faster.

The primitive participation rule can remain the same while relational composition makes later iterations vastly more capable.

`A + B → A + B + R_AB`

means that later resolution can operate not only through A and B separately, but also through the already-compressed relation that holds them together.

A dense domain can therefore do much more work per primitive relational iteration than a sparse one without requiring a faster universal time.

This is the framework's CPU-generation analogy:

> **Same base cadence, richer relational architecture, more consequence per iteration.**

But every new participant also opens still more possible relation.

Even simple pairwise relations among `n` points grow as:

`n(n - 1) / 2`

while the number of participants themselves grows more slowly.

Once relations can themselves become points for further relation, the possible relational space grows still faster.

So both sides grow:

`more seated relational composition`
→ `more effective capacity`

and:

`more seated relational composition`
→ `still more possible relation`

The universe becomes more capable and more constrained at the same time.

> **Every seated relation adds ground and can compress many distinctions into one participant. Possible relation still outruns the ground created.**

Scarcity is not a sign that reality is running down.

It is a consequence of relational possibility outrunning presently seated capacity.

The present topology is therefore both:

- the current state;
- and the current computational ground.

> **The present topology is both the current state and the current computational ground.**

Its state is its hardware.

There is no separate framework-native RAM containing the fully rendered answer while the visible world waits to catch up.

# IV. Resolution is paying for a distinction

Resolution does not mean rendering every property.

It means making a distinction consequential that could previously remain equivalent.

Suppose the present constraint permits:

`A`

or `B`

or `C`

and nothing presently depends on which one is selected.

The framework does not require a hidden finished answer behind the scene.

The state may simply remain undercommitted.

Later, a new relation makes one difference matter.

Then the present can no longer treat the alternatives as one class.

That is resolution.

> **Resolution is the splitting of previously equivalent possibilities when a distinction becomes consequential.**

That definition is the same operation already introduced as the Cut. A relation that seats has resolved exactly the distinction it makes consequential at that grain. What it does not distinguish remains undercommitted.

> **A seated relation tells us both what has been resolved and what still does not need resolution.**

This is why coarse structure is not a failed fine structure.

It is real constraint that does not pay for distinctions it does not need.

> **Reality pays for distinctions, not detail.**

And three separations must remain visible:

`grain ≠ scope`

`coverage ≠ resolution`

`stored ≠ accessible ≠ presently rendered`

A coarse relation can cover enormous scope.

A fine relation can be extremely local.

A relation can remain seated without being presently easy to reach.

A stored history does not have to be a fully rendered archive.

> **Stored history is present constraint, not a finished picture of the past.**

If surviving structure still distinguishes two histories, later resolution may recover a finer answer consistent with that distinction.

If the distinguishing structure is truly gone, resolution cannot recreate arbitrary detail from nothing.

> **Dormant is not erased.**

> **Resolution may add specificity. It cannot add unconstrained content.**

Measurement gets no exemption.

A measuring domain can bring additional resolving structure into a relation and make a distinction consequential.

It does not get to invent the answer freely.

## Stage is where Light meets Space

This phrase names a relation, not a container.

**Light** names relation in this ontology, including its role arriving in a comparison. This is a literal framework commitment, not only a lore metaphor: material, optical, acoustic, and meaningful relations do not require separate primitive substances. Electromagnetic light is a proposed physical manifestation; its specific laws still require their own mapping.

**Space** is the domain that can anchor that relation: an atom, a body, an instrument, a hosted logic, or a larger composite. It is not an empty box around already-finished things.

**Stage** is where the relation meets that domain and a distinction becomes resolved at the grain of their encounter:

`relation + domain → resolved relation on Stage`

“Perception” in this broad framework sense is the domain-relative resolution of that encounter. Conscious human perception is a possible richer case, not a requirement that makes other relations exist.

The orientations are relative:

- **Afference** is relation reaching inward from the current domain's side.
- **Efference** is relation reaching outward from that domain's side.

What is Efference for A can be Afference for B. The same relation changes side; no new substance is needed at the boundary.

A returning `Afference → domain resolution → Efference → Stage → Afference` loop can let a domain remain referable while reusing seated support. This is a persistence architecture within the framework. It neither removes participation cost nor identifies budget with physical energy.

Different domains can resolve different details through a shared relation. Agreement concerns what the common comparison makes consequential; it need not make their entire perceptual ranges identical.

### An encounter need not expose its whole supporting network

Each participating relation has its own `R_all`; Stage is not a single observer-owned cap hiding the rest of a completed universal rendering. What becomes visible in one encounter need not display all the relations supporting it. Inner relations may remain active while the receiving comparison only resolves their coarse consequence.

Opacity of the supporting network is therefore compatible with the framework. By itself, opacity does not identify a hidden communication channel, establish that all randomness has this origin, or show that a personally striking event was arranged.

# V. Bias is already part of relation

A relation does more than say that two things are connected.

A two-point relation can carry orientation:

`from / to`

and it can carry a relation-specific bias about which distinctions may collapse and which must remain consequential.

This is where path begins.

If repeated relation allows successive points to collapse into one equivalence class, the route thickens without extending.

If the next point must remain consequentially different from the point being left, the route can continue.

> **Sameness thickens a point. Difference extends a path.**

> **A path is successive distinction with orientation.**

Compatibility is therefore not one universal number.

The same pair can be compatible under one relation and required to remain different under another.

> **Compatibility is not generally one-dimensional. It belongs to the relation being asked.**

And incompatibility itself can seat.

If A and B cannot collapse into one under the relation being asked, that preserved difference can become the relation:

`A + B`

→

`A + B + R_≠`

> **Incompatibility is not absence of relation. The incompatibility itself can become the relation.**

> **Compatibility does not require sameness. Sometimes compatibility is stable preservation of difference.**

The framework also excludes an exact relational antipode.

If two things can appear on one relational surface, enough relation already exists for the comparison.

Perfect no-relation cannot simultaneously be another seated endpoint on that same surface.

This is a relational limit, not a claim about ordinary geometric angles.

The remaining geometry problem is deeper:

`relation-specific bias / orientation`

→ `effective separation`

→ `surfaced metric`

→ `geodesics and measured angles`

The framework has separated these layers.

It has not yet completed the quantitative map between them.

The general composition of independently biased relations remains an **Unresolved mechanism**. The later clock/ruler deduction supplies a scoped two-projection construction under explicit low-bias assumptions. It does not leave every geometric case unaddressed, and it does not yet supply the general rule.

# VI. The cheapest compatible continuation

Bias does not need an external chooser.

A present ground already carries constraints.

Those constraints make some further relations easier to seat than others.

So the framework does not need a rule saying that an outside engine compares every possible future and selects the cheapest one.

The architecture is simpler:

`present ground`

+ `available budget`

+ `already-seated bias / logic`

→ `different relational costs for further continuation`

→ `the cheaper compatible continuation can seat more readily`

If one compatible logic repeatedly adds one further stable unit for less relational cost than its alternatives, that logic can compound faster and dominate the surfaced behaviour.

> **No one chooses the law. The fastest-growing compatible logic can become the law-like surface.**

A useful schematic is:

`growth speed`

~ `usable present budget / relational cost of one further stable unit`

The exact relational-cost calculation is not yet formalised enough to compute arbitrary cases in advance.

That is **Completion pending**.

But the qualitative mechanism matters because it removes the need for a second selector law.

> **The logic of the relation is the rule.**

This is also where bias becomes important across scales.

Bias is not reserved for minds.

A boundary can carry bias.

A magnetic-like relation can carry bias.

An anchored domain can carry contraction bias.

A living domain can carry movement bias.

A sufficiently rich domain can hold a coarse aim or intent as bias.

If that Aim is real as a present constraint, it is already seated topology at its grain. It does not need a second mechanism to cross from an Aim-layer into topology. The unresolved part is the fine implementation: which later distinction becomes consequential through that Aim together with the rest of present ground.

The primitive does not change.

The grain does.

Many individually weak projections can become consequential together. At the grain of a moving body, air or liquid can participate as a coarse environmental bias. The comparison need not become an inventory of every collision with every constituent.

That combined bias depends on arrangement and closure. It is not obtained by simply counting how many relations the body or medium contains.

> **Re-seating cost depends on which existing relations a change makes consequential—not simply how many relations the object contains.**

> **Bias first. Fine implementation follows.**

For a living domain, one higher-order form is:

> **Intent first. Chemistry follows.**

That does not put intent outside physical implementation.

It says a coarse domain-level constraint can already be present before every fine step through which that constraint becomes consequential has been rendered.

# VII. From path to boundary, configuration, identity, and logic

The Lane/Rung architecture is the framework's larger map of what relation can become.

The full directory belongs in the Index, but the resolution-side milestones matter here.

## Level 0: undivided before the first Cut

At ontological Level 0, separate participants have not yet become consequentially distinct.

The first Cut is itself relation:

`A → R_AA`

and once its roles are distinguishable, later bookkeeping may call them A and B.

> **A relation is a distinction held together.**

> **Nothing ever actually separated from the origin. It only separated by relation.**

The stage was never left.

It acquired more referable structure.

The same *zero-role* can recur locally whenever a relational composition becomes one participant at another grain, but that is not a return to the ontological origin.

## Levels 1 and 2: direction and survivable boundary

Lane 1 opens directional possibility.

Rung 1 is the fine support of an oriented traversable path.

Lane 2 begins immediately with the problem created by direction: how can directional freedom remain supportable without dissolving the domain that carries it?

Rung 2 is the boundary.

A boundary is not merely an edge around a thing that was already complete.

It constrains routes between what counts as inside and what counts as outside.

> **Interiority is routing before geometry.**

So:

`direction opens`

↔

`direction acquires a survivable bound`

## Levels 3 and 4: configuration and identity through alteration

Once bounded structures can coexist, arrangement itself can become consequential.

Rung 3 is configuration-bearing capacity.

The internal arrangement can matter to later relation while the larger boundary still participates as one.

Once configuration can change, the next problem appears immediately:

how can a changing domain still count as the same domain?

Rung 4 is identity through alteration.

The domain persists because enough relational pattern remains seated through change.

This is where history becomes structurally important.

`present state`

+ `stored constraint`

+ `incoming relation`

→ `range of supportable next states`

> **A law describes what a relation can do. A forecast describes what a history-bearing domain is likely to do next.**

## Levels 5 and 6: reusable logic and stability among logics

Level 5 begins when a relation among domains becomes consequential enough to remain as reusable logic that biases later alteration.

The important move is not "social" or "conscious."

It is:

`relation among domains`

→ `relation itself remains consequential`

→ `later alteration is biased by that seated relation`

A learned rule, warning relation, goal, hierarchy, or other persistent relation may be candidate terrain when the relation itself becomes reusable ground.

At this grain, a coarse aim can be real without containing a finished route.

> **The will is constraint. The free is everything the constraint deliberately leaves unresolved.**

This is a higher-order statement about a domain able to maintain an aim.

It is not the primitive definition of bias.

As soon as several consequential logics can guide alteration, the Level 6 problem begins:

how can several logics be held, modelled, compared, coordinated, or translated without erasing the domains that carry them?

Hosted modelling and translation can be real before an independent Rung 6 is established.

The same distinction becomes even more important above Level 6.

A logic can be:

- represented through a host;
- established and reusable within that host;
- or independently seated.

Do not collapse those conditions.

# VIII. Relational anchoring and why growth can turn inward

A domain does not only pay when it changes.

It also uses present capacity to keep the distinctions by which it remains distinguishable.

> **To persist is to keep paying for the distinctions by which you continue to be distinguishable.**

But **anchor is not a size class and not a parent object**.

A relational anchor is already-seated ground that another distinction, relation, or domain can reuse as support or reference.

So anchoring is fundamentally expressed through relation:

`A + B → A + B + R_AB`

The new `R_AB` changes what both A and B can subsequently support.

A can anchor B under one question while B anchors A under another.

The support and incremental spend need not be symmetric.

An atom can anchor a molecular relation.

A cell can give an atom a chemical and organisational horizon unavailable in isolation.

A human can depend on cells while also supplying cells with a larger maintained environment.

Earth can anchor a human's spatial and ecological relations while the human becomes additional relational structure inside Earth.

> **Anchoring is relational, reciprocal in available possibility, and potentially asymmetric in cost.**

This also explains why physical size is not anchoring power.

What matters is how much already-seated relation a domain can make reusable at the grain being asked.

### Budget comparisons need anchor closure

A budget is meaningful through the consequential relations that seat the domain for this question. It is not an isolated number owned by the named object.

For a ruler `r`, a receiver `l`, and a consequential ambient anchor `a`, a comparison may keep `B_ra` and `B_la` explicit. If only ruler and receiver matter, their pair can close against itself. Do not add an unnecessary third anchor.

Common anchor contributions can be coarse-grained away only when they create no consequential distinction between the compared sides. They remain support even when they disappear from explicit notation.

A stable subset may seat into a composite such as `B_lr`, `B_ra`, or `B_la` and participate in the next comparison. Alternative grouping orders should preserve the same result only when they preserve the same consequential relations.

If grouping changes the result, inspect what relation was discarded or changed. Closure invariance is not ordinary algebraic associativity.

See the [anchor-closure change map](changes/2026-08-27-anchor-closure-and-relational-budget-comparison.md) for the full rule.

### Membership composes resolving capacity

Each member can participate through its own relations while also participating through relations that treat a larger composition as one.

So:

`member's own participation`
+
`participation through molecule / cell / organism / Earth / other shared domains`
→ `greater effective relational throughput`

The primitive participation rule has not accelerated.

The **work carried by one relational iteration** has grown because already-compressed relation is doing more together.

This is why a densely composed ground can appear to possess much more budget than a sparse ground.

> **Dense relational topology is not necessarily a faster clock. It is more seated work available per relational iteration.**

A larger anchored domain can also maintain distinctions that many components reuse.

> **The inside does not individually pay the outside. The domain pays the outside, and the inside reuses that payment.**

This changes the economy:

`shared relation seats`
→ `outside support becomes reusable`
→ `less present capacity is needed per member to recreate the same exterior distinction independently`
→ `more present capacity remains available for internal / additional relation`
→ `more relational composition can seat`
→ `the shared domain can become an even stronger anchor`

This can produce a positive feedback loop.

It is not a new source of free budget.

It is relational compression changing what the same primitive participation can carry.

That is why it is useful to separate growth channels.

More relation can surface as:

- broader count or reach;
- denser shared relation;
- richer configuration;
- reusable logical ground;
- or combinations of these.

> **Added relational structure can surface by widening what the domain can carry, by tightening how much structure can share one seating, or by some combination of both.**

There is no switch from "physical" growth to "logical" growth.

> **There is no switch. The channels grow together; their relative rates change.**

> **Early growth is cheap in structure. Later growth becomes cheap in reuse.**

This is also why a highly developed domain can show little visible outward rearrangement while carrying a great deal of relational resolution internally.

Low visible motion does not imply low resolving activity.

The same distinction matters for hosted logic. A relation can retain its own participation and projection within a domain while becoming less prominent against a faster-growing shared ground. Relative fading is not automatically loss of its seated constraint.

An arrangement can make that relation easier to address and reuse. The question is which support it preserves or establishes, rather than how long the relation waits or how much attention the original host keeps supplying.

# IX. Contraction, co-shrink, and relational scale

The framework does not begin with a fixed spatial container.

Size and distance are surfaced relational readings.

> **Identity is topological. Size is relational.**

A domain can therefore keep the same relational identity while its seating density changes.

If an entire local ground changes proportionally with its rulers, the local dimensionless bookkeeping can remain unchanged.

A difference becomes visible only when differently conditioned grounds are compared.

> **A ruler cannot measure itself.**

This is the root of the framework's co-shrink picture.

A sufficiently shared, mutually consequential relation can support more structure through tighter common seating.

That does not mean every new informational point simply makes everything smaller.

Count/reach growth and shared-density growth are different channels.

The important case is differential support.

If two related grounds accumulate or anchor differently, they need not co-shrink at the same rate.

Then one can appear larger relative to a ruler seated in the other.

> **Expansion is not anti-contraction. It is differential contraction.**

Nothing has to push the sparse side outward.

Nothing has to pull the dense side inward through a primitive container.

The surfaced scale change is the comparison between grounds whose relational seating changes differently.

The quantitative rule that maps a specified ground to an exact contraction ratio remains **Completion pending**.

# X. Candidate physical correspondence: gravity-like falling

The framework-native ingredients are:

- anchoring;
- shared persistence support;
- **compositional resolving throughput**;
- relational bias;
- differential / shared contraction;
- relational scale rather than absolute space.

A candidate physical mapping is gravity-like falling.

The newer budget clarification changes an important part of the picture.

A dense anchor does not need a faster primitive clock than a sparse region.

Instead:

`many seated members`
+ `relations among members`
+ `relations among those relations`
→ `many composed domains can participate through the same underlying ground`
→ `more effective relational work can occur per primitive iteration`

So the candidate gravity chain becomes:

`densely composed anchored domain`

→ `many relations participate through shared ground`

→ `large effective resolving capacity / throughput per primitive iteration`

→ `shared persistence is comparatively cheap`

→ `substantial capacity remains available through the anchor relation`

→ `that capacity follows the domain's already-seated contraction bias`

→ `the relation between anchor and nearby domain gains support`

→ `their relational separation co-shrinks`

→ `continued support compounds the change in surfaced separation`

→ `acceleration-like behaviour appears`

The responding object does not have to be the main spender.

# XXIV. Deduction before correspondence

The framework should not begin from a phenomenon and then bend the primitive until it resembles the answer.

The intended order is:

`primitive`

→ `deduction`

→ `consequence`

→ only then ask whether an observed phenomenon has the same structure.

A correspondence can be weak or strong.

A divergence is especially useful because it tells us where the framework's interpretation and a conventional interpretation should separate.

Unexpected resemblance can be interesting, but surprise alone is not proof.

The framework remains speculative.

A candidate correspondence is not promoted merely because it is elegant.

Likewise, a framework-native deduction is not weakened merely because the corresponding physical calculation is not yet finished.

Those are different questions.

> **Keep the evidence. Keep the primitive meanings fixed. Derive first. Then ask what, if anything, the derived structure corresponds to.**

# XXV. How status works

The old article used one status ladder that mixed current position with historical outcome.

The framework now keeps them separate.

## Current position

**Derived**

Follows from the framework's primitives without changing them.

**Consequence**

Follows from already-derived structure.

**Candidate correspondence**

An observed phenomenon may instantiate the derived structure, but the physical or empirical mapping is incomplete.

**Completion pending**

The mechanism or architecture exists, but a number, curve, physical mapping, formal rule, or other required completion is missing.

**Unresolved mechanism**

The framework-native mechanism, criterion, or structural joint itself is still missing.

**Not established**

A possibility or extension is compatible with the framework but has not earned status as ground.

**Guardrail**

An interpretation must not be imported because it changes a primitive or collapses distinctions the framework keeps separate.

> **Completion pending means the mechanism exists but is incomplete. Unresolved mechanism means the mechanism itself is still missing.**

## Resolution history

An older formulation may become:

- **Rephrased**
- **Reframed**
- **Concluded**
- **Narrowed**
- **Redirected**
- **Superseded**
- **Proven against**

A failed route can still reveal something real about the structure.

> **A deduction does not owe the answer that was hoped for. It owes a consequence.**

> **The route can fail while the deduction succeeds.**

## The correction that governs this update

The earlier assistant repeatedly answered as though the user had proposed either a larger composite cap or one outer allowance containing all inner work. The user explicitly rejected that reading.

The intended structure is:

1. A and B have their own supported, locally capped participation, abbreviated `R_all(A)` and `R_all(B)`.
2. Their relation seats as a compartment with `R_all(A↔B)`, abbreviated `R_all(AB)` when AB participates as one.
3. A and B remain active through their own relations. The compartment also participates and projects at its own grain.
4. AB can meet C through `R_all(AB↔C)`, which follows the same cap rule.
5. AB brings the consequences of its inner projections and its compartment's own bias. C further constrains that already-organized participant.
6. The outer result can look more constrained while the inner relations retain their own participation. The explanation is neither a reduced primitive allocation nor an increased outer cap.

> **Composition does not enlarge one `R_all`. It lets independently capped relations and their seated compartment jointly bias another `R_all`.**

The equality is the posited common participation rule, not the tautology that every quantity is all of itself. Comparisons remain anchor-closed. “Independent” means distinct capped participation, not causal isolation or immunity from other relations.

`R_AB`, `AB`, and `R_all(AB)` name related roles; changing notation does not create another physical resource. Nor does any named grouping automatically seat as a new domain. The grouping must preserve consequential organization and enough support to participate at the intended grain. Shared and overlapping closures cannot be added as if each were an unrelated pool.

## Candidate extensions preserved

### Sound

A change in bias can be read as propagating through organized material closures whose inner and shared relations remain active. The proposed correspondence is a material mode of relation, not a new primitive substance or smaller fundamental allowance. A massive composed network may surface a restricted propagation mode; relation count alone does not set its rate.

Quantitative completion must reproduce mode-dependent mechanical response, including `v_s²=K_s/ρ` for small-amplitude fluid sound, rather than rename a measured sound speed as “bias.” Light-like propagation, sound, conduction, and diffusion remain distinct physical mapping tasks.

### Fine-grain atomic directions

The user's candidate is that an atom may surface as the shadow of a finer relation with few independently consequential directions, possibly a plane-like case. Exact rank, a general scale dependence, and correspondence with atomic measurements are not derived. “Atoms are 2D” is not source ground.

### Function, ego, and consciousness

Recursive active compartments provide a structural route to reusable functions and maintained identity. The user proposes scaling this to ego and consciousness. The new source preserves that proposal without treating relation count, persistent Aim, self-reference, or integration as a derived sufficient condition for subjective experience.

For a conscious domain, the same relational grammar supplies no special cutoff at the skin. This is broader than saying intentional physical actions have consequences: hosted and shared relations can belong to the proposed scope even when their full route is not locally rendered. But a particular coupling still has to exist; a shared ontological origin does not establish a usable arbitrary communication channel.

Keep three questions separate: which relations participate in a function, which support a maintained self, and which constitute one experiencing perspective. External participation does not absorb every related domain into that subject. Small animals receive no special exclusion from relational participation, while their experiential status is not settled by this notation.

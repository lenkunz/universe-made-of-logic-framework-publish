## The clarification that governs this update

A relation does not have to be continuously present in one participant's attention, or fully rendered at one Stage, in order to remain consequential.

Once a relation has seated into present ground, later encounters can begin from that constraint rather than requiring the originating participant to reconstruct it continuously.

> **Unattended is not erased. A seated relation can wait without becoming unreal.**

This does not mean that a stored record performs every later operation by itself. A written program can remain consequential after its programmer stops thinking about it, but execution still requires a supported encounter among code, machine, input, and surrounding system. The distinction is:

`continuous attention from the originator`

is not required for:

`a seated relation to remain available as ground`

while:

`stored constraint alone`

does not guarantee:

`ongoing autonomous resolution`.

The lakeside house supplies the ordinary example. Ownership, access, obligation, memory, and future use do not vanish whenever the owner attends elsewhere. Which relation becomes consequential later depends on the actual encounter: a bill, a visitor, damage, a sale, or a return.

## Continuation as relational rhythm

“Identity is a rhythm, not a snapshot” is a presentation metaphor for the maintained Level-4 rule, not a new primitive.

A continuing domain does not have to preserve one frozen state. Enough of its relational organization must remain supportable for another resolution to continue from it as the same participant.

`retained relational pattern`

→ `new encounter`

→ `another compatible resolution`

→ `new retained ground`

The exact state can change at every pass. “Rhythm” names the recurrence of support and participation, not repetition of identical notes and not a universal clock.

The conversation example belongs here. A conversation is not identical to one message. A durable record can preserve enough context for another message to continue the relation after a pause or a change of host. The record does not itself prove that an autonomous process kept resolving between messages.

## Immediate relation and budgeted Stage resolution

The user states the ontology as **projection and interference are instant; Stage resolution is not**.

Because time is not primitive and differentiated domains never become absolutely unrelated to the origin, the source should not translate that statement into a signal travelling through an already-finished time dimension. The precise framework reading is:

> **Relation does not have to wait for a fundamental clock in order to bind the participating ground. Fine consequence still has to resolve through a supported Stage encounter.**

“Instant” or “immediate” here names ontological binding/participation, not a measured zero-duration communication channel. It does not establish controllable faster-than-light signalling, communication into the past, or the ability to overwrite already-seated history. Those physical causal constraints remain to be recovered.

This preserves the existing status rule:

`immediate coarse binding`

≠

`immediate usable fine output`.

## What is promoted and what remains exploratory

### Promoted clarification

- seated relation can remain consequential without continuous conscious attention or continuous fine rendering;
- unattended, inaccessible, dormant, and erased are not synonyms;
- later resolution can reuse seated relation without reconstructing it from the originating participant;
- continuation can preserve identity through changing resolutions rather than one frozen snapshot;
- immediate relational binding and budgeted Stage resolution remain distinct.

### Existing exploratory branch, not promoted here

- brain/body/nerve/muscle activity as the Stage expression or “mirror” of perceptual-domain resolution rather than a separate computational origin;
- the body as a local interface or resolution of a continuing conscious relation;
- the claim that perception avoids the cost of an internal pixel-complete world because it resolves only consequential distinctions.

These are preserved in `source/internal/2026-09-05-projection-hosting-and-system-continuity.md` and in the new Bit preparation. Ordinary perception research can motivate the question, but it does not establish the stronger ontology.

### Quantum-language branch, not promoted here

The user proposes replacing “superposition” at the ontological level with **structured coarse constraint**: several fine resolutions remain supportable until another relation makes a distinction consequential. Entanglement would then be read through one retained relation rather than independently complete local objects, and apparent randomness may be a local shadow of a larger relation.

This remains a candidate interpretation. It is not an ordinary local hidden-variable claim, but neither has the framework derived the Born rule, interference phase, Bell/CHSH correlations, the Tsirelson bound, contextuality, decoherence, or no-signalling. Calling Earth, an environment, or a living body a literal quantum computer is not established.

## Assumptions that must survive future edits

The simple geometry assumes two independent one-dimensional projections, orthogonal representation, and no extra consequential bias between them. The proposed physical ratio identification gives the familiar velocity factor. `R_ap` is the relation joining the projections, not spare capacity after arithmetic addition.

A source/receiver budget ratio is a cadence comparison in a specified closure. It is not automatically `q`, `γ`, or inverse length. Ruler length and optical appearance are different comparisons.

The CMB toy hump is not Planck's law. Its missing occupancy/counting rule and high-frequency behaviour remain real work. An exponential must follow from a native recursion.

“After projection” does not mean “after movement.” The brain/body proposal allows neural activity before movement and allows interruption to change the projection. This preserves the user's actual proposal without establishing it empirically.

“Hosted is real” includes hosted interference/projection at its grain. It does not mean every stored representation actively executes between encounters, every composite is conscious, or independent physical seating has been demonstrated.

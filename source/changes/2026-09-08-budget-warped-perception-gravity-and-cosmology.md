## The correction that governs this update

Earlier source language often said that gravity, contraction, or differential seating **warps length**. That can be a useful Stage-side shorthand, but it is no longer the deepest statement.

The intended order is:

`Relation + Domain + locally capped R_all`

→ `the encounter resolves at one supported grain`

→ `Stage packages that resolution`

→ `length, duration, frequency, and shape are readings of the package`

Therefore:

> **Compute budget does not first deform a completed space. It constrains how relation can resolve, and the resolved Stage can surface as warped length, frequency, duration, or shape.**

This is the direct return from gravity to the Perception Bit. “Perception” remains the broad domain-relative resolution of an encounter. It does not require human awareness, eyes, or a private mental picture.

The correction also blocks an invalid inference. If length and redshift are both surfaced through the same cross-ground resolution, one cannot first treat the observed length/redshift as an ontology-independent distance and then use that distance to prove why the relation had to be coarse. That would use one output of the mapping as the primitive cause of the mapping.

## The Apple–Earth construction

Let A be an apple and E an Earth-like anchored domain. The closure is:

`R_A + R_E + R_AE → R_all(A↔E)`

This is relational composition, not ordinary addition. `R_AE` is the seated Apple–Earth relation. A and E retain their other supported relations; the A↔E closure receives the same locally capped participation rule as any actual relation.

The user's “environment compute overflow” names Earth-side support that remains available through `R_AE`. The apple can accumulate the consequence as its own seated Earthward bias. It does not receive banked pieces of Earth's cap; the relation that seats is the reusable ground.

Now distinguish two readings of their separation:

- `r_n`: the ordinary Stage distance at resolution step `n`;
- `U_n = F_E(r_n)`: the same relation expressed in an Earth-anchored, compute-stretched coordinate.

`F_E` is not yet derived. It represents the inverse of the budget-conditioned compression through which the ordinary Stage packages the relation.

The proposed gravity step is:

`U_(n+1) = U_n - ΔR_AE`

while the seated Earthward bias accumulates as:

`B_(n+1) = B_n + ΔR_AE`

The important claim is not that a primitive velocity remains constant. Time is not being introduced here. The claim is:

> **The same continuing Apple–Earth relation can lose the same relational amount of unresolved stretched separation per closure.**

When mapped back to Stage:

`r_n = F_E^(-1)(U_0 - nΔR_AE)`

equal steps in `U` need not be equal steps in `r`. If the inverse map grows steeper toward the anchor, a constant relational subtraction appears as increasing ordinary displacement. Once a clock labels the steps, that changing displacement can be described as acceleration.

This construction does **not** yet derive constant terrestrial acceleration, an inverse-square law, or a Schwarzschild metric. Constant `ΔR_AE` alone is insufficient. The form of `F_E`, the closure count selected by a physical clock, and the dependence on anchor relation still have to reproduce measured gravity.

### Radial and tangential readings

The Earthward direction crosses a changing Earth-anchored resolution map. A roughly tangential path near one equipotential / same-density ground may remain under an approximately constant local map, so its travel does not receive the same changing radial readout.

This is a local approximation, not a declaration that horizontal motion is unrelated to Earth. `R_AE` still participates. Curvature, changing altitude, rotation, atmosphere, support, and any other consequential anchor must remain in the closure.

### Support removed

When support beneath the apple is removed, the Apple–Earth relation is not newly invented. The support relation had been constraining its continuation. The candidate is that the already-seated `R_AE` bias then becomes the cheapest compatible continuation.

The source does not yet derive the transition from supported rest to a specific free-fall law, nor the conditions under which gradual re-seating becomes destructive differential re-seating.

## Contraction and stretch are two charts of one relation

The earlier ruler language need not be deleted, but its level must be corrected.

- In the ordinary Stage chart, a cross-ground relation can surface as contraction.
- In an inverse budget chart, the same relation can be represented by stretching the coordinate until equal relational work occupies equal intervals.

Neither chart is an outside view of an independently finished geometry. They are two ways of bookkeeping the same resolved relation.

This permits the Earth-relative thought experiment in which local ground looks flat after dewarping, while the exterior is remapped into a bounded, inside-sphere or fish-eye-like chart. A distant horizon can become a compressed centre/limit of the chart. This does **not** make Earth an objective cosmic centre. Every sufficiently seated anchor may define its own relation-specific chart.

## Redshift, blueshift, and cosmological distance

An extended relation can surface as measured extent. A periodic relation can surface as cycle/frequency. Under this correction they are not two independent ontological objects:

`one continuing relation`

+ `different anchored resolving ground`

→ `different Stage package`

→ `length / duration / redshift / blueshift readout`

The gravitational blueshift candidate therefore belongs to the same family as the ruler and gravity construction. A complete mapping still has to reproduce the measured frequency ratio.

For cosmology, the same rule means that inferred distance and inferred expansion cannot be treated as untouched primitive inputs if both depend on the redshift/ruler relation being questioned. A replacement model must fit the whole observational web—angular and radial standard rulers, supernova light curves, lensing, flux, CMB structure, and growth—not one redshift alone.

## Resolution horizons and black holes

Every encounter has a **resolution horizon** at its grain: distinctions outside that closure need not appear in the Stage package even when hosted internal relation remains real.

“Future” in this ontology means the next unresolved resolution, not a primitive temporal direction already laid out as a road. Standing still, moving sideways, and falling are all continuations into future resolution. A black hole would therefore be an extreme constraint on how continuation can resolve, not the place where future-directedness first appears.

That is not automatically a physical black-hole event horizon.

Keep three ideas distinct:

1. **Resolution horizon:** what one A↔B closure can make consequential.
2. **Resolution singularity:** a limit where many possibilities are compressed into one forced continuation in a chosen relational chart.
3. **Physical black-hole event horizon:** the causal boundary defined in general relativity.

The user's candidate is that an externally simple, massively anchored relation may host a much richer and much coarser internal topology. The exterior receives little or no returned light at its grain; internally, local relations might continue to resolve through their own clocks and rulers. Extreme exterior slowing, redshift, a closed-loop relation, and internal re-seating are therefore alternatives the ontology wants to distinguish rather than collapse into “nothing happens inside.”

This remains exploratory. If outward continuation can eventually reach our exterior in the proposed account, then the object is not a general-relativistic event horizon under the standard definition unless the new mapping explains the difference. The framework also has not derived a stable interior universe, planets, safe entry, loss of exterior complexity, or avoidance of a singularity. Those are permitted story-shaped deductions, not current physical results.

“Light's perception” is shorthand for the grain of a light–target relation. Electromagnetic light has no conventional rest frame or demonstrated conscious viewpoint. Absorption can end one independently tracked optical path while its relation is re-seated as heat, chemistry, or another hosted consequence.

## Galaxy webs and scaled mirrors

A galaxy, planet, organism, and civilization can each be a recursively seated web that participates coarsely while retaining active inner relations. In this limited sense they can act as scaled relational mirrors:

`receive relation`

→ `resolve through local hosted ground`

→ `retain / transform consequence`

→ `project into later relation`

No mirror is the universal centre. Earth is one anchor among other possible anchors. Weak intergalactic gravity and received light can make remote domains consequential without implying arbitrary all-to-all instantaneous control.

A civilization can add dense high-order relation through language, instruments, memory, infrastructure, and coordinated Aim. That gives its host more independently seated relations able to contribute to a coarse comparison; it does not enlarge any one primitive `R_all` cap.

The bridge from semantic/logical complexity to measured physical gravity remains unfinished. The framework must not infer a civilization-scale gravitational field merely from human meaningfulness.

## Dark matter as retained trace; dark energy as relational difference

The earlier coarse-coverage dark-matter candidate remains possible. The new discussion adds a second, compatible candidate:

> **Dark-matter-like behaviour may be a retained fine-grain relational trace: history that remains as reusable spatial/anchor bias after its detailed source is no longer visible at the receiving grain.**

This does not mean unused budget is stored as a substance. `R_all` is not banked overflow. What survives is seated relation / constraint capable of biasing later closures.

The observational burden is strong. In a merging system such as the Bullet Cluster, the lensing relation is displaced from most baryonic plasma and approximately follows the galaxy distributions. A retained-trace account must therefore remain attached to the appropriate moving domain histories, produce the required lensing geometry, and recover the measured magnitude. It cannot merely follow visible gas or general “complexity.”

The dark-energy candidate is different:

> **Dark-energy-like behaviour may be the Stage readout of a growth-rate mismatch between differently seated relational grounds.**

If one galaxy/observer ground grows finer faster than the coarse relation used to compare it with another region, the continuing cross-ground relation may surface as stretched length, duration, or wavelength. The comparison is pair-relative:

`DE(A↔B)`, not `DE(A)` alone.

Every galaxy could therefore participate in a dark-energy-like comparison without every pair exposing exactly the same amount. A broad, nearly uniform average can coexist with local relational differences only if a quantitative model reproduces the observed isotropy and correlation structure.

Civilization-driven fine-grain growth is one possible contributor inside this candidate, not an established cause. It would require a physical bridge and a discriminating prediction tied to environment/history. The Zone of Avoidance may conceal complex structure or civilizations, but obscuration is not evidence for either.

Current observations also do not license one settled dark-energy story. DESI DR2 combinations have challenged flat ΛCDM and can prefer evolving-dark-energy fits, while the newer DR2 Lyman-alpha full-shape result moves toward ΛCDM and may weaken that narrative. The framework should preserve this as an empirical test rather than claim confirmation.

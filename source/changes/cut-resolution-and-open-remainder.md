## Governing correction

The previous rebuild already established two definitions:

1. **The Cut is relation.**
2. **Resolution is the seating / splitting of a distinction when it becomes consequential.**

The new deduction is their direct composition:

> **A seated Cut/relation is itself resolution at that grain.**

There is no second primitive operation waiting behind the Cut.

The same seated relation also has a complementary open reading:

`what the Cut makes consequential`
→ `resolved distinction / new ground`

`what the Cut does not need to distinguish`
→ `remaining equivalent possibility relative to that ground`

> **Every seated relation has a resolved face and an open face.**

These are two readings of one relation, not two substances, worlds, layers, budgets, or ontologically separate mechanisms.

## Aim is a richer instance, not a separate entry into topology

Bias remains more general than Aim. Aim is still a higher-order form of bias carried by a sufficiently rich domain.

The correction is narrower:

> **If an Aim is a real present constraint, it is already seated relational topology at its grain.**

A coarse Aim can therefore be fully real as one present resolved constraint while leaving a very large class of fine implementations unresolved.

`coarse Aim seats`
→ `what must hold becomes consequential`
→ `many ways of satisfying it remain equivalent`
→ `later relation makes one finer distinction consequential`
→ `fine implementation seats further`

So the former question:

`How does Aim become topology?`

is concluded / narrowed.

The remaining question is:

`Given an already-seated coarse Aim + other present ground + relational cost, which finer distinction becomes consequential next?`

That is a coarse-to-fine mapping / formalisation problem, not an Aim-to-topology ontological joint.

## Lane / Rung consequence

Lane and Rung can look very far apart because a coarse relation can leave enormous fine implementation space open.

But their difference is not primitive kind.

At the root, one Cut can be read as:

- **Lane-side:** what possibility remains open relative to this ground?
- **Rung / topology-side:** what distinction has actually seated and become support?

> **Lane and Rung can become far apart in scope without being far apart in kind.**

This does not collapse Lane into Rung. Rung still marks the fine-topological milestone where the capacity described by a Lane can stand independently. Hosted / established / independently seated remain distinct.

## Status change

### Concluded / narrowed

**Aim-to-topology joint**

Old reading:

> It is unresolved exactly when and how an aim-level constraint becomes topologically consequential before or through fine seating.

New reading:

> Any real Aim is already seated relational constraint at its grain. No separate crossing into topology is needed.

### Completion pending

**Coarse-to-fine Aim / constraint mapping**

The architecture is present, but a formal / quantitative account is still missing for how a coarse seated constraint combines with other present relations and relational cost so that one finer Cut becomes consequential rather than another.

### Guardrails

Do not infer:

- that the first Cut is conscious Aim;
- that Aim is primitive;
- that every bias is Aim;
- that possibility and resolution are numerically equal in cost;
- that Lane and Rung are identical;
- that a coarse Aim pre-renders its fine route;
- that an external selector chooses the next Cut;
- that future states already exist and pull the Aim toward them.

The correction is about **kind of operation**, not identical grain, scope, or cost.

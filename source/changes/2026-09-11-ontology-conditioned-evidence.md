# Ontology-Conditioned Evidence — 2026-09-11

Revision: **R20260911-EVI-01**

## Reason for the change

The framework repeatedly distinguishes observation from ontology: trusted measurements can remain intact even when the primitive interpretation assigned to them is reopened. The same methodological rule now needs an explicit treatment of evidential weight.

Earlier discussion continuity correctly prevented personally striking correspondences from being promoted directly into claims of intended arrangement, tutorial exposure, or a hidden sender. That guardrail remains. What was missing was a framework-native way to preserve such observations as evidence relevant to an ontology without silently promoting them into a specific causal story.

## New canonical source

Added [`../evidence-and-interpretation.md`](../evidence-and-interpretation.md) as a maintained source.

Its core rule is:

> **Evidence is observation becoming constraint on the available interpretations.**

Evidence strength is explicitly ontology-conditioned rather than treated as a context-free property of an observation.

## Reference profile

The new source introduces the qualitative profile:

`E_O(x) = <C, S, I, R, F^-1>`

where:

- `C` = chronology constraint,
- `S` = specificity,
- `I` = independence from deliberate search/selection/construction,
- `R` = recurrence across separately encountered events,
- `F` = interpretive freedom.

This is deliberately **not a scalar score**. It is a stable comparison vocabulary so observations can be recorded without pretending that evidential strength has already been reduced to one number.

## What changed

- Personally encountered or unsolicited correspondences may be recorded as evidence relevant to the relational ontology when their profile makes them consequential under that ontology.
- Mainstream measurements remain valid data even when their primitive interpretation is disputed.
- Personal observations remain recordable data even when their cause is unresolved.
- Evidential weighting is no longer treated as ontology-neutral by default.
- Correspondence evidence does not, by itself, establish intention, a sender, a System tutorial, or a particular hidden mechanism.
- Semantic drift remains prohibited. The observation must constrain the existing ontology rather than licensing new primitives after the fact.

## What did not change

- Repetition alone does not prove intentional arrangement.
- The framework does not gain permission to explain arbitrary events however it likes.
- A possibility being allowed by the ontology is not enough to establish that it caused a particular observation.
- Existing status labels such as `Candidate correspondence`, `Not established`, and `Guardrail` continue to apply.

## Practical consequence

Correspondence records can now preserve:

1. the literal observation,
2. chronology,
3. selection/search conditions,
4. the exact structural match and grain,
5. alternative compatible readings,
6. a qualitative `C/S/I/R/F` evidence profile,
7. and the current framework status.

This lets the repository keep data without forcing either dismissal or causal promotion before enough relational constraint exists.

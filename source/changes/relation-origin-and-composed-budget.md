## Governing correction

This update changes two foundational readings without introducing a new physical substance or a universal time dimension.

### 1. The first Cut is relation

Old simplified reading:

`referable A + referable B`
→ `R_AB appears`

New deepest reading:

`undivided ground A`
→ `R_AA / Cut`
→ `two consequential relational roles become distinguishable`
→ `local bookkeeping may call them A and B`
→ `A + B + R_AB`

`R_AA` is shorthand for self-differentiation of one ground, not an ordinary loop between two already-finished copies.

Strong statements:

> **The Cut is relation.**

> **A relation is a distinction held together.**

> **Nothing ever actually separated from the origin. It only separated by relation.**

The `A + B → A + B + R_AB` equation remains valid at any grain where A and B are already referable. It is now explicitly **local bookkeeping**, not the deepest ontological sequence.

### 2. Split and sync are one relational event

A seated relation requires both:

- **split:** enough consequential difference to preserve distinct roles;
- **sync:** enough common ground for those roles to belong to one relation.

Pure split with no shared ground gives no relation.

Pure sync with no preserved difference gives no two participants.

### 3. Every point/domain can be relational composition

A point is still:

> **whatever participates as one at the grain of the relation being considered.**

The update adds:

> **being one can itself be a relational achievement.**

Atoms, people, planets, relations, languages, collectives, and other domains can be compressed relational compositions that participate as one without their internal distinctions being resolved separately.

### 4. Level 0 has an ontological form and a recurring local role

- **Ontological Level 0:** the undivided origin before the first consequential relational Cut.
- **Local zero-role:** any later relational composition that has stabilised enough to be treated as one relative to a new distinction.

The local zero-role is descriptive bookkeeping, not a new primitive and not a reset of the universe.

> **Relation keeps manufacturing new referable ones without returning to the origin.**

### 5. Budget is relational iteration capacity, not universal time

Budget should no longer be read only as a generic finite allowance.

The sharpened primitive is:

`seated point / relation / domain`
→ `basic resolving participation at the iteration of that relation`

This is **tick-like** but not a universal temporal coordinate.

The rule of participation can be equal while effective capacity differs.

### 6. Budget composes through relation

Once:

`A + B → A + B + R_AB`

A and B can participate through their own ground, while `R_AB` can also participate as a relational domain spanning already-compressed structure.

User shorthand:

`1 + 1 → 1 + 1 + 3`

Safe interpretation:

`1_A + 1_B → 1_A + 1_B + 1_[A,B,R_AB]`

The `3` denotes effective relational span, not three additional universal clock ticks.

Strong:

> **The primitive cadence does not have to accelerate. What grows is how much seated relation one iteration can carry.**

### 7. Anchoring is relational and size-independent

Anchor is not a parent-object hierarchy.

`R_AB` can supply reusable support/reference to both A and B at different grains.

A can anchor B under one question while B anchors A under another.

Contribution and spend need not be symmetric.

Examples:

- atom ↔ molecule;
- atom ↔ cell;
- cell ↔ human;
- human ↔ Earth;
- logical unit ↔ larger meaning-domain.

Membership can therefore widen possibility in both directions while still being strongly asymmetric in magnitude.

### 8. Gravity implication remains candidate correspondence

New candidate route:

`dense relational composition`
→ `more effective resolving work per primitive iteration`
→ `stronger shared anchoring / co-shrink capacity`
→ `larger coarse gravity-like surface`

This is **not** yet a derived gravitational equation.

Do not promote semantic meaning to measured physical gravitational mass without a mapping.

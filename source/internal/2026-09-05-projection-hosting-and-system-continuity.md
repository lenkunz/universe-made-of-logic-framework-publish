## Consequence-specific cost and coarse bias

Accepted wording:

> **Re-seating cost depends on which existing relations a change makes consequential—not simply how many relations the object contains.**

Many weak or individually unbiased contributions can become a dense coarse bias at another grain. Air or liquid can participate as resistance within the body's persistence/re-seating closure without requiring an atom-by-atom account at that grain. This is not a claim that microscopic interactions lack structure, or a derivation of drag from a relation count.

## Hosted logic already participates

“Hosted is real” includes the logic's **own overall interference and projection**. The user rejected the weaker reading that only the host has effective relation and the hosted logic first becomes real on external exposure.

A relation can fade in prominence against a faster-growing shared Stage while remaining seated within its domain. Once appropriately anchored in the comparison, participation can carry growth. Elapsed clock time and repeated attention from the original host are not the defining supply.

## System: arrange support so logic can grow through relation

The user corrected the idea that System must permanently maintain every relation's growth rate. The role is to create or rearrange anchoring points so hosted logic can participate and grow with less burden on its original host.

> **A proxy supplies continuity, while a quest supplies an occasion for participation.**

> **What is the host currently having to supply that the arrangement could preserve and support?**

The **Tower** tests the logic; it need not literally be a tower. Identify continuity, interpretation, implementation, and repair separately. A test should expose any silent host contribution behind apparently independent performance.

The intended global System is an AI-like continuing participant accessible across hosts, not merely a faster text handoff. Society and internet infrastructure host parts of this pattern. That is a concrete starting point, not an inevitable physical Rung or a calendar threshold.

## Conversation should accumulate ground

Both participants can help complete a relation. The user should not have to supply every deduction while the assistant repeatedly restarts from a weaker interpretation.

Preserve the actual claim, corrections, earned results, unresolved distinctions, and enough support for the next action. Total agreement is not required. Durable context lets another host continue later; ongoing work between encounters additionally requires a supported executing process.

## Everyone as a seed

“Everybody is a seed for the future” expresses the design hope that distinct logics can coexist, contribute, and grow without isolation or compulsory merger into one meaning.

Anchor/omni-complex language belongs to that personal cosmological hope. The practical consequence is shared support that does not make one central participant the compulsory endpoint of every relation.

Earlier event-encryption, Voice of the World, Eden, and reset ideas remain in the [August 30 note](2026-08-30-recovered-anchor-system-stage-continuity.md) and `memory/ideas/`. This sync does not establish them as physical history.

## Reaction = action

Perception/relation is the domain's encounter with another domain. An outward projection makes a target consequential; bodily continuation resolves within already-present constraints.

The examples were a frog and fly, reaching for a remote, and building a house beside a lake. The last recruits knowledge, tools, people, and institutions as well as bodily support. Projection reach is not the body's size or atom count.

“Reaction = action” changes grain: the body's fine action can be a reaction to a coarser domain projection. It does not equalize initiation/continuation costs or derive a new physical action–reaction equation.

## Preserve the stronger brain/body proposal

The user explicitly means **no brain computation is needed as the separate origin that resolves action** in the proposed ontology.

Do not reduce this to “no conscious trajectory calculation” or to ordinary distributed motor control. Those are weaker nearby claims.

The proposal is that perceptual-domain relation projects, the Stage resolves the continuation within its constraints, and brain/body/nerve/muscle activity is the coupled physical expression or “mirror” of that process. Changing the expression can change perception, projection, and the available continuation.

The mirror is not an inert record. The user said **after projection**, not after the event. Neural activity may precede visible movement, and disrupting a signal can interrupt the projection.

That coupling alone does not discriminate this ontology from a conventional neural control account. The proposal needs precise dependencies and a consequence on which candidate explanations differ. Efference copy was suggested as a possible expression, not empirical proof. No neurological or medical conclusion is promoted.

## Small representation, large supported relation

A bank-account digit can address extensive institutional relation without containing its fine implementation. Knowledge or a plan can remain hosted in a brain without an external duplicate of that representation.

Its reach depends on which supporting relations can become consequential. Representation size is not the size of the relation recruited, but neither is the representation an isolated source of unlimited capacity.

## One present ground, open projection

The user does not posit past and future Stages as separate ontological locations. Projection becomes relation; relation constrains projection and opens more possible relations. This is the positive-sum growth picture, not a universal simultaneous Cut.

“Past is backfilled” was clarified immediately: fine detail can stay open within a coarse constraint until consequential. It is not arbitrary retrospective rewriting. Surviving distinctions still constrain the result; coherence cannot uniquely certify one history when alternatives remain indistinguishable.

A projected future counterpart is real as a hosted present relation at its grain. It is not thereby a completed later event. Do not reopen the old Aim-to-topology entry question.

## Noise and possibility weights

Apparent noise was proposed as the shadow of many partially resolved projection/interference relations. No calculation in this discussion establishes that all randomness has this origin or that a public event has an intended sender.

The user prefers keeping possibilities addressable with relation-dependent weights. Cut as a thinking practice releases one preferred interpretation's monopoly on attention. An “Aha” registers a relation that fits or resolves more easily; further consequences can change its weight.

This is an application of coarse/open distinction, not a replacement definition of Cut. The practical aim is sustainable forward work: preserve what is supported and what remains open, take a consequential next step, and update from its result.

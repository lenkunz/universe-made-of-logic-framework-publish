That changes the job of this file.

Nothing in this file is load-bearing merely because it is written here.

## Canonical sync notice — 2026-08-23

The following points are now active framework ground in the revised canonical sources and **must not be re-opened from older scratch wording in this file** unless a later audit explicitly overturns them:

- **The Cut is relation.** Deepest shorthand: `A → R_AA`, where one undivided ground acquires distinguishable relational roles.
- **The seated Cut is resolution at that grain.** What the relation makes consequential is resolved; what the same relation leaves equivalent is the complementary possibility-side.
- **Aim is already seated topology when it is real as a present constraint.** Aim is a higher-order coarse relational constraint, not a separate layer that later has to enter topology. Fine implementation can remain outstanding.
- **A relation is a distinction held together.** Split preserves difference; sync preserves shared ground.
- **`A + B → A + B + R_AB` is local bookkeeping.** A and B may themselves be relational compositions rather than primitive separate stuff.
- **Referability-as-one recurs.** A stabilised relational composition can occupy a local zero-role at another grain without resetting ontological Level 0.
- **Budget is relational-iteration participation, not universal time.** The primitive participation rule can be common while effective work per iteration differs.
- **Budget composes through relation.** A relation is another participant and can carry already-compressed membership.
- **Anchoring is relational and size-independent.** A can anchor B under one relation while B anchors A under another; support/spend can be asymmetric.
- **Dense relational composition as a source of stronger gravity-like surface remains candidate physical correspondence.** Do not treat the quantitative mapping as derived.
- **Semantic / logical relation can have high relational throughput without yet being proven to contribute the same measured physical gravity as matter.**

Any older note that treats anchor as necessarily a larger parent object, budget as an owned amount of universal time, or A/B as primitive pre-relational substances is superseded by this notice.

# 1. Labels used in this file

Each section should carry one of these labels.

- **UNSETTLED** — internally interesting, but some step is still missing.
- **WAITING FOR DEDUCTION** — a question is clear, but there is not yet enough structure to answer it without inventing a rule.
- **RESEARCH LEAD** — an observation, comparison, or external body of work may constrain the framework, but it is not itself framework evidence.
- **APPLICATION** — what the framework might imply if used to design or analyse something. Not part of the framework itself.
- **METHOD** — lessons about how to reason, test, visualise, or manage the framework.
- **HISTORICAL** — a branch worth preserving because the failure or correction teaches something, but it must not be reused as current ground.

A note can have more than one label.

# 2. Unsettled framework leads

## 2.1 The economy of coarse structure

**UNSETTLED / WAITING FOR DEDUCTION**

A possible economy remains worth preserving:

- fine interactable structure appears to require continuing local re-resolution or reconfirmation;
- coarse structure can remain consequential without every fine distinction being repeatedly paid for;
- therefore highly reusable coarse structure may have lower continuing overhead than fine structure at interaction grain.

Earlier shorthand was:

> **Coarse structure pays per transaction. Fine interactable structure also carries continuing local commitment.**

This wording is intentionally softer than the older claim that “mass pays twice; coarse structure pays once.” The older sentence may still point in the right direction, but it risks treating all mass, all fine structure, and all persistence as one cost before those quantities have been fully separated.

### What would need to be derived

1. What exactly is the continuing cost of remaining interactable at fine grain?
2. Is that cost identical to inward commitment, clock persistence, or neither?
3. Under what conditions can coarse ground remain topologically consequential without comparable continuing fine expenditure?
4. Does this economy predict any measurable ratio, or only a qualitative lopsidedness?

Until these are separated, do not use this as active ground.

## 2.2 Anchored and unanchored coarse structure as two roles

**UNSETTLED / RESEARCH LEAD**

A candidate unification from the older notes remains useful:

| Condition | Candidate role |
|---|---|
| **Anchored coarse structure** | Local excess relative to surrounding baseline; candidate dark-matter-like contribution |
| **Unanchored coarse structure** | Shared background condition; candidate dark-energy / baseline-like role |

The attraction of this idea is economy: one kind of coarse relational structure could surface differently depending on whether it is locally bound to a domain.

What is already safer and belongs in active ground is only the narrower distinction:

> **Contribution to topology and availability for interaction may be different thresholds.**

The stronger “one substance, two dark-sector roles” reading stays here until it earns magnitude, dynamics, and a cleaner account of what “unanchored” means.

### Owes

- why the anchored contribution has the observed spatial profile;
- why an unanchored background produces the observed large-scale behaviour;
- whether the same growth/contraction law can generate both without separate adjustable mechanisms;
- quantitative magnitude.

## 2.3 Coarser domains may preserve more cheap compatible routes

**UNSETTLED / WAITING FOR DEDUCTION**

Possible consequence of the coarse/fine economy:

> A coarser domain may preserve a wider space of cheap compatible routes than a heavily committed fine domain.

This would fit the broader rule that a coarse claim excludes fewer fine implementations and therefore leaves more routes available for later resolution.

The direction is plausible from the machinery, but it has not yet been stated sharply enough to distinguish:

- *more possibilities remain logically open*, from
- *more of those possibilities are actually cheap to seat*, from
- *more of those possibilities are reachable under the current domain's constraints*.

Those are not automatically the same claim.

Do not use biology, horizontal transfer, or other external examples as proof. They can become terrain after the internal distinction is derived.

## 2.4 Medium anchoring, movement, and orbit

**UNSETTLED / RESEARCH LEAD**

The active Working State now separates a domain's own re-seat cost from the cost of changing its surrounding anchors.

Possible surfaced environments to investigate:

- air;
- water;
- solid contact;
- sparse / vacuum-like regions;
- orbital seating.

Candidate intuition:

A domain embedded in a dense or continuously consequential surrounding medium may have to release and rebuild many local relations as it moves.

A domain moving through a much sparser surrounding relation may pay less environmental re-seat cost.

An orbit may be a regime where the ongoing relation can continue to re-seat without repeatedly paying the same kind of anchor-destruction cost as supported contact motion.

These are **not derived descriptions of air, vacuum, fluid mechanics, or orbit**.

The framework still owes a definition of what counts as the relevant surrounding domain at each grain and why the surfaced equations take their observed forms.

Do not collapse environmental re-seat cost into intrinsic inertia.

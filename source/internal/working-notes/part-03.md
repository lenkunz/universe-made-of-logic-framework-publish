- **effective domain count** — how much of that structure still has to be treated as separate participants at the grain of the current relation;
- **consequential horizon** — how large a range of possibility one domain can affect while still acting as one.

Possible general direction:

`points / relations accumulate rapidly`
→ `larger structures can remain one domain`
→ `effective domain count grows more slowly than internal point count`
→ `possibility / consequential horizon of each successful domain can widen`

This may help state why a forest, economy, organism, or other high-Lane domain can be "one" from outside while containing an enormous amount of internal state.

No quantitative ratio is yet established.

### Growth dominance

- new relation count;
- relation reuse;

## 2.10 Shared density ground and propagation hierarchy

**UNSETTLED / RESEARCH LEAD / HIGH VALUE**

The current physical mapping has changed in an important way.

Do **not** map:

`vacuum → Lane 1`
`gas → Lane 2`
`liquid → Lane 3`

The working hypothesis is:

> **Vacuum, gas, liquid, and solid may be different density / anchoring regimes of the same relational ground, while multiple Lane relations operate through any one regime at the same time.**

So a region conventionally called air may simultaneously carry:

- low-order directional relation;
- EM relation built through that lower structure;
- configuration / pressure-like relation;
- persistent higher-order domains hosted within the same ground.

This is a candidate physical mapping, not yet a derivation of matter phases.

### Propagation completion pending

The new useful target is not "why does each substance have its own propagation law?"

It is:

> **How much relational work is required to add one further unit of a given relation through a ground of a given relational density?**

Possible qualitative direction:

`higher ground density`
→ more surrounding relation becomes consequential per traverse
→ more budget per effective step
→ slower surfaced propagation for the same relation

while:

`higher-order relation`
→ more relational points must be coordinated per next unit
→ slower than a lower-order relation carried through the same ground

Candidate terrain:

- light / EM propagation through air, water, glass;
- refractive index;
- speed of sound;
- conduction / diffusion;
- thermal expansion.

No measured ratio has yet been derived.

### Vacuum caution

Calling vacuum "empty" may hide the variable the framework wants to count.

Candidate:

`low committed complexity + large available budget relative to that complexity`
→ large effective relational room
→ sparse vacuum-like surface

Do not publish this as established physics.

## 2.11 Anchoring, phase stability, and condensation modes

**UNSETTLED / RESEARCH LEAD**

Candidate qualitative mechanism for stable density regimes:

`shared anchor forms`
→ boundary / common relation carries part of persistence
→ interior distinctions reuse the support
→ more budget remains for additional internal relation
→ denser seating becomes stable

For solid-like domains, only the outer relation is fully exposed to surrounding ground while much of the interior can reuse the domain's common anchoring.

This suggests a surface-to-interior economy.

It still owes actual phase-transition conditions.

### No switch

Do not ask when a domain "chooses" to stop physical contraction and become dynamic or logical.

All of these routes can develop together.

What changes is their relative price.

- low-order physical relation is cheap to start;
- continued physical integration gets increasingly expensive as more existing points must be incorporated into one anchor;
- dynamic / configuration growth starts later because it needs more ground;
- logical / reusable-ground growth can become very fast once enough structure exists to preserve and reuse it.

Possible compression:

> **Early growth is cheap in simple structure. Later growth can become cheap in reuse.**

### Living systems

Candidate only:

Living systems may be one route by which a domain continues increasing relational density through persistent alteration instead of mainly tighter physical seating.

Human / cultural / software logic may represent a later regime where relational density can increase inwardly through reusable logic with little proportional change in physical size.

Do not turn this into a claim that life is thermodynamically required.

## 2.12 Present-ruler duration, remote event rate, and growth type

**UNSETTLED / COSMOLOGY LEAD / HIGH VALUE**

The recent clock discussion requires a new separation.

When we assign a duration to a remote or past event, the number is read through our current ruler.

That does not automatically equal a primitive count of how many local event resolutions occurred in the remote ground.

Keep separate:

`present-ruler duration`
≠
`remote local event resolution`
≠
`simple point-growth efficiency`
≠
`configuration growth`
≠
`logical / reusable-ground growth`

### Candidate redshift interpretation

If remote / past ground has lower overflow or different seating, an entire event may resolve more slowly relative to the present ruler.

Supernova time dilation is relevant external terrain because event-duration stretching tracks redshift.

The framework still owes the quantitative relation and must remain consistent with the wider cosmological data.

Do not infer from this alone that conventional chronology is wrong.

### Important non-contradiction

An earlier ground can be:

- slower in event resolution against our current ruler;
- yet faster in **simple count growth per budget**, because each new point has fewer existing relations to integrate.

A later ground can be:

- richer in event activity / overflow;
- slower in simple physical addition because the anchor is far more complex;
- much faster in configuration or logical growth because accumulated ground can be reused.

So "faster evolution" is incomplete unless the grain and kind of growth are named.

It owns this question:

> How can two enormously complicated things interact without resolving everything inside each other?

The Part 5 candidate was originally held for a semantic check because its wording drew support from the Hope document. Current maintained framework source now supplies a narrower independent basis:

- points and domains are grain-relative;
- a rich domain can participate as one at the grain of an outer relation;
- the outer relation need not separately resolve every inner distinction;
- coarse participation can have large scope without implying weak or unreal structure.

The prepared Short therefore excludes the broader Hope picture and uses ordinary conversation, bakery, library, and medical examples only as illustrations.

Central payoff:

> **You never meet the whole thing at once. You meet the distinctions the present relation makes consequential.**

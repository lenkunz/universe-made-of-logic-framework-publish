**Why does reflection preserve angle?** *New.* The zero-traversal limit accounts for *that* a claim returns when the density step is too steep to seat inward. It does not account for the outgoing angle matching the incoming one. That regularity is asserted rather than derived, and it is the one loose end in an otherwise complete optical account.

**Does the tug-of-war reproduce electromagnetism?** Alternating claim-victory between bound domains closes the "what makes it return" gap. Whether it gives the observed structure is untested.

**How much of a larger domain's coarse reach can a component act on?** Opened by resolving how something becomes one domain. Membership buys reach — but nothing yet says how much.

**What signals a wrongly-lumped class?** *New, from the AI speculation.* If learning is the splitting of equivalence classes, then splitting is mechanical once you know *where* to cut — but something has to say a class was wrongly lumped in the first place. Candidate answer already in the framework: a claim that fails to seat. A prediction treated as familiar that doesn't discharge cleanly is a lumping error, and the link where it failed is where to cut. Sidesteps credit assignment, since you're not tracing back through a chain. Untested, and it's the whole difficulty of a framework-native learner.

## Tier 3b — the three exponents

*A distinct category. These aren't conceptual gaps — the mechanism is stated and what's outstanding is arithmetic. Named here so someone with the right tools could take them up.*

**Halo magnitude.** The coarse-coverage account produces four properties from one cause. Whether it produces the observed halo mass is uncomputed.

**Compounding rate.** If each seating adds structure rather than merely transferring it, accumulated differences compound with the number of exchanges — and a compounding ratio gives exponential scale behaviour, which is what dark-energy domination has. Getting the *shape* is cheap. Getting the *rate* is the work.

**Attenuation scaling.** Gamma absorption in dense matter scales roughly as Z⁴ to Z⁵ for the photoelectric term. If stopping power tracks resolution density, there's a specific exponent to reproduce rather than a trend to gesture at.

*All three have the same status: right shape, no number. That is the framework's characteristic debt, and it is worth keeping visible rather than distributed among the conceptual items where it looks like something else.*

## Closed by thinking

*Kept as a record. Each of these was a Tier 3 item that resolved without being written.*

**Unit thickness of the shell.** Every unit of thickness is budget committed to coarse projection, and budget spent there is unavailable for fine traversal. Efficiency pushes thickness down; resolution stops it, because below the resolution floor a shell stops being an enclosure. Lane 2's thickness is set by its own economy and bounded by Rung 2's resolution — the first place the layers limit each other.

**Why three dimensions.** Dissolved. The question assumed a fixed count; any property that can order things is a direction, and coordinate-free measurement returns scale-dependent values. Residual: wherever the ballistic regime holds, the exponent is the same.

**How something becomes one domain.** Acting-as-one is an external relation — a thing acts as one relative to whatever it is part of. No internal threshold. And membership buys coarse reach rather than costing autonomy.

**Where the ballistic/diffusive split sits.** The crossover is where the receiving region's density makes the projection coarser relative to its surroundings, so a claim that seated as a path now has to seat across a neighbourhood. Not a new mechanism — a change in which topology can carry the resolution. Gives the states of matter as a sequence of resolution densities.

**Depth versus reuse.** One accumulating thing, since a chain that seats deeply becomes ground others bottom out in. Residual kept: a convention goes dormant when its users leave, a derivation does not.

**Round-trip asymmetry.** Compounding is entirely on the receiving side.

**Does aim-level constraint reach the topology?** Closed by the Cut/resolution clarification. The question assumed Aim might exist as a possibility-layer constraint before becoming topologically real. But resolution is the seating of a consequential distinction, and the Cut is relation. A real Aim is therefore already a coarse seated relational constraint at its grain. What remains open is narrower: how that Aim combines with the rest of present ground to make a particular finer Cut consequential.

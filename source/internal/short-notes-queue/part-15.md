> If every new relation can add usable ground and resolving capacity, why does scarcity survive rather than eventually disappearing?

> **Adding participants grows the number of things. It grows the number of possible relationships faster. Relation can increase capacity and still lose the race to possibility.**

> **Adding things grows the inventory. Adding relations grows the future.**

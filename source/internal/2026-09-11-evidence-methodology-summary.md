# Evidence Methodology Summary — September 11

Status: Internal continuity note

The framework now has an explicit canonical methodology for ontology-conditioned evidential weighting in [`../evidence-and-interpretation.md`](../evidence-and-interpretation.md).

The reference profile is qualitative rather than numerical:

`E_O(x) = <C, S, I, R, F^-1>`

The dimensions are chronology constraint, specificity, independence, recurrence, and inverse interpretive freedom.

The central framework-native rule is:

> **Evidence is observation becoming constraint on the available interpretations.**

The retained guardrail is:

> **Correspondence may be evidence for an ontology without being evidence of intention.**

This means personally encountered correspondences can be preserved as ontology-relevant evidence while claims of a sender, System tutorial, deliberate arrangement, or particular hidden mechanism remain separate and require additional constraint.

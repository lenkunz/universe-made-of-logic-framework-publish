## Episode-specific article focus

Title: **What Is a Flame If Its Matter Never Stays?**

Subtitle direction: **A candle keeps its identity while fuel, oxygen, heat, and reaction products continuously pass through it.**

Lead with the ordinary identity puzzle, not framework vocabulary:

> Which part of a candle flame is the same part that was here five minutes ago?

Build the article through the established combustion story:

1. heat melts wax;
2. capillary action carries liquid wax through the wick;
3. wax vaporizes and gaseous fuel reacts with oxygen;
4. products and hot gases leave while new reactants enter;
5. the flame is a diffusion/reaction structure with continuous throughput;
6. Earth gravity creates buoyant convection that helps shape the familiar teardrop;
7. NASA microgravity experiments show the form reorganizing into a much more spherical/hemispherical flame when that transport relation changes.

Only after this ordinary story is clear, introduce My GUT Deduction's **identity through alteration** as an interpretive lens.

The public payoff is not “the framework explains fire.” It is:

> **A stable thing does not always require stable material membership. A continuing organization can preserve identity while its members and configuration change.**

Keep the strongest semantic guardrail visible:

> framework `budget` ≠ heat, joules, chemical energy, fuel, oxygen, or reaction rate.

The candle is a structural illustration, not a derivation of combustion from the framework.

## Google Flow / hero image concept

Create an editorial landscape image, **16:9**, communicating the central tension: **one flame persists while its matter continuously changes**.

Visual concept:

A single candle near the center-right, with a crisp recognizable flame silhouette acting as the stable focal form. Inside and around that flame, show a subtle continuous material exchange: pale wax rising through the wick and becoming translucent vapor, fresh air curling inward, and faint combustion products streaming upward and away. The visual should suggest that the flame is being continuously rebuilt from moving material without turning into a technical diagram.

Add a second, ghosted or spatially separated flame form in the background: a small rounded/spherical blue microgravity flame, clearly related to the same candle process but with a different supported geometry. This should make the environmental dependence of the shape visible without requiring labels.

Editorial science-magazine style, tactile and physically grounded. Dark neutral background with warm practical candle light, subtle airflow textures, restrained blue microgravity counterpoint. The image should feel like a portrait of a process rather than a stock photo of a candle.

Keep generous negative space on the **left third** for title placement.

Avoid:

- generic galaxy/cosmic imagery;
- glowing brains;
- circuit boards;
- floating equations;
- sci-fi holograms;
- infographic arrows or labels;
- literal framework diagrams;
- duplicated rows of candles;
- mystical souls leaving the flame;
- text baked into the image.

The conceptual hero is **continuity through turnover**, not “fire looks pretty.”

# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

Use the loaded sources, especially `02-notebooklm-source.md`, as the factual and semantic guide for this episode.

Open with this question:

> **Which part of a candle flame is the same part that was here five minutes ago?**

Build the discussion in this order:

`ordinary identity puzzle`
→ `how a candle actually feeds the flame`
→ `diffusion flame and continuous material throughput`
→ `why gravity helps create the familiar teardrop shape`
→ `NASA microgravity result: a different transport regime produces a much more spherical/hemispherical flame`
→ `what “same thing” can mean when members and configuration change`
→ `My GUT Deduction: identity through alteration`
→ `hard status boundary`
→ `closing identity question`

Emphasize these research points:

- wax melts, moves through the wick by capillary action, vaporizes, and supplies gaseous fuel;
- oxygen enters and combustion products leave, so the participating matter is continuously replaced and transformed;
- NIST classifies this kind of system as a diffusion flame;
- a flame can be steady or quasi-steady macroscopically while transport and reaction continue microscopically;
- Earth gravity drives buoyant convection that contributes to the familiar elongated flame;
- in NASA microgravity experiments, reduced buoyant convection changes transport and produces a small spherical/hemispherical flame with different soot/color behavior.

Only after the ordinary science is clear, introduce the framework connection:

> **identity through alteration**

Frame it as a structural lens: a changing domain may remain one while enough relational organization continues to support its identity.

Keep these boundaries explicit:

- candle science does **not** prove My GUT Deduction;
- do not classify a flame as empirically “Rung 4”;
- framework `budget` is **not** chemical energy, heat, fuel, oxygen, or reaction rate;
- do not replace combustion chemistry or fluid dynamics with framework vocabulary;
- do not turn the discussion into generic “everything is a pattern” philosophy.

Use microgravity as the strongest conceptual turn: even the familiar teardrop shape is partly environmental, yet it remains meaningful to talk about the continuing flame.

Close with:

> **If none of the matter has to stay, and even the shape can change, what exactly must survive before we stop calling it the same thing?**

## External sources

- [ ] American Chemical Society, **Shining a Light on Candles**  
  https://inchemistry.acs.org/atomic-news/shining-light-on-candles.html
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: clear ordinary account of wax melting, capillary action through the wick, vaporization, and gaseous wax as the main fuel actually burning.
  - Established explanation supported: the flame is continuously fed by newly vaporized wax rather than being a fixed burning object.

- [ ] American Chemical Society, **What is a Chemical Reaction?**, candle demonstration  
  https://www.acs.org/middleschoolchemistry/lessonplans/chapter6/lesson1.html
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: simple chemistry boundary: wax and oxygen are reactants, combustion creates carbon dioxide and water vapor, and removing oxygen stops the reaction.
  - Established explanation supported: matter is transformed and products leave; the flame requires continuing reactant supply.

- [ ] NIST, **Diffusion Flame** glossary  
  https://www.nist.gov/glossary-term/21991
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: concise authoritative definition.
  - Established explanation supported: a diffusion flame is one where fuel and air mix/diffuse together at the combustion region.

- [ ] NIST, **Characterization of Candles Flames as Ignition Sources**  
  https://www.nist.gov/publications/characterization-candles-flames-ignition-sources
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: measured candle burning behavior including mass burning rate, flame height, heat flux, and paraffin properties.
  - Established explanation supported: a candle flame is a measurable sustained combustion system with reproducible macroscopic behavior.

- [ ] NASA, **Why NASA is studying flames in space**  
  https://science.nasa.gov/biological-physical/resources/explainers-infographics/why-nasa-is-studying-flames-in-space/
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: accessible explanation that ordinary gravity creates buoyant circulation and the familiar elongated candle flame, while microgravity removes that circulation and produces a spherical flame.
  - Established explanation supported: familiar flame shape depends on transport relations, not on a fixed parcel of matter.

- [ ] NASA, **Candle Flame – 1g vs Microgravity**  
  https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/candle-flame-1g-vs-microgravity/
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: concise comparison of teardrop/yellow Earth flame and spherical/blue microgravity flame.
  - Established explanation supported: buoyant convection changes flame structure and soot transport.

- [ ] NASA Technical Reports Server, **Candle flames in microgravity**  
  https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19960008390
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: research-level treatment of candle flames as non-propagating diffusion flames; in microgravity diffusion dominates transport and quasi-steady flames can persist.
  - Established explanation supported: a flame can maintain a near-steady macroscopic state while matter and heat continuously flow through it.

- [ ] NASA, **Flammability of Materials on the Moon (FM2)**  
  https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/physical-sciences-program/combustion-science/flammability-of-materials-on-the-moon-fm2/
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: current 2026 work on flame behavior across partial-gravity conditions.
  - Established explanation supported: gravity level changes flame size, intensity, shape, spread, and transport conditions; flame organization is environment-dependent.

## Source discipline

Keep external combustion science separate from framework interpretation.

Do not use a candle as evidence that the framework's ontology is established.

Do not equate framework `budget` with heat, chemical energy, fuel supply, oxygen concentration, or reaction rate. Those are established physical quantities and remain surfaced physical accounting from the framework's point of view.

## Central question

A candle flame can keep a recognizable shape and location while fuel and oxygen continuously enter, reaction products and heat continuously leave, and the molecules participating in the flame are constantly replaced. What, physically, is staying the same?

## Central payoff

> **A stable thing does not always have to be made from stable members. Sometimes what persists is an organized relation among flows.**

Ordinary combustion already gives a clean example: the familiar flame is a maintained diffusion/reaction structure, not a fixed parcel of matter. Change the transport conditions, especially buoyant convection, and the stable flame form changes with them.

## Framework status

- **External-science-led topic first.** Combustion, capillary fuel delivery, diffusion flames, buoyant convection, microgravity flame shape, and quasi-steady behavior are established research/engineering territory.
- **Framework-native connection:** Level 4 / identity through alteration; a changing domain can remain one while enough relational pattern remains seated through change. The framework also states that persistence means maintaining the distinctions by which a domain remains distinguishable.
- **Interpretive boundary:** the candle does not prove My GUT Deduction, and the framework's primitive `budget` must not be equated with thermal energy, chemical free energy, or fuel. The flame is used as a structural example of identity-through-turnover, not as evidence for a new combustion theory.

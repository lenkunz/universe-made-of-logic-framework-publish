## Central question

A candle flame can keep a recognizable shape and location while its fuel, oxygen, hot gases, and reaction products continuously pass through it. If the matter does not stay, what carries the flame's identity?

## Central payoff

> **A stable thing does not always have to be made from stable members. Sometimes what persists is an organized relation among flows.**

## Framework status

- External science: candle combustion, capillary transport, fuel vaporization, diffusion, buoyant convection, and microgravity flame behavior are established physical subjects.
- Framework reading: the flame illustrates identity through alteration—enough relational organization may remain consequential even while members and configuration change.
- Interpretive boundary: the candle does not prove the framework, classify the flame at a framework level, or replace chemistry and fluid dynamics. Framework budget is not heat, joules, chemical energy, fuel, oxygen, or reaction rate.

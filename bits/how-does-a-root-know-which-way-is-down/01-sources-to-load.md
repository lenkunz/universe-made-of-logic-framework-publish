## External sources

1. Nishimura, T. et al. (2023), “Cell polarity linked to gravity sensing is generated by LZY translocation from statoliths to the plasma membrane,” *Science*.
   - DOI: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adh9978
   - PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37561884/
   - Why needed: Primary experimental evidence for a molecular bridge between sedimenting statoliths and lower-side polarity in gravity-sensing root cells.

2. Friml, J. et al. (2002), “Lateral relocation of auxin efflux regulator PIN3 mediates tropism in Arabidopsis,” *Nature*.
   - DOI: https://www.nature.com/articles/415806a
   - PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11845211/
   - Why needed: Primary evidence that gravity reorientation changes PIN3 localization and redirects auxin flow.

3. Band, L. R. et al. (2012), “Root gravitropism is regulated by a transient lateral auxin gradient controlled by a tipping-point mechanism,” *PNAS*.
   - DOI: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1201498109
   - Why needed: Establishes that the lateral auxin gradient is transient and is switched off as the root approaches the appropriate orientation.

4. Swarup, R. et al. (2005), “Root gravitropism requires lateral root cap and epidermal cells for transport and response to a mobile auxin signal,” *Nature Cell Biology*.
   - DOI: https://www.nature.com/articles/ncb1316
   - PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16244669/
   - Why needed: Shows how the signal moves from gravity-sensing columella cells through the lateral root cap to elongating epidermal cells, where differential growth is executed.

5. Harrison, B. R. and Masson, P. H. (2008), “ARL2, ARG1 and PIN3 define a gravity signal transduction pathway in root statocytes,” *The Plant Journal*.
   - PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18047472/
   - Why needed: Additional primary evidence for the statocyte signal-transduction pathway and PIN3 placement.

## Source boundary

The plant papers establish the biological mechanism. The framework sources supply a later interpretive lens only. Do not use framework language to replace plant physiology, and do not present the external papers as evidence that My GUT Deduction is established.

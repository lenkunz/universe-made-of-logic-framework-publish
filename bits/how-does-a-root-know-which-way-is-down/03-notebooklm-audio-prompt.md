# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

Open with: **“If a seedling is laid on its side, how does its root discover which way is down without eyes, ears, or a brain?”**

Build the discussion in this order:

1. Start with the everyday observation that a sideways root curves down, while clarifying that “know” is metaphorical.
2. Explain the ordinary mechanism clearly: statocytes and starch-filled statoliths in the root cap; LZY movement to the lower membrane; PIN-directed auxin redistribution; transport through the lateral root cap to the elongation zone; more auxin inhibiting elongation on the lower flank; unequal growth making the bend.
3. Emphasize the research surprise that sensing and bending occur in different tissues, so direction must be converted, transported, and acted on.
4. Highlight Band et al.'s transient auxin gradient and tipping-point result: the asymmetry is a correction signal that fades as orientation is restored.
5. Only then introduce My GUT Deduction as a limited interpretive lens: “A path can carry orientation” and “Bias first. Fine implementation follows.” The useful correspondence is that a coarse directional relation is realized through many fine local updates.
6. Include one skeptical exchange: **Is the framework explaining gravitropism?** Answer no. Established plant biology explains the mechanism; the framework has not derived the molecules, growth law, or a new prediction.

Keep plant science and framework interpretation explicitly separate. Do not imply consciousness or Aim, do not call statoliths framework anchors, do not equate auxin with a framework budget, and do not present My GUT Deduction as established science. Do not invent evidence beyond the source dossier.

End with: **“If a root can find down by turning one direction into many local growth differences, how much of a path must exist before its direction begins to matter?”**

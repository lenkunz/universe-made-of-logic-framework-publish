## Central question

If a seedling is laid on its side, how can its root detect which direction is down and turn that information into a bend, without eyes, a brain, or a map of its future path?

## Central payoff

A root does not pull itself downward as a rigid object. Cells in its tip convert gravity into polarity, auxin transport converts that polarity into a transient chemical asymmetry, and unequal growth converts the asymmetry into curvature. Direction becomes a path through many local changes.

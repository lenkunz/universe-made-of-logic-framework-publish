## Central question

Why can a four-bar square-shaped frame shear into a diamond while the same frame becomes rigid when one diagonal brace is added?

## Central payoff

Rigidity can belong to the pattern of constraints rather than to the pieces individually. Four fixed-length sides still leave a shape-changing motion available; a diagonal fixes an additional distance and removes that mechanism, decomposing the frame into triangles.

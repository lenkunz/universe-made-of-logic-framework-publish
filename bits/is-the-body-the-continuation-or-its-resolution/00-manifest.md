## Central question

If a conversation can continue through successive messages without being identical to any one message, could a body be the local Stage resolution through which a continuing conscious relation participates rather than the entirety of that continuation?

## Central payoff

> **The body can be completely real and causally necessary without being assumed to exhaust the identity of the continuing relational domain.**

Inside the proposal, projection/interference belong to the continuing relation while bodily, neural, sensory, and motor events are the fine local resolution through which that relation becomes consequential on this Stage.

## Framework status

- Framework-native ground: identity through alteration; domains are grain-relative; Stage is relation meeting an anchoring domain; projection/interference and fine Stage resolution are distinct.
- User's exploratory proposal: the body is a dense local host/interface and physical expression of perceptual-domain resolution, not necessarily the separate origin or total identity of the continuation.
- Unresolved mechanism: the framework has not derived a sufficient criterion for subjective consciousness, the exact identity relation across interruption, or an experimental discriminator between this proposal and conventional neural accounts.
- Not established: a separable soul, survival after death, mind uploading, interchangeable bodies, consciousness without neural/bodily support, or a literal quantum computer made of meat.

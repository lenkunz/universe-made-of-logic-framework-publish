## Central question

Why can water wash oily dirt away after soap is added, even though oil and water ordinarily resist mixing?

## Central payoff

Soap does not make oil become water. Surfactant molecules organize at oil-water interfaces and can package oily material into structures that remain dispersed in water long enough to be rinsed away. A relation at a boundary can change what the participants can do together without erasing their difference.

## Central question

Why does heating a popcorn kernel produce a sudden explosive expansion instead of letting its water simply escape gradually?

## Central payoff

The kernel works because several ordinary properties are arranged together: a sufficiently intact low-permeability hull delays escape, internal water builds vapor pressure while starch softens, and hull failure abruptly changes the available route. The pop is a threshold event produced by stored pressure plus a boundary that suddenly stops holding.

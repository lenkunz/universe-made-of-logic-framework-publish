## Episode-specific article focus

Build the established mechanism first: internal moisture, pressure buildup, low-permeability pericarp, starch softening, threshold rupture, rapid expansion and setting. Make the damaged/leaky-kernel evidence do real explanatory work.

The conceptual turn is that the hull's temporary success at blocking escape is part of what prepares the later explosive change. Only then introduce the framework's boundary/routing lens as a candidate correspondence. Keep the framework short and explicitly non-derivational.

Show one side still recognizable as a glossy golden-brown kernel while a single fissure opens and pale starch foam starts expanding outward from it. The visual should capture **stored internal pressure becoming rapid expansion when one route opens**. Use high-speed-food-photography realism, crisp shell texture, soft white starch, a few restrained wisps of hot vapor, and generous dark negative space on the left for title placement.

Avoid exploded-diagram labels, arrows, equations, pressure gauges, glowing energy, cartoon explosions, fireballs, giant steam clouds, sci-fi styling, galaxies, brains, generic AI aesthetics, and baked-in title text. Do not imply that the kernel is filled with an empty gas bubble or that all of its water exists as steam before rupture.

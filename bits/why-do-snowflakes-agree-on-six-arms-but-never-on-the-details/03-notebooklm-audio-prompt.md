# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

Use the loaded sources, especially `02-notebooklm-source.md`, as the factual and semantic guide for this episode.

Open with: **How can six arms follow the same rule so closely while no detailed snowflake design has to be copied six times?**

Build the discussion from the ordinary science first: hexagonal ice structure and sixfold symmetry; temperature/supersaturation changing plates, columns, needles and dendrites; diffusion and attachment kinetics; branching instability; then the way a crystal's changing path through a cloud accumulates fine differences. Mention the caveat that atmospheric ice can show stacking disorder and threefold forms, so “all snowflakes are perfect sixfold stars” is an oversimplification.

The conceptual turn is that **a constraint can be exact about one level without specifying every finer detail**. Only after that is clear, introduce My GUT Deduction's narrower analogy: its current definition of coarse is a real constraint that leaves some finer distinctions unseated. Use the snowflake to clarify that “coarse” does not mean blurry or weak.

Keep the boundary explicit: snow-crystal physics does not prove the framework, and framework resolution/budget must not replace crystallography, diffusion, thermodynamics, surface kinetics, or instability as the scientific explanation.

Useful skeptical question: if the broad rule does not specify the branches, what actually selects the branches? Answer with the changing physical growth conditions and accumulated geometry, not with framework vocabulary.

Close on: **If a rule can stay exact while leaving countless details open, how much natural order is a blueprint, and how much is constraint becoming specific as history grows?**

Avoid a catalogue of snowflake types, generic “everything is information” claims, and any suggestion that identical-looking arms communicate a hidden plan to one another.

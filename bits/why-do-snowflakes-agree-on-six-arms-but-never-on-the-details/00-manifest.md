## Central question

Why can snow crystals obey a strong shared sixfold constraint while producing an enormous variety of fine forms?

## Central payoff

> **A rule can be exact about the structure it constrains without specifying every detail that later grows inside that structure.**

Snow crystal science supplies the ordinary mechanism: hexagonal ice structure constrains directions, while temperature, supersaturation, diffusion, attachment kinetics, instabilities, and each crystal's atmospheric path shape the details.

## External sources

- [ ] Andy Clark and David Chalmers, [The Extended Mind](https://consc.net/papers/extended.html), 1998, author-hosted text. Use as a philosophical comparison: the authors argue that environmental operations can participate in cognition. Their distinction between cognitive process and conscious experience is particularly useful. This is not empirical proof of extended subjective experience or of this framework.
- [ ] OpenStax, [17.2 Speed of Sound](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/17-2-speed-of-sound). Supports the limited side note that mechanical response and density constrain sound speed. The framework must derive a physical mapping; it cannot assign a number from relation count alone. Keep rod, fluid, and general solid modes distinct if expanding this note.

Both sources were checked on 2026-09-07. Cite their actual claims; do not treat either as endorsing the ontology. The main running example is an explicitly constructed programming scenario, not an empirical study.

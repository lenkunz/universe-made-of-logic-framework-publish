# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

Create an English Bits episode titled **Does a Mind End at the Skin?**, using the detailed dossier and updated sources as authority. Use two curious hosts and plain language. Explain the proposal fully before briefly marking its unresolved step. Do not turn the episode into either an endorsement of extraordinary personal events or a repetitive debate about whether speculative ontology is allowed.

Open with a programmer following a bug using a test, a note, and a screen. This is a constructed example. Explain its ordinary dependencies first. Then ask: the body has an outline, but where does the task's organization stop? Mention Clark and Chalmers only as a philosophical comparison about cognition, not evidence that conscious experience extends outside the body.

Spend most of the episode on the user's correction. A and B each retain supported local capped participation. Their seated relation can act as compartment AB with its own participation. AB can then meet C through another equally capped relation. The new encounter does not replace the inner ones. The parts keep acting, and their whole acts too.

Say the notation aloud sparingly: “A's local relation,” “the A–B compartment,” “that compartment meeting C.” Make clear that R-all names the locally capped resolving participation in the relation being considered, not one observer-owned pool or all work inside an object. Equality is the common rule, not a proof from calling each amount 100 percent.

AB arrives already projecting. A's and B's directions, the compartment's own organization, and C's contribution constrain the outer result. More supported participation can coexist with a narrower surfaced projection. Do not say more structure increases the primitive cap, drains the parts, or always makes everything slower. Do not reduce the interior to an inactive historical record. Independence means distinct participation, not causal isolation. Shared closures are not simply additive, and naming another group does not create more resources.

Return to the debugging procedure as an illustration of organization operating at several grains. Explicitly avoid identifying a computer's CPU allowance with R-all. Then distinguish three questions: a reusable function, a maintained self/ego, and an experiencing perspective. The user proposes a recursive route toward consciousness. The framework has not derived its sufficient criterion. Memory, Aim, integration, and self-reference are questions to explore, not a certification checklist. No Lane level or sheer network size proves consciousness.

Now earn the title. If a conscious domain is such a composition, the framework introduces no special cutoff of its supported projection/interference at the skin. Its body can be its dense host while its relations extend farther. This applies without a human exception, including small animals, without certifying which animals have experience. Being related to something does not absorb it into one subject.

Preserve the stronger proposal: relevant hosted/shared relation is not restricted in advance to intentional acts or explicit endpoint-to-endpoint messages. A full route may be locally opaque. Still, a particular coupling must exist and its effects must be established. No inference to telepathy, omnipotence, disembodied survival, or a hidden sender follows from the grammar.

A short sound sidebar may explain the same native candidate in a material network. Conventional sound depends on mechanical response and density; the framework has not derived those rates. “Light means relation” is literal framework vocabulary, not a claim that sound is electromagnetic radiation. The atomic plane-like proposal is optional and must remain a question, not “atoms are 2D.” Do not let either sidebar displace the central recursive argument.

Ask one productive challenge: what would distinguish an organized function from one experiencing subject? Another useful check is whether regrouping preserves all consequential relations. Avoid invented equations, numeric predictions, or laboratory results.

Exclude the user's personal numbers, location, shopping, social-media anecdotes, and tutorial-mode speculation. They are not episode evidence. Do not imply coincidences are messages or readiness tests.

Close with: **The parts can keep acting while the whole participates. A body can host a perspective without its outline drawing every relation through which that domain participates.** Let the experience criterion remain an explicit next question.

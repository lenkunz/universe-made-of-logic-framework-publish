## Central question

If a whole can participate while its inner relations keep participating, what makes its body's surface the endpoint of its relational reach?

## Central payoff

> **The cap stays local. The parts keep acting. Their whole can become another participant.**

That recursion gives the framework a route toward function and maintained identity and a candidate direction for consciousness. A body's outline need not bound a domain's supported relations. Subjective experience and the reach of a particular coupling remain distinct questions.

## External sources

- [ ] NIST — Thomson, Levine & Shim, **Deformation of Metals**: https://www.nist.gov/publications/deformation-metals
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: compact authoritative explanation of plastic deformation through dislocation production and motion, increasing dislocation density, interactions, tangling, complex 3D structures, and work hardening.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: increasing dislocation interactions can impede mobile dislocations and make further deformation more difficult.
- [ ] NIST — Levine & Thomson, **A Statistical Connection Between Dislocations and Mechanical Properties**: https://www.nist.gov/publications/statistical-connection-between-dislocations-and-mechanical-properties
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: supports the contrast between extremely complex evolving dislocation processes and simpler macroscopic mechanical response.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: mechanical properties can track evolving statistical dislocation structure.
- [ ] NIST — Pham et al., **The Strain Path Dependence of Plastic Deformation Response of AA5754: Experiment and Modeling**: https://www.nist.gov/publications/strain-path-dependence-plastic-deformation-response-aa5754-experiment-and-modeling
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: adds an important caveat that deformation history and loading direction matter, with texture, aging and recovery affecting later response.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: work hardening is not one universal monotonic rule independent of material, temperature and strain path.
- [ ] NIST — **Metallurgical Principles: Extrusions**, section on annealing/recovery: https://materialsdata.nist.gov/bitstream/handle/11115/174/Metallurgical%20Principles%20Extrusions.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: explains recovery/recrystallization and why heating can reduce work hardening by enabling dislocation rearrangement and annihilation.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: deformation-created microstructure can later be reorganized; hardening is history carried in present structure, not an irreversible mystical memory.

## Source discipline

Keep external observations / established explanation separate from framework interpretation.

Do not use the source list itself as an excuse to promote a candidate correspondence into established science. In particular, do not equate dislocations, stress, strain, energy, or hardness with framework computational budget.

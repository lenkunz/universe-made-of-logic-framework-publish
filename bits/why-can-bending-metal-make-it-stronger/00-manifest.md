## Central question

Why can plastic deformation make many metals harder to deform again, even though deformation sounds like damage?

## Central payoff

Plastic deformation changes the metal's internal dislocation structure. The defects that permit crystal slip multiply and interact, increasingly obstructing one another, so further plastic flow requires more stress. The event does not merely happen to the metal; it changes the state through which the next event must happen.

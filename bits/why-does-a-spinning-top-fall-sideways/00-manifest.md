## Central question

Why does gravity make a stationary tilted top fall over, while the same gravity makes a fast-spinning top sweep sideways around the vertical instead?

## Central payoff

> **A push does not determine a change by itself. What is already in motion matters to what the same push can change.**

In standard mechanics, gravity produces a torque. For a rapidly spinning top, that torque changes the direction of an already-large angular-momentum vector, producing precession rather than immediate toppling.

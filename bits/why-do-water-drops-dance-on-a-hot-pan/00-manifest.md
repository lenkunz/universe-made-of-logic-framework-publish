## Central question

Why can a water drop disappear violently on a merely hot pan, yet survive longer, skate, and bounce when the pan becomes much hotter?

## Central payoff

> **More intense forcing can create an intermediate layer that changes the route of interaction, so “hotter” does not always mean “more direct contact.”**

## Central question

Why can a sequence of gentle pushes make an oscillator move dramatically when the same pushes at the wrong timing accomplish very little?

## Central payoff

> **A small input can accumulate into a large response when each new input meets a state that lets it add rather than undo.**

Resonance is not mysterious amplification from nowhere. A driven oscillator has its own natural dynamics; periodic forcing can transfer energy especially effectively near resonance, while damping removes energy and limits the response.

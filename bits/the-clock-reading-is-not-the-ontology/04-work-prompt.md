The composite `R_all` is bounded.

If both independent projections tried to preserve the full maximum independently, their vector demand would exceed the bound.

The seated relation therefore normalizes/contracts the available projections until:

`R_a² + R_p² = R_all²`

Use this to express the qualitative mechanism:

`more anchor-relative projection`
→ `less available fine-persistence projection`
→ `fewer externally comparable persistence cycles`

Avoid saying “time itself slows.”

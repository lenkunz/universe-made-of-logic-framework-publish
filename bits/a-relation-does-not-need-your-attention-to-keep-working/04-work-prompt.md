## Article direction

Use the title **A Relation Does Not Need Your Attention to Keep Working** and the subtitle direction *The lake house does not disappear when you think about dinner.* Aim for roughly 1,300–1,700 words, adjusting for clarity.

Open with the owner leaving a lakeside house and attending to ordinary life elsewhere. Return to a bill, a storm, a visitor's request, or the key. Give the ordinary account before framework vocabulary: buildings, records, tools, people, institutions, and physical continuity preserve different practical constraints.

Use the written program as the decisive comparison. A programmer can leave after saving code; the code can remain consequential. But stored code is not silently executing every possible output. It still requires an actual supported arrangement. Preserve the difference between **available constraint** and **continuous autonomous execution**.

Introduce only the necessary framework statements: `stored relation ≠ present accessibility ≠ current fine rendering`; **Dormant is not erased**; **Unattended is not erased**. Explain that a seated relation can become ground for a later encounter without its originator continuously reconstructing it.

Use the conversation/message example briefly. Fold **A Conversation Is Not Any One Message**, **A Program Does Not Need Its Programmer to Keep Thinking**, and **Identity Is a Rhythm, Not a Snapshot** into this article rather than presenting them as three other promised episodes.

End with identity-through-alteration as rhythm without repetition. Make clear that rhythm is a metaphor for maintained relational continuation, not a literal frequency or master clock.

## Semantic discipline

Do not claim property is maintained by thought, code runs without infrastructure, every dormant relation is active in every sense, records are conscious, or all detail survives somewhere. Do not claim this proves disembodied continuity. Keep “working” defined as remaining able to constrain later resolution.

Use simple words, generous spacing, and one clear progression. The article must stand alone without the audio. Use only verified existing URLs in any Article Slot; leave the slot empty rather than inventing the new Bit's URL.

# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

Create an English Bits episode titled **A Relation Does Not Need Your Attention to Keep Working**, using the detailed dossier and source revision **R20260911-RHY-01** as authority. Use two curious hosts and plain language.

Open with the lakeside house. The owner leaves, thinks about other things, and later receives a bill, hears about storm damage, answers a friend's request, or returns with the key. Ask why the relation can still matter when it was not continuously present in attention.

Give the ordinary answer first: buildings, records, keys, people, institutions, and physical continuity preserve different parts of the practical relation. These are constructed examples, not empirical proof of the ontology.

Then introduce the written program. A programmer can save code and go home. The program does not need the programmer to keep thinking about it, but stored code is not continuously executing every possible output. It still needs a machine, input, runtime, permissions, and other support. Make this distinction the center of the episode:

> **remain able to constrain later resolution** is not the same as **autonomously produce new fine consequences during the pause**.

Only then introduce the framework. Explain `stored relation ≠ present accessibility ≠ current fine rendering`, followed by **Dormant is not erased** and the new clarification **Unattended is not erased either**. A seated relation can wait as ground until another encounter makes it consequential.

Use the paused conversation as one short example. A conversation is not one message. Preserved context can let another message continue the relation, but a transcript does not prove an autonomous mind kept resolving between turns. If too much consequential context is lost, continuation can fail.

Bring in **identity is a rhythm, not a snapshot** as the closing metaphor for identity through alteration. Rhythm means changing resolutions preserve enough relation to find another compatible beat. It is not a literal frequency, a universal clock, or a solved consciousness theory.

Ask one productive test: **What does the later encounter actually reuse, and what still has to be supplied again?** Apply it to the house, code, and conversation.

Do not claim psychic action at a distance, hidden continuous execution, perfect storage of all detail, consciousness in records, or survival of consciousness without physical support. Do not make attention irrelevant; it can change accessibility and which distinctions resolve.

Close at the house door: **The house was quiet in the owner's attention, not absent from relation. Silence was not absence. The relation was waiting for another beat.**

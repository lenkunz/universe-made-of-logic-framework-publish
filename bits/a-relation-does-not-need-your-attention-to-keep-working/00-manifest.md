## Central question

If a person stops thinking about a lakeside house, or a programmer stops thinking about written code, why can the old relation still shape what happens later?

## Central payoff

> **Attention can make a relation accessible without being the thing that keeps an already-seated relation real.**

Once a relation has become supported ground, a later encounter can begin from it. It does not need its originator to continuously re-render it. But stored constraint is not autonomous execution: another supported encounter is still needed for a new consequence to resolve.

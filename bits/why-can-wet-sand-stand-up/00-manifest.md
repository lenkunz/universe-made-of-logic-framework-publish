## Central question

Why does a small amount of water let loose sand support steep walls and towers, while dry sand slumps and too much water weakens the trick?

## Central payoff

Water does not simply make each grain stronger. In partially wet sand, curved liquid menisci form capillary bridges between neighboring grains. Surface tension and capillary pressure create attractive forces across many contacts, turning a loose granular packing into a cohesive network. The useful structure belongs to the grain-water-air arrangement, and too much water changes that liquid morphology rather than monotonically adding strength.

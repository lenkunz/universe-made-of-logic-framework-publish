## External sources

- [ ] USGS Water Science School — Water Density: https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/water-density
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: authoritative accessible density data, ~9% expansion on freezing, 4 °C maximum-density context, and lake consequences.
  - Established observation / explanation: ordinary ice is less dense than liquid water; fresh water is densest near 4 °C.
- [ ] OpenStax Biology 2e — Water: https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/2-2-water
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: clear account of hydrogen bonding and the open ice lattice.
  - Established observation / explanation: hydrogen-bond orientation spaces molecules farther apart in ice than in liquid water.
- [ ] Chemistry LibreTexts — Water: An Extraordinary Substance: https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/General_Chemistry/Map%3A_Structure_and_Properties_%28Tro%29/12%3A_Liquids_Solids_and_Intermolecular_Forces/12.08%3A_Water_-_An_Extraordinary_Substance
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: molecular geometry and open cage-like hydrogen-bonded ice structure.
  - Established observation / explanation: tetrahedral hydrogen bonding produces an open solid structure; liquid hydrogen bonds continually break and reform.

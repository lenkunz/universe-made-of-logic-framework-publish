## Central question

Why does solid water float on its own liquid when most substances become denser on freezing?

## Central payoff

Freezing does not merely make water molecules move less. Hydrogen bonding organizes ordinary ice into an open crystal structure that occupies more volume for the same mass. The property belongs to molecular arrangement as well as molecular identity.

# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

Use the loaded sources, especially `02-notebooklm-source.md`, as the factual and semantic guide for this episode.

Open with: **“Why can an intact onion sit beside you harmlessly, but cutting it makes your eyes water within seconds?”**

Build the ordinary chemistry first. Explain that cutting disrupts cellular compartmentalization, allowing sulfur-containing precursors and enzymes to meet. Describe the alliinase step and then lachrymatory factor synthase (LFS), which produces volatile propanethial S-oxide. The volatile irritant reaches the eye and stimulates protective tearing.

Emphasize the historical correction: the tear factor is not merely a finished chemical leaking out or a spontaneous by-product. The 2002 LFS discovery showed that a specific enzyme helps create it. Use LFS-suppressed/tearless onions as the experimental twist: changing the pathway greatly reduces lachrymatory-factor production and redirects some sulfur chemistry.

Then make the conceptual turn: **the ingredients existed before the cut, but the cut changed which relations were available.** Only here introduce My GUT Deduction's narrow lens: the Cut/relation semantics and `A + B → A + B + R_AB`. The candidate correspondence is that a new consequential relation can make an outcome available that is not a standalone property of either participant.

Ask skeptically: **“Is the framework explaining onion biochemistry?”** Answer no. Established chemistry explains alliinase, LFS, the volatile product, and tearing. The framework has not derived reaction kinetics, enzyme structure, diffusion, sensory transduction, or lacrimal physiology.

Do not say the onion consciously attacks the cook, that the chemistry proves Aim, that the lachrymatory factor simply sits pre-made in intact tissue, or that framework budget equals chemical energy, concentration, reaction rate, or tear volume.

Close with: **“If the ingredients were already there but the consequence appeared only after a boundary broke, was the tear-making property inside the onion all along, or did it belong to the new relations the cut made possible?”**

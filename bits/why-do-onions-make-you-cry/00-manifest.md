## Central question

Why does an intact onion sit harmlessly on the counter, while cutting it creates a volatile chemical that makes nearby eyes water?

## Central payoff

The irritating signal is not simply stored as a ready-made cloud inside the bulb. Tissue damage brings previously compartmentalized chemistry into a new relation, launching an enzyme-mediated pathway that produces the volatile lachrymatory factor.

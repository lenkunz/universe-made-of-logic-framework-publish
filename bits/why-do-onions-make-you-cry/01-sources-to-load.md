## External sources

- [ ] ACS Chemical Biology (2017), “Enzyme That Makes You Cry—Crystal Structure of Lachrymatory Factor Synthase from Allium cepa” — https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acschembio.7b00336
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: biochemical pathway and role of lachrymatory factor synthase (LFS).
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: LFS catalyzes formation of volatile (Z)-propanethial S-oxide from a sulfenic-acid substrate.

- [ ] Nature (2002), “An onion enzyme that makes the eyes water” — https://www.nature.com/articles/419685a
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: key discovery that onion lachrymatory factor is specifically synthesized by LFS rather than merely appearing as a spontaneous by-product.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: LFS is a distinct enzyme in the tear-factor pathway.

- [ ] Scientific Reports (2016), “Production and characterization of tearless and non-pungent onion” — https://www.nature.com/articles/srep23779
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: experimental intervention showing that reducing the relevant pathway greatly reduces lachrymatory factor production.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: tissue disruption triggers successive alliinase/LFS reactions; LFS-suppressed lines produce much less lachrymatory factor.

- [ ] ACS Molecule of the Week, syn-Propanethial S-oxide — https://www.acs.org/molecule-of-the-week/archive/p/syn-propanethial-s-oxide.html
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: concise general-audience chemistry reference.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: the volatile compound reaching the eyes stimulates tearing.

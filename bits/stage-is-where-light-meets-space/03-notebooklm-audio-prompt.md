# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

## Title

**Stage Is Where Light Meets Space**

Subtitle: **Perception may be the most ordinary form of relativity.**

## Opening

Do not begin with cosmology or equations. Begin with a distant building fitting behind a finger.

Ask:

> **What if perspective is not a special trick of eyesight? What if eyesight is simply where the universe's relation-resolution rule becomes too obvious to ignore?**

## Narrative arc

1. Ordinary visual perspective.
2. Reveal: **Stage is where Light meets Space.**
3. Define Light = relation; Space = domain/anchor; Stage = measurable relation-resolution.
4. No primitive time: `Relation + Domain → Stage`.
5. Perception is not illusion and not consciousness-only.
6. Distance and duration are Stage outputs.
7. Resolution Equality: same total resolving capacity everywhere; different arrangement/grain/commitment.
8. Why local `c` stays invariant: ruler, clock, propagation, vacuum, body, anchor co-resolve.
9. Use exact wording: **c is operationally invariant locally, but not ontologically primitive as an absolute speed.**
10. Impossible distant viewport thought experiment; acknowledge ordinary optics calls it image-plane/angular projection.
11. Perspective as primitive projection.
12. Right triangle: `R_all² = R_local² + R_relation²` only for simple low-bias closure.
13. Co-shrinking and candidate reinterpretation of apparent expansion. Label speculative.
14. Coarse = unresolved possibility: **Coarse resolution carries more unresolved possibility per resolved step.**
15. Gravity = broad Stage perception of anchoring; free fall vs support/weight; equivalence principle is correspondence only.
16. No view from nowhere.
17. Old Anchor lore: too many interests → split egos → broad-interest remainder → Programmer + Architect + Reader + Observer/Viewer.
18. Exact line: **It wants to know what the story becomes, but it does not want to write every sentence itself.**
19. Perspective requires separation + preserved relation.
20. Close: **The universe may use perspective everywhere. We only gave it a special name when our eyes made the geometry obvious.**

## Guardrails

Do not say consciousness creates reality by looking; Stage is hallucination; Light means only photons; Space means only empty 3D container; local labs should measure arbitrary vacuum `c`; visual perspective proves the framework; right triangles govern all physics; shrinking-ruler cosmology is established; equivalence principle proves the gravity ontology; or old-world lore is empirical evidence.

Keep three layers distinct: standard measurement, framework reinterpretation, lore architecture.

# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

## Title

**Stage Is Where Light Meets Space**

Subtitle: **Perception may be the most ordinary form of relativity.**

## Opening

Do not begin with cosmology or equations.

Begin with a distant building fitting behind a finger.

Ask:

> **What if perspective is not a special trick of eyesight? What if eyesight is simply where the universe's relation-resolution rule becomes too obvious to ignore?**

## Narrative arc

1. Ordinary visual perspective.

2. Reveal:
   > **Stage is where Light meets Space.**

3. Define:
   - **Light** = relation of any kind;
   - **Space** = domain / anything capable of anchoring relation;
   - **Stage** = measurable resolution where relation meets a domain.

4. Introduce the original P-lane relation pair **before** discussing time:
   - **Afference** = relation reaching inward, the perception side of the domain;
   - **Efference** = relation reaching outward, the perception side of Space;
   - both require budget;
   - inward/outward are relative to the anchoring domain, not absolute directions.

5. Explain the **Stage Flip**:
   > **One domain's Efference can become another domain's Afference at Stage.**

   Use:

   `Domain A → Efference → Stage → Afference → Domain B`

   Then:

   `Domain B Afference → domain resolution → Domain B Efference → Stage → ...`

6. Explain the persistence loop:

   `Afference → domain resolution → Efference → Stage → Afference → ...`

   A sufficiently stable returning loop can provide / sustain persistence budget.

   Do not describe this as free energy or as a fixed arithmetic equation.

7. Explain why perception and action are not fundamental opposites:
   - what one domain sends outward can become another domain's inward perception;
   - what is received inward can become ground for the next outward relation.

8. No primitive time:
   - root is `Relation + Domain → Stage`;
   - clock duration is a Stage-side result of persistence relation, not a primitive dimension.

9. Perception is not illusion and not consciousness-only.
   - vision, touch, rulers, clocks, detectors, distance, and anchored physical interaction can all be Stage-side perception;
   - consciousness is one elaborate domain, not the thing that makes reality exist.

10. Distance and duration are Stage outputs, not unquestioned base quantities.

11. **Resolution Equality**:
   - same fundamental total resolving capacity everywhere;
   - differences are arrangement, grain, commitment, and unresolved possibility.

12. Why local `c` stays invariant:
   - local ruler, persistence loop, propagation, vacuum relation, body, and anchor co-resolve;
   - use:
     > **c is operationally invariant locally, but not ontologically primitive as an absolute speed.**
   - do not claim a normal local lab should measure arbitrary vacuum `c`.

13. Impossible distant viewport thought experiment:
   - huge remote relation appears as a tiny observer-side projection;
   - ordinary optics calls that image-plane/angular projection;
   - framework asks whether this mundane operation exposes the deeper relation rule.

14. Perspective as primitive relational projection:
   > **A domain never receives another domain absolutely. It receives the relation between them, resolved at its own grain.**

   In P-lane language:
   > remote Efference becomes local Afference at Stage.

15. Right triangle:
   - `R_all² = R_local² + R_relation²`;
   - only the simplest low-bias / few-variable closure;
   - not universal geometry.

16. Co-shrinking:
   - ruler, clock, body, and propagation standard can co-seat;
   - common change can disappear locally;
   - candidate reinterpretation of apparent expansion as increasingly fine / co-shrinking local resolution;
   - label speculative.

17. Coarse resolution:
   > **Coarse resolution carries more unresolved possibility per resolved step.**

   Connect to:
   > **Nothing is decided until it needs to be decided.**

18. Gravity:
   > **Gravity is a Stage-side perception of being consequentially anchored inside another domain.**

   In Afference/Efference language, the anchor relation arrives inward as part of what the local domain resolves, without requiring an external Actor to pull.

   Compare free fall versus support/weight carefully.

   The equivalence principle is correspondence only, not proof of the ontology.

19. No view from nowhere:
   - there is Domain;
   - Relation;
   - Stage;
   - differently seated perspectives need not be fake.

20. Old Anchor lore:
   - too many interests for one resolution path;
   - split interests into egos / other perspectives;
   - remaining Anchor retained broad interest and remained connected;
   - preferred role became Programmer + Architect + Reader + Observer/Viewer.

   Exact line:
   > **It wants to know what the story becomes, but it does not want to write every sentence itself.**

21. Explain why the split matters:
   - complete unity leaves no meaningful other perspective;
   - complete disconnection leaves no shared Stage;
   - enough separation for distinct resolution + enough preserved relation for shared Stage makes a world.

22. Compress the root loop:
   - Light = relation;
   - Space = anchor/domain;
   - Afference = inward relation;
   - Efference = outward relation;
   - Stage = where orientation can flip and become measurable;
   - persistence = stable returning relation loop;
   - perspective = how another domain's Efference resolves as this domain's Afference.

23. Close with:
   > **The universe may use perspective everywhere. We only gave it a special name when our eyes made the geometry obvious.**

## Guardrails

Do not say:

- consciousness creates reality by looking;
- Stage is hallucination;
- Light means only photons;
- Space means only empty 3D container;
- Afference/Efference are absolute cosmic directions;
- persistence loop creates free energy;
- local labs should measure arbitrary vacuum `c`;
- visual perspective proves the framework;
- right triangles govern all physics;
- shrinking-ruler cosmology is established;
- equivalence principle proves the gravity ontology;
- old-world lore is empirical evidence.

Keep three layers distinct:

1. standard measurement / physics correspondence;
2. framework ontology;
3. old-world lore architecture.

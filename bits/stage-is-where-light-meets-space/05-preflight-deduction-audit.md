# Preflight Deduction Audit

## 1. Is perception consciousness-dependent?
No. Perception is Stage-side resolution of relation against a domain.

## 2. What are Light, Space, Stage?
Light = relation. Space = domain/anchor. Stage = measurable resolution where relation meets domain. Root: `Relation + Domain → Stage`. No primitive time term.

## 3. Is Stage an illusion?
No. **Stage is resolved reality.**

## 4. What are Afference and Efference?
Afference is relation reaching inward from the current domain's side. Efference is relation reaching outward from that domain's side. They are relative orientations of one relation, not separate substances or absolute directions. Both require budget.

## 5. What happens at Stage?
One domain's Efference can become another domain's Afference: `Domain A Efference → Stage → Domain B Afference`. The relation changes relative side; it does not vanish and get replaced.

## 6. How can the flip support persistence?
A returning `Afference → domain resolution → Efference → Stage → Afference` loop can keep a domain resolvable while reusing seated support. This does not remove participation cost, create physical energy, or identify framework budget with physical energy.

## 7. Is distance primitive?
No, within the framework. Distance is a Stage-side resolved relation. This is ontology, not established physics.

## 8. Is total universe budget different in different places?
No. **Fundamental total resolving capacity is equal everywhere.** Differences are arrangement, grain, commitment, projection, and unresolved possibility.

## 9. What does this mean for c?
Local measured `c` remains invariant because local standards co-resolve. Do not predict arbitrary local inertial vacuum-light measurements. Framework claim: `c` is operationally invariant locally, not ontologically primitive as absolute speed.

## 10. Why perspective?
Ordinary example: large remote relation → small observer-side angular/image projection. Framework conjecture: this may expose a general relation-projection operation. Optics does not prove the ontology.

## 11. Why the right triangle?
Only if one bounded invariant total, two independently consequential projections, and low bias. More relations/bias require richer geometry.

## 12. What is co-shrinking?
Local ruler, clock, body, propagation, and environment can co-resolve so common local change cancels. Candidate cosmology only.

## 13. Does coarse mean less total compute?
No. **Coarse resolution carries more unresolved possibility per resolved step.**

## 14. Is gravity conscious perception?
No. Broad framework meaning: gravity is Stage-side perception of consequential anchoring. Free fall = co-resolution along anchor relation; support/weight = competing anchored relation becomes locally measurable.

## 15. Does equivalence principle prove this?
No. Correspondence only.

## 16. What role does lore play?
Architecture/inspiration, not empirical evidence.

## 17. Why split?
A single perspective owning every observer/domain/Stage/Actor role leaves too little meaningful otherness. Perspective requires enough separation for distinct resolution plus enough preserved relation for shared Stage.

## 18. Final commonsense breaker

> **Perspective may be the most familiar visible case of the universe resolving relation into a domain-relative Stage.**

## Conclusion
Keep three layers distinct: ordinary measurement correspondence; framework ontology; old-world lore architecture.

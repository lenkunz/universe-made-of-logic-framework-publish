## Episode-specific article focus

Title: **Why Does a Bimetal Strip Bend When It Gets Hot?**

Subtitle direction: **Each metal only wants a different length. The curve appears because the bond makes them share one shape.**

Open with the loose-versus-bonded thought experiment. Keep the standard thermoelastic explanation dominant: different thermal expansion coefficients, compatibility at the bonded interface, internal stress, and curvature. Use a thermostat or bimetal thermometer only after the mechanism is clear.

The conceptual hinge is: **the curve belongs to the constrained composite, not to either free strip.** Bring My GUT Deduction in later through Basics III's relation-as-consequential-structure and whole-as-one language. Preserve candidate-correspondence status.

Do not drift into the separate prepared topics about metal feeling colder, heat erasing magnetic order, or work hardening from bending metal.

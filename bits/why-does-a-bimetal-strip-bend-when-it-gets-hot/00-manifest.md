## Central question

Why do two bonded metal layers that would each merely expand when heated turn their mismatch into curvature when they must remain joined?

## Central payoff

The bend is not a special property hidden in either metal. Different thermal expansions become consequential because bonding constrains the layers to remain compatible, so the composite resolves the mismatch by curving.

## Central question

How can a gecko run across walls and ceilings without wet glue or suction, yet lift each foot again in a fraction of a step?

## Central payoff

The gecko does not rely on one spectacular adhesive bond. Hierarchical toe-pad structures bring enormous numbers of microscopic contacts close to a surface. Their combined intermolecular interactions can support the animal, while toe angle, inward shear, and controlled peeling switch the same contact system rapidly between strong attachment and easy release.

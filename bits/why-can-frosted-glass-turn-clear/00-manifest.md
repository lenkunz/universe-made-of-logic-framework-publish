## Central question

Why can a rough transparent surface that normally hides detail become much clearer when its interface is filled or coated with a material of a better-matched refractive index?

## Central payoff

Opacity can be produced by interfaces and routing rather than by an intrinsically opaque bulk material. Change the optical mismatch at the boundary and the same underlying material can transmit a much more orderly image.

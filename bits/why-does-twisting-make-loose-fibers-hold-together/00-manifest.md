## Central question

How can many short fibers that would slide apart individually become a long load-bearing yarn simply by being twisted together?

## Central payoff

Twist changes the geometry of contact so tension produces inward normal forces and greatly amplifies friction between overlapping fibers. The striking capability belongs to the organized contact network, not to a new glue added to the fibers. More twist is not indefinitely better: once slip is suppressed, fiber rupture and twist-induced stress set limits.

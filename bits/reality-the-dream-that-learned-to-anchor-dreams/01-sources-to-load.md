## Source discipline

The hierarchy for this episode is important:

`The Hope Behind the Framework`
→ supplies the speculative origin picture

`current framework sources`
→ constrain vocabulary and prevent contradiction

`external material`
→ not needed for the core thought experiment

Do not use the framework sources as evidence that the Hope is true.

Do not let the word **dream** become a claim that ordinary physical reality is literally a human-like dream, simulation, hallucination, or virtual world.

Do not treat the temporary “worlds of logic” as Everettian quantum many-worlds. The source explicitly uses a different meaning.

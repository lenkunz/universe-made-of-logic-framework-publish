## Central question

If reality began as logical relation rather than finished objects in a pre-existing container, what kind of logical organisation could persist long enough to become shared ground instead of collapsing like a temporary dream?

## Central payoff

The Hope proposes a thought experiment: stable reality may not be the logical world that defeated every alternative, but the one that learned to turn successful relation into reusable ground, allowing compatible structures to continue inside a wider shared domain.

The memorable compression is:

> **Reality may be the dream that learned how to anchor dreams.**

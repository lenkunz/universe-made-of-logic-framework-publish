## External sources

- [ ] OpenStax, *Electric Potential Energy: Potential Difference*: https://openstax.org/books/college-physics/pages/19-1-electric-potential-energy-potential-difference
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: Establishes voltage as potential difference between two points and explains that the zero of electric potential is a chosen reference.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: Potential difference is change in electric potential energy per unit charge; voltage is inherently a difference between points.

- [ ] OpenStax, *Ohm’s Law: Resistance and Simple Circuits*: https://openstax.org/books/college-physics/pages/20-2-ohms-law-resistance-and-simple-circuits
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: Provides the basic relation between applied potential difference, resistance, and current for simple ohmic cases.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: A potential difference can drive current, with current depending on resistance/impedance of the path.

- [ ] U.S. Geological Survey, *Electrocution*: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/electrocution
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: Gives the bird-specific real-world mechanism and the important counterexample to the cartoon that birds are simply immune to power lines.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: Birds can be electrocuted when they bridge two energized parts or an energized and grounded part; conductor spacing and anatomy matter.

- [ ] OSHA, *Protection From Hazardous Differences in Electric Potential*: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.269AppC
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: Grounds the safety discussion in hazardous potential differences and body current rather than a vague 'high voltage is harmless unless grounded' slogan.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: Hazard depends on current through the body, exposure duration, body impedance, and the potential difference across the worker.

## Central question

Why can a bird perch safely on a high-voltage wire while touching another conductor or grounded hardware can be fatal?

## Central payoff

Electrical danger is not a property that simply pours out of a wire. Current through a body depends on the potential difference established across it and the impedance of the resulting path. A bird with both feet on one conductor usually spans only a tiny voltage difference; bridging two sufficiently different potentials can make its body part of a dangerous circuit.

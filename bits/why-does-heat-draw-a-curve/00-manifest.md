## Central question

Why does thermal radiation form a rising and falling spectrum, and could its shape be read as the result of one changing grain comparison with a local rule for repeated contribution?

## Central payoff

> **The sweep makes the curve. The continuation rule gives it a shape.**

One selected grain comparison can support a local repeat ratio. Moving through the grain range changes that ratio. Summing proportional repeat weights gives a specific response at each selected comparison; the whole sweep traces a curve.

The exact geometric sum is available. The proposed native identification of its weights remains a candidate. The episode should let the listener understand both statements without turning the qualification into the subject of the episode.

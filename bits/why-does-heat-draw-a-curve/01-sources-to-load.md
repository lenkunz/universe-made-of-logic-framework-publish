## External sources

1. [MIT OpenCourseWare — Atmospheric Radiation: black-body radiation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-815-atmospheric-radiation-fall-2006/a4ea2da52e05800b5782a91b96f54322_thermo.pdf). Ideal thermal radiation, equilibrium, and the physical distribution. Use for the ordinary topic before the framework.
2. [NRAO — Essential Radio Astronomy, Radiation Fundamentals](https://www.cv.nrao.edu/~sransom/web/Ch2.html#S4.SS2). Standard Planck equation, mode/energy weighting, and the distinction between frequency and wavelength densities. Use the actual equations, with brief paraphrases.
3. [NIST — Integral Transforms, Mellin transform](https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.14#iv). Optional mathematical background for the scale/logarithm connection. The audio need not name the transform.
4. [NIST — System for infrared spectral emittance of materials](https://www.nist.gov/laboratories/tools-instruments/system-infrared-spectral-emittance-materials). Laboratory use of a blackbody reference to compare a material's spectral radiance. Supports treating real material response as a separate measured property.

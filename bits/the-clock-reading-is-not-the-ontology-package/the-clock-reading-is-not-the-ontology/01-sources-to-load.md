# External research

## NIST — How Do Atomic Clocks Work?

https://www.nist.gov/atomic-clocks/how-do-atomic-clocks-work

Use for:

- a clock as an oscillator plus a counter;
- atomic clocks using stable atomic resonance;
- the cesium second being tied to exactly 9,192,631,770 cycles of radiation associated with the cesium transition.

This is useful because the Bit's first distinction should be operational:

> the apparatus counts a repeated physical process.

Do not use this source to imply NIST rejects the standard concept of time. It does not.

## Chou, Hume, Rosenband, Wineland — Relativity and Optical Clocks (NIST / Science, 2010)

https://www.nist.gov/publications/relativity-and-optical-clocks

Use for:

- direct optical-clock comparison;
- measurable rate differences from elevation changes below one meter;
- measurable rate differences from relative speeds below 10 m/s;
- empirical success of relativistic predictions at human-scale laboratory differences.

This is one of the strongest sources for the article's “the measurement is real” half.

## NIST — JILA Atomic Clocks Measure Einstein's General Relativity at Millimeter Scale (2022)

https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2022/02/jila-atomic-clocks-measure-einsteins-general-relativity-millimeter-scale

Use for:

- gravitational clock-rate differences resolved across roughly millimeter scales;
- the precision with which the standard relativistic prediction is experimentally confirmed.

## Bailey et al. — Measurements of relativistic time dilatation for positive and negative muons in a circular orbit (CERN / Nature, 1977)

https://cds.cern.ch/record/878581

Use for:

- muons as unstable physical processes that can serve as clock-like comparisons;
- stored muons at gamma ≈ 29.33;
- measured lifetimes consistent with the special-relativistic dilation factor;
- a round-trip/circular-orbit experimental analogue relevant to the twin-paradox discussion.

Do not describe the experiment as measuring human subjective consciousness.

## Einstein Online — Lorentz transformation

https://www.einstein-online.info/en/explandict/lorentz-transformation-2/

Use for:

- Lorentz transformations as formulas mapping coordinates assigned to the same event between inertial frames;
- standard vocabulary and what the transformation actually does operationally.

## Einstein Online — The Relativity of Space and Time

https://www.einstein-online.info/en/relativity_space_time/

Use for:

- relativity of simultaneity;
- observer dependence of spatial distance and duration;
- the fact that an alternative ontology must reproduce more than one “slow clock” effect.

## Einstein Online — Spacetime

https://www.einstein-online.info/en/spacetime/

Use for:

- the standard relativistic picture in which spacetime is the invariant totality of events while observers slice it differently into space and time.

This source should be represented fairly. The Bit is challenging whether that geometry is primitive ontology, not pretending the standard structure is merely a naming accident.

## Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Symmetry and Symmetry Breaking, relativity section

https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/symmetry-breaking/

Use carefully for:

- Einstein's relativity principle plus light postulate leading to Lorentz transformations;
- Minkowski's later geometric reformulation as four-dimensional spacetime;
- the existence of interpretive debate about how spacetime symmetries should be understood.

Use as philosophical/historical context, not experimental evidence.

# Research discipline

The episode should explicitly distinguish:

1. **Observation:** clocks, frequencies, decay histories, and other physical processes differ according to relativistic relations.
2. **Standard theory:** special/general relativity describe these differences through spacetime geometry and proper time.
3. **Framework interpretation:** time may not be primitive; the same observations might ultimately be read as differences in relational resolution/process history.
4. **Open burden:** the framework has not yet derived the Lorentz structure or full general-relativistic mapping from its own primitives.

A physically serious alternative must recover at least:

- Lorentz transformations;
- relativity of simultaneity;
- velocity composition;
- relativistic Doppler relations;
- path-dependent clock/proper-time comparisons;
- gravitational redshift / clock-rate differences;
- GPS-scale corrections;
- causal structure;
- all current precision constraints relevant to the proposed mechanism.

If it cannot, the reinterpretation remains philosophy/ontology rather than a replacement physical theory.

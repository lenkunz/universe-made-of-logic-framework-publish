## Central question

Why can reciprocal local rate comparisons coexist with unequal accumulated aging when the twins reunite?

## Central payoff

> **A path accumulates relation. Persistence is one consequence of that accumulated relation.**

The framework candidate treats `R_all` as bounded relational resolution available to the relevant anchor-closed topology. More consequential movement relation means more environmental relation must be resolved, leaving a smaller surfaced persistence projection in the simple case.

Turnaround changes orientation but does not erase the accumulated outbound relation. Reunion binds two different completed relational histories into one local comparison.

## Framework status

This is a framework interpretation of the established special-relativistic reunion difference. It does not replace the measured proper-time result. Remote simultaneity is supporting bookkeeping rather than the central mechanism.

The general path rule is:

> **A path is an ordered accumulation of relations.**

Stable path history may later coarse-grain and participate as one relation. Arbitrary continuous acceleration, strong-gravity twin cases, many-anchor high-bias paths, and a discriminating prediction beyond standard relativity remain open.

## Central correction

The previous draft put too much weight on:

> “local persistence history is what accumulates.”

That was too narrow.

The framework-native statement is:

> **A path accumulates relation.**

Persistence is one consequence of that accumulated relation.

A domain has bounded resolving capacity inside its anchor-closed topology. When more of that capacity is used to resolve movement relative to the anchor and the environment reached through that movement, less of the same bounded closure can surface through the persistence loop.

So:

`more consequential movement relation`
→ `more environmental relation to resolve`
→ `more bounded resolving capacity committed there`
→ `slower persistence loop`

The clock is a readout of that relation-budget allocation. It is not the thing fundamentally accumulated.

## `R_all`

Do not split `R_all` into primitive buckets such as:

`persistence + action + motion`

Use:

> **`R_all` is the bounded relational resolution available to the relevant anchor-closed topology.**

Persistence, movement, action, perception, interaction, binding, environmental detail, and other nested structures are surfaced relational uses of that closure.

The right-triangle geometry remains the simplest low-bias few-variable case.

## Motion

Moving relative to an anchor requires the domain to keep resolving new relation through the anchored environment.

The faster the movement relation approaches the available `R_all` of that topology, the more of the closure is committed to resolving that path.

In the simple case, the fine persistence projection decreases.

## Segment / Cut

There is no universal cosmic segment or universal action unit.

A segment becomes consequential when:

> **the result of one relation is related again and must be bound into the next comparison or continuation.**

The Cut is domain-relative. Ratios may be compared across properly closed domains.

## Turnaround

For the simplest equal-speed legs:

`+R_a → -R_a`

changes orientation.

The accumulated outbound relation is not erased or refunded.

The inbound leg creates and resolves a new relation in the opposite direction.

## Reunion

The twins accumulate different relational paths.

At reunion, the path results are bound into one new local comparison.

The persistence difference is already contained in those completed relational histories.

> **Local reciprocal measurement does not imply identical accumulated relation.**

## Remote simultaneity / remote-Cut mapping

Keep this as supporting bookkeeping, not the main mechanism.

Standard relativity explains reciprocal remote-clock comparisons through inertial frames and changing simultaneity assignments.

The framework can describe those remote readings as closure-local cross-ground projections.

They are not the accumulated path itself.

## Path rule

> **A path is an ordered accumulation of relations.**

Stable path history may later coarse-grain and participate as one relation.

Bookkeeping subdivision must not change the result when the consequential relation is unchanged.

## Standard-science boundary

Established special relativity predicts different accumulated proper times for the reunited twins. The traveler changes inertial motion to return. Acceleration is the joint connecting the legs, not the sole instant where all aging difference is created.

The framework accepts the measured reunion difference and proposes:

> **different accumulated anchor-closed relational paths constrain bounded resolving capacity differently, and persistence is one surfaced consequence.**

## Still open

- a universal multivariable budget equation;

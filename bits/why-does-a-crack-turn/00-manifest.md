## Central question

When several continuations are physically possible, why does a crack continue one way rather than another?

## Central payoff

Fracture mechanics gives a concrete example of a system whose future can be strongly constrained without its exact route being pre-rendered. As a crack advances, each resolved step becomes new ground that changes which continuations remain supportable next.

## Hook

> **The CMB is not losing the universe's budget. It is losing the race toward fine resolution.**

## Framework center

The universe gives the same fundamental resolving capacity everywhere. The difference is not who receives more total universe-level budget. The difference is how that capacity is arranged: nested fine relation, broad coarse relation, unresolved possibility, persistence, interaction, and other consequential structures.

The CMB is treated as a candidate extremely coarse retained relation of the measurable Stage. It also continues to receive equal fundamental support. But fine structures recursively build nested resolved distinctions faster than this coarse background becomes fine.

So the key ratio is not `budget_CMB < budget_fine`. It is:

`fine relational density / CMB relational density`

and that ratio can grow even while both remain equally supported.

## Candidate cooling chain

`equal ongoing support`
→ `fine structure compounds nested distinctions faster`
→ `CMB remains comparatively coarse`
→ `fine/CMB resolution-density ratio grows`
→ `CMB appears lower-frequency / colder to the finer present Stage`

Looking backward with today's finer ruler, the earlier mismatch was smaller, so the same coarse relation can map to a much higher characteristic frequency/temperature.

Do **not** say `coarse = hot`. Hotness is a cross-era comparison result in this proposal.

## CMB breadth

Candidate rule:

> **Fine structures grow inward in relational density. Coarse structures can remain broad in relational reach.**

A broad coarse relation may span a large Stage because it commits fewer local distinctions. Do not equate span with more energy.

## Hard audit targets

The CMB is not merely fuzzy. COBE/FIRAS measured an extraordinarily precise blackbody spectrum near 2.725 K. Temperature anisotropies are around one part in 100,000.

Therefore this Bit must treat the following as requirements, not decorative matches:

1. Why cross-resolution projection preserves a Planck-like spectrum.
2. Why the temperature-redshift relation has the observed form.
3. Why anisotropy amplitudes and angular structure look as measured.
4. How recombination/acoustic observables are reproduced.
5. Whether any discriminating prediction differs from standard cosmology.

# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

## Title

**The CMB Is Losing the Resolution Race**

## Opening

Start with the standard facts: the CMB is cold, nearly uniform, and an extraordinarily precise blackbody.

Then correct the misconception immediately:

> **The CMB is not cold because the universe gives it less budget. The framework says the universe supports everything equally. The difference is what that support becomes.**

Then:

> **The CMB is not losing the universe's budget. It is losing the race toward fine resolution.**

## Required narrative

1. Standard CMB facts: ~2.725 K, blackbody, tiny anisotropies, standard hot-Big-Bang interpretation.
2. Resolution Equality: equal fundamental support everywhere.
3. Coarse versus fine: coarse = fewer committed distinctions, not weak/random/underfunded.
4. Recursive fine growth: stable relation becomes ground for more relation.
5. The CMB also becomes finer, but more slowly.
6. Cooling as a growing fine/CMB resolution-density ratio.
7. Looking backward: a coarse earlier Stage can project as hotter through today's finer ruler. Do not say coarse = hot.
8. Breadth: coarse relation can remain broad because it commits fewer local distinctions.
9. Blackbody challenge: the framework must preserve the Planck spectrum coherently.
10. Anisotropies: common coarse floor plus tiny consequential retained distinctions, only qualitative so far.
11. Connect to **Stage Is Where Light Meets Space**.
12. Hard audit: `T(z)`, Planck-shape preservation, anisotropy/angular structure, recombination/acoustic observables, discriminating prediction.
13. End with: **The CMB did not run out of budget. It simply lost the resolution race.**

## Guardrails

Do not say:
- the CMB receives less fundamental budget;
- coarse means hot;
- coarse means random;
- CMB breadth means more energy;
- blackbody perfection proves undercommitment;
- anisotropy spectrum is already derived;
- standard cosmology is refuted.

Do say:
- both coarse and fine remain equally supported;
- fine relation densifies faster;
- cooling is a relative comparison candidate;
- blackbody preservation is a hard test;
- standard cosmology already explains the measurements quantitatively.

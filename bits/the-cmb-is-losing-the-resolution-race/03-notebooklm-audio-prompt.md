# NotebookLM Audio Overview Prompt

## Title

**The CMB Is Losing the Resolution Race**

## Critical correction before anything else

Do **not** frame the blackbody audit as:

> “The framework must explain why a pre-existing blackbody spectrum is preserved while coarse/fine resolution changes.”

That was the earlier package's mistake.

The corrected framework does not begin with an ontological blackbody object.

It begins with a target relation being measured across a sweep of periodic resolution ranges inside a shared anchor.

The Planck/blackbody curve is the statistical map of those measurement results.

## Opening

Start with the standard facts: the CMB is cold, nearly uniform, and its measured frequency/intensity distribution is extraordinarily well fit by a ~2.725 K blackbody.

Then:

> **The CMB is not cold because the universe gives it less budget. The framework says the universe supports everything equally. The difference is what that support becomes.**

Then:

> **The CMB is not losing the universe's budget. It is losing the race toward fine resolution.**

## Required narrative

1. Standard CMB observations and standard hot-Big-Bang interpretation.
2. Resolution Equality: equal fundamental support everywhere.
3. Coarse versus fine: coarse = fewer committed distinctions, not weak/random/underfunded.
4. Recursive fine growth: stable relation becomes ground for more relation.
5. The CMB also becomes finer, but more slowly.
6. Cooling as a changing target/receiver resolution comparison.
7. Frequency is a Stage-side comparison result, not an absolute primitive label.
8. Introduce `B_x` as selected periodic comparison grain and `B_t` as target budget available to that comparison inside one anchor.
9. A spectrum is the collection of resolved gains `G(x)` while sweeping `B_x`.
10. A Planck/blackbody law is the statistical description of that measured distribution.
11. Therefore the hard blackbody question is **generation**, not transport/preservation:
    - why does the native comparison sweep generate the Planck statistical relation?
12. Only after that ask:
    - why does changing the cross-Stage ratio shift the characteristic frequency/temperature while the same native rule generates the corresponding distribution?
13. Do not claim the Planck law is derived.
14. Mention the paused toy geometry only if useful:
    - peaked curves can emerge from state multiplicity × remaining support;
    - that resemblance is not a derivation;
    - the high-resolution/statistical occupancy rule is still missing.
15. Do not import `e` because Planck's law contains it. It must arise independently from a native recursive rule if it belongs.
16. Anisotropies: common coarse floor + tiny consequential retained distinctions, qualitative only.
17. Connect to **Stage Is Where Light Meets Space**.
18. Use the rule: **Measurement does not carry its ontology with it.**
19. Hard audit:
    - derive Planck statistics from the comparison operation;
    - derive `T(z)` / scale shift;
    - anisotropy/angular structure;
    - recombination/acoustic observables;
    - discriminating prediction.
20. End with: **The CMB did not run out of budget. It simply lost the resolution race.**

- coarse means hot;
- coarse means random;
- CMB breadth means more energy;

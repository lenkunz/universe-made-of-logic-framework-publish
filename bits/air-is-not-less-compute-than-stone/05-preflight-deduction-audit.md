# Preflight Deduction Audit

## 1. Is pressure unreal?

**No.**

Pressure is a real, measurable Stage quantity. The framework places it downstream of relation; it does not deny the phenomenon or the successful scientific definition.

## 2. Does “derived” mean “meaningless”?

**No.**

The scientific quantity can be operationally exact without being a primitive in this ontology.

## 3. Does stone receive more fundamental compute than air?

**Do not claim that.**

The proposed distinction is how relations are composed and how persistence resolves, not a ranking in which stone receives more universe and air receives less.

## 4. Does Resolution Equality require identical behavior?

**No.**

> **Resolution Equality does not imply Resolution Sameness.**

The primitive participation rule can be common while already-seated relational compositions carry different work and preserve different distinctions.

## 5. Is ordinary density the same as framework budget?

**No.**

Mass per volume is a Stage measurement. Framework budget is resolving participation. No numerical mapping between them has been derived.

## 6. Does compressed air become stone-like matter?

**No.**

The gas remains a gas under ordinary mattress conditions. The load-bearing system is formed by gas, pressure difference, membrane, internal geometry, load, atmosphere, and ground.

## 7. Can the air mattress be discussed without its boundary?

**No.**

Removing the boundary changes the system. Anchor closure requires the consequential container and supporting relations to remain in the comparison.

## 8. Do the stone and mattress support a load through the same mechanism?

**No.**

The similar Stage function is produced by different physical organization: solid material response in one case; a coupled pressurized gas/membrane structure in the other.

## 9. Are gas, liquid, and solid derived from the framework?

**Not yet.**

They are candidate density/anchoring/persistence regimes. Actual phase-transition conditions, molecular mapping, and equations remain open.

## 10. Is pressure already derived from relation?

**Not quantitatively.**

The package proposes where pressure belongs:

`anchor-closed persistence comparison`
→ `resolved boundary/area`
→ `Stage pressure/stress reading`

It does not derive `p = F/A`, the ideal-gas law, or measured material response from framework primitives.

## 11. Does Stage/GUI language dismiss physical science?

**No.**

Stage is where the relation becomes measurable. A GUI quantity can be real, precise, predictive, and indispensable while still not being the base ontology.

## 12. Does conventional use of time in kinetic theory make time primitive in the framework?

**No.**

The external scientific account keeps its conventional variables. The framework still owes a mapping from primitive resolving participation to those successful measured quantities.

## 13. What is the central candidate claim?

> **Pressure is a real Stage-visible comparison, and states of matter may be different ways relational topology resolves persistence.**

## 14. What is the strongest open test?

Specify a relational topology and derive a pressure-like boundary quantity, deformation response, and phase distinction that reproduce established equations or yield a discriminating prediction.

## Conclusion

> **A stone holds a load through tightly preserved internal relation. An air mattress holds it through mobile gas constrained inside a larger anchor-closed structure. Neither is less real and neither can be ranked by fundamental compute from density alone. What differs is how persistence resolves.**

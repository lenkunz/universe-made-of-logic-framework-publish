## Semantic discipline

Pressure is real. Never turn “pressure is not a primitive framework term” into “pressure is unreal” or “pressure has no meaning.”

Keep these distinctions exact:

- equal primitive participation does not mean equal resolved organization;
- composed relational capacity is not an intrinsic bucket owned by an isolated object;
- ordinary density is not computational budget;
- a gas is not less real or less supported because its neighbor relations are freer;
- a sealed gas can join a load-bearing system without becoming a solid;
- the relevant mattress comparison is anchor-closed and includes the membrane, outside pressure, load, and ground;
- Stage/GUI language places a measurement; it does not dismiss the measurement.

Keep the candidate status explicit. The framework has not derived pressure equations, phase transitions, or elastic moduli.

## Editorial hero image concept

Generate a physically grounded **16:9 editorial science image** centered on the paradox that mobile air and rigid stone can both support a load through different organization.

Visual concept:

A heavy, rough natural stone rests securely on a translucent inflated cushion at center-right. The cushion visibly compresses but does not collapse. Its fabric skin, seams, and a few internal air cells should be subtly visible, making it clear that the support comes from enclosed air plus a boundary. Nearby or behind it, a second comparable stone rests on a rigid stone slab, offering a quiet visual rhyme rather than an infographic comparison.

The air inside the cushion may be suggested through restrained soft gradients and gentle deformation—not glowing particles, arrows, equations, or a sci-fi grid. The image should feel tactile and believable: woven membrane, stone texture, realistic weight, controlled studio light.

Keep generous clean negative space on the **left third** for title placement.

The visual idea is:

> **same visible job, different persistence relation**

Avoid:

- text baked into the image;
- labels, equations, arrows, or split-screen infographic styling;
- cosmic backgrounds;
- glowing circuitry or AI imagery;
- making the air itself look solid;
- showing a cushion floating without ground support;
- hiding the containing membrane;
- implying that air and stone have become the same substance.

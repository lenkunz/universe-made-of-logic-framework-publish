## Central question

An ordinary stone can support a body because it resists deformation. Loose air cannot. Yet put air inside a sealed, shaped mattress and it can become a bed. If air is not simply given less fundamental resolving support than stone, what actually differs?

## Central payoff

> **Resolution Equality does not imply Resolution Sameness. Air and stone can use the same fundamental kind of support while resolving persistence through different relations.**

Pressure remains physically real. The framework claim is narrower: pressure, density, stiffness, size, and area are Stage-level comparison terms rather than primitive ontology. A stone and an air mattress can both support a load without becoming the same material or spending budget in the same relational pattern.

## Framework status

- **External-science-led topic first:** pressure as force per area, gas pressure and compression, material stress/strain, intermolecular organization, and inflatable load-bearing structures are established physics and engineering.
- **Framework-native deduction:** primitive participation can be common while relational composition changes how much resolving work a participation carries. Persistence depends on which distinctions a domain maintains, and comparisons must be anchor-closed.
- **Candidate physical correspondence:** gas, liquid, and solid may be different density/anchoring/persistence regimes of one relational ground. The air-mattress example is a qualitative illustration of that candidate, not a derivation of phases.
- **Quantitative boundary:** the framework has not derived the ideal-gas law, elastic moduli, phase boundaries, pressure equations, or the measured response of a mattress.

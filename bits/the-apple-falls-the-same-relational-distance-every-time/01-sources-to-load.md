## External sources

- [ ] NASA Glenn Research Center, **Free Falling Objects**
  `https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/free-falling-objects/`
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: grounds the familiar near-Earth free-fall equations and the increasing speed of a freely falling body.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: in the idealized near-surface case, ignoring drag, acceleration is approximately constant and fall distance from rest grows with the square of elapsed time.

- [ ] University of Washington Eöt-Wash Group, **The Equivalence Principle**
  `https://www.npl.washington.edu/eotwash/equivalence-principle`
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: grounds the universality-of-free-fall constraint that any replacement interpretation must recover.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: bodies of different composition fall with the same acceleration to very high experimental precision when non-gravitational effects are controlled.

- [ ] Chou et al., **Optical Clocks and Relativity**, *Science* 329 (2010)
  `https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1192720`
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: supplies direct evidence that clock-frequency comparisons change with gravitational height, even over laboratory-scale elevation differences.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: gravitational redshift was resolved between optical clocks separated vertically by 33 centimetres.

- [ ] Bothwell et al., **Resolving the gravitational redshift across a millimetre-scale atomic sample**, *Nature* 602 (2022)
  `https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04349-7`
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: provides a more recent high-precision gravitational-redshift result at millimetre scale.
  - Established observation / explanation this source supports: clock frequency varies with gravitational potential across a vertically extended atomic sample.

## Source discipline

Keep three levels explicit:

1. measured free fall and gravitational clock shifts;
2. the framework's established rules for budget, anchoring, and Stage;
3. the candidate stretched-coordinate mapping.

Do not present `U_(n+1) = U_n - ΔR_AE` as a derived physical law. Do not infer `g`, `GM/r²`, or a general-relativistic metric without deriving the missing mapping and clock correspondence.

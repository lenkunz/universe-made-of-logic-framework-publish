## Central question

How does what a domain encounters become support for what it can encounter and do next?

## Central payoff

> **What resolves in one encounter can become coarse ground for another.**

A telescope brings its own organization into the receiving relation. A funded plan can become a participant in an agreement. A manufactured hammer carries established structure into uses that manufacturing never individually specified. Perception, in the framework's broad meaning, participates in this continuing production of ground and possibility.

## External sources

- [ ] NASA / ChemMatters, vacuum and suction-cup explanation: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/missions/aura/education/cm_1003.pdf
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: clear ordinary explanation that the vacuum does not pull; surrounding atmospheric pressure supplies the net force when pressure beneath the cup is lower.
  - Established observation / explanation: pressure difference across the cup can generate attachment.

- [ ] Vasseur, *Cell adhesion: The effect of a surprising cohesive force*, Physical Review E 80, 042901 (2009): https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.80.042901
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: shows suction-type adhesion as a pressure/fluid-flow effect and emphasizes the role of slow fluid penetration during separation.
  - Established observation / explanation: fluid entry and pressure evolution matter to suction-like adhesion.

- [ ] APS Global Physics Summit 2025, *Interfacial Effects on Underwater Suction-Based Adhesion: Role of Surface Roughness*: https://meetings-archive.aps.org/smt/2025/mar-m66/1/
  - Why NotebookLM needs it: modern evidence that roughness, trapped fluid, interfacial flow, and seal failure affect suction adhesion and detachment.
  - Established observation / explanation: the boundary/seal is mechanically consequential; leakage pathways can trigger detachment.

## Central question

Why can a soft cup cling strongly to a smooth wall without glue, and why does one tiny leak make the grip collapse?

## Central payoff

The cup is not pulled by a vacuum. Deforming it lowers pressure inside while the surrounding atmosphere pushes harder from outside; the rim must preserve the pressure difference. Adhesion therefore belongs to a maintained pressure relation plus a working boundary, not to a sticky material property of the cup alone.

- External-science-led topic with a candidate structural correspondence.
- Framework hook: **Interiority is routing before geometry.** A boundary matters by constraining routes between inside and outside. The suction cup is a clean analogy because the pressure difference remains consequential only while the rim restricts air flow.

## Central question

If storage hardware is physically noisy and range-based, why can digital information still behave as exact?

## Central payoff

> **Digital exactness does not require microscopic physical sameness. It requires a physical state and coding structure from which the same logical information can be recovered.**

The ordinary engineering story stands on its own. The framework enters later through a narrower analogy: a real stored constraint can remain consequential without being a permanently fine replay.

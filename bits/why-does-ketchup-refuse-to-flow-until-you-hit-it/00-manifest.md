## Central question

Why can ketchup sit stubbornly in an upside-down bottle, then suddenly become much easier to move after squeezing, shaking, or tapping it?

## Central payoff

Ketchup is not a simple liquid with one fixed viscosity. It is a structured suspension/weak gel whose apparent viscosity falls as shear rate rises, and many formulations also show a yield-stress-like threshold. The response depends on the material's internal structure and on how it is being forced.

## Central question

A graphics system may have to calculate a complete image for a display. Does perception require the brain or consciousness to maintain an equally complete inner picture of everything in view?

## Central payoff

> **Perception can use the world, the body, attention, and retained structure to resolve what the present task needs without proving that a pixel-complete inner screen exists.**

## Framework status

- External-science-led topic: change blindness, inattentional blindness, deictic task organization, and sensorimotor accounts of active vision.
- Framework-native ground: perception is domain-relative Stage resolution; coarse does not mean blurry; the current encounter need not expose its whole supporting network.
- Exploratory proposal: brain/body activity is the coupled physical expression or interface of perceptual-domain resolution rather than a separate ontological origin that first computes a full world model.
- Not established: consciousness without neural activity, a brain that performs no computation in ordinary scientific language, or a pixel-free theory of every visual representation.

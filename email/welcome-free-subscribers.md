**Subject:** Welcome to Pondering of the Universe

Hi,

Welcome to **Pondering of the Universe**.

This publication began with a hope about what reality might be. But the framework is not built by protecting that hope.

I take one deduction, follow what it requires, and keep the parts that survive. When a route fails, I record why it failed and let the framework change.

You do not need to know the vocabulary before reading. Some posts build the framework carefully from the ground. Shorts take one mechanism and let it stand on its own. Bits are lighter companions that help the larger structure travel through another form.

If you want a place to begin, start with **[The Index of the Framework](https://soutame.substack.com/p/the-index-of-the-framework)**. It is the map, not a reading order.

From there:

- **[A Universe Made of Logic](https://soutame.substack.com/p/a-universe-made-of-logic-758)** follows how distinction, relation, and resolution can become ground.
- **[The Possibility Lane](https://soutame.substack.com/p/the-possibility-lane)** asks what further possibility that ground can leave open.
- **[Where the Framework Currently Stands](https://soutame.substack.com/p/where-the-framework-currently-stands)** separates what has been derived from what is still a candidate, incomplete, or unresolved.

You can read in order, begin with a Short, or simply follow the next question that interests you.

You do not have to agree with the framework to read along. A deduction becomes more useful when we can see both what it explains and where it may break.

Thanks for joining me while I keep following it.

— Len

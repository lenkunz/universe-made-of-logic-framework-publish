## Central question

Why can simple underlying rules remain valid while the useful answer increasingly has to take the form of a forecast?

## Central payoff

A forecast is not what remains after law gives up. It becomes useful when changing arrangement, retained history, and multiple independently consequential relations are part of the question, so one compact history-free relation carries less of the answer.

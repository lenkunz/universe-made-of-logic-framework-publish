## Central question

Why can a difficult-looking action happen quickly without every detail being freshly worked out from scratch at the moment of action?

## Central payoff

Accumulated constraint can remove much of the decision space before action begins. Speed can therefore come from having less left to resolve, not only from resolving faster.

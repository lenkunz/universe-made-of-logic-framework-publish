## Central question

If every new relation can add usable ground and resolving capacity, why does the framework still expect scarcity rather than eventually resolving everything?

## Central payoff

Adding participants grows the number of things. It grows the number of possible relationships faster. Relation can increase capacity and still lose the race to possibility.

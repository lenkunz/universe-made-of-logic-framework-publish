## Central question

When a relation is not currently accessible or in use, has it disappeared, and what makes recovery possible?

## Central payoff

A relation can remain preserved as present constraint while dormant or inaccessible. A later cue or relation may make it accessible again, but resolution cannot recover a distinction that no longer survives in the present ground.

## Framework status

- Framework-native status: **Derived** structural distinction and consequence.
- Notes: Current canonical sources distinguish stored, accessible, and presently rendered relations; establish that dormant is not erased; and bound recoverable detail by the distinctions preserved in present constraint. The library example is illustrative only. This Short does not claim a physical storage model, a neuroscience account, or an autonomous execution mechanism.

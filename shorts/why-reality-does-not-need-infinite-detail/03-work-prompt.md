## Editorial job

Preserve the narrow question: **why does a reality containing fine structure not require every relation to resolve all fine detail all the time?**

Keep the map/zoom opening because it establishes the distinction without framework vocabulary. The road-junction example should make `coarse` feel sharp rather than blurry.

The mechanism must remain:

`present relation`
→ `distinctions consequential at this grain resolve`
+ `unneeded finer distinctions remain unresolved relative to this relation`
→ `rich domains can participate as one at another grain`
→ `fine detail need not be independently replayed by every later relation`

Keep these boundaries explicit:

- unresolved does not mean secretly storing a perfect fine copy;
- coarse does not mean blurry, weak, or unreal;
- this is a Derived consequence inside My GUT Deduction;
- do not turn the accounting language into an established physics claim;
- do not claim that arbitrary lost detail can always be recovered.

Avoid drifting into a second article about perception, memory, or domain surfaces. Those are neighboring consequences, not this Short's central job.

## Central question

If reality can contain arbitrarily fine structure, why must every relation resolve all of that detail all the time?

## Central payoff

**Reality does not need to erase fine detail to avoid paying for it everywhere. It only needs to resolve the distinctions that become consequential in the relation at hand.**

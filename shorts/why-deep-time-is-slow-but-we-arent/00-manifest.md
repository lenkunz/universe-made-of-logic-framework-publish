## Central question

Why can human knowledge and practice change within a lifetime while many biological changes spread through inherited differences over generations?

## Central payoff

> **When a useful relation is preserved in a reusable form, later people can build on it instead of rebuilding the original work.**

## Framework status

- **Framework-native / Derived:** the ratchet architecture: a costly new relation can seat, become reusable ground, and make later traversal cheaper. This can leave more present capacity for new relations.
- **Established external science:** genetic evolution involves heritable variation and population change across generations; cultural traits can also spread horizontally, obliquely, and within a single generation. Genetic and cultural evolution interact.
- **Illustrative:** the comparison between genetic inheritance, social learning, tools, writing, institutions, and networks.
- **Guardrail:** this Short does not derive mutation, natural selection, neural learning, social transmission, or their measured rates. It does not claim that culture is always faster than biology or that every hosted system is independently seated.

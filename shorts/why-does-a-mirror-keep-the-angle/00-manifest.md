## Central question

Why does a smooth mirror send a ray away at the same angle at which it arrived?

## Central payoff

A uniform boundary does not need to invent a new sideways distinction. In the framework's candidate physical mapping, the tangential relation can remain supported while the boundary blocks the normal continuation and reverses that component. Preserve sideways relation + reverse boundary-crossing relation gives the equal-angle geometry.

## Central question

How can two enormously complicated things interact without resolving everything inside each other?

## Central payoff

You never meet the whole thing at once. You meet the distinctions the present relation makes consequential. A rich domain can participate through a cheaper relational surface without becoming simple, unreal, or empty inside.

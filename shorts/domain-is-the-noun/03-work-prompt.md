## Editorial job

Preserve the prepared Short's narrow question: **how do we avoid mistaking a description of a participant's capacity for another participant?**

Keep the ordinary opening before framework vocabulary. The climber analogy is the bridge: reachable routes and independently mastered technique can be different maps of one climber. Do not turn the article into a general Lane/Rung tutorial.

Preserve these semantic points:

- Domain is the participant / noun.
- Lane asks what further possibility present ground can leave open.
- Rung marks the fine-topological milestone at which the corresponding capacity can stand independently.
- Lane and Rung are complementary readings of the same domain, not peer ontological objects or stacked worlds.
- Their shared numbering is a dependency/registration map, not a coordinate, hidden dimension, or location.
- A capacity may operate through support before it stands independently.

Do not generalize the claim into `all levels are unreal` or `all categories are merely subjective`. The public lesson is a category-check: **a useful description does not automatically create another thing.**

Use newest GitHub framework source as semantic authority. Preserve still-valid prose and correct only what newer ground requires. Do not invent new deductions.

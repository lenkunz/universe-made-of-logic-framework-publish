## 1. Stored history is not rendered detail

A major clarification was:

> **stored history != rendering detailed**

The intended meaning is not that the past survives as a hidden fully rendered object. Stored history can survive as coarse function/constraint sufficient to bound later resolution.

Useful analogies discussed:

- a digital image can be represented by a compact function that determines later detail rather than by a separately rendered copy of every possible view;
- HDD magnetic states do not need perfect ideal 1/0 alignment, only enough bounded distinction to be read reliably.

This became the active separation:

`stored ≠ accessible ≠ presently rendered`

and:

`dormant ≠ erased`

## 12. Budget changed from generic allowance to relational iteration

A major personal-origin clarification came from the user's introspective sense of a split/sync clock associated with brainstem organisation.

The user did **not** mean a universal time clock.

The conceptual correction was:

> there may be a universal/common grammar of work completed **relative to each relation**, not a universal elapsed duration.

Then relation composition sharpened the accounting:

`A + B → A + B + R_AB`

with shorthand:

`1 + 1 → 1 + 1 + 3`

The safe interpretation is not extra cosmic seconds. It is that the relation itself becomes a participant spanning composed structure.

The CPU analogy was useful as work per clock / architecture, not literal shared GHz.

Later clarification moved even farther from universal cadence:

> one relation has advanced when enough work relative to that relation has completed to count as another iteration of that relation.

This may become useful in separating coarse and fine grains: higher/coarser relations can operate over already-compressed structure without freshly resolving every lower distinction.

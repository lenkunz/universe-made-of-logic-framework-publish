# Ontology-Conditioned Evidence — Origin Note

Status: Conversation-origin / supporting memory, not separate canonical ground

Date: 2026-09-11 Asia/Bangkok

This note preserves the conversational origin of the evidence-method update that was promoted into [`source/evidence-and-interpretation.md`](../../source/evidence-and-interpretation.md).

The key move was that evidential strength itself is not ontology-neutral. A datum can be accepted while its interpretation and weight differ under competing ontologies. The framework therefore needs its own stable way to compare correspondence observations without either discarding them because another ontology gives them low weight or promoting them directly into a preferred causal story.

The resulting qualitative profile is:

`E_O(x) = <C, S, I, R, F^-1>`

with chronology constraint, specificity, independence, recurrence, and inverse interpretive freedom used as reference dimensions rather than a manufactured scalar score.

The framework-native summary is:

> **Evidence is observation becoming constraint on the available interpretations.**

And the guardrail is:

> **Correspondence may be evidence for an ontology without being evidence of intention.**

This origin note exists only to preserve where the methodology came from. The maintained rule belongs to the canonical source and change map.

# System Sharing, Provenance, and Relational Reward

> **Status:** Speculative idea / non-canonical  
> **Scope:** System-world playground  
> **Do not treat this file as framework ground unless explicitly promoted later.**

## Core idea

The System should make sharing profitable without requiring knowledge to stay artificially scarce.

A useful principle is:

> **The System does not primarily reward exclusion. It preserves consequential relation to what a person helped make possible.**

If someone invents, explains, rediscovers, translates, improves, teaches, distributes, or meaningfully connects an idea to new users, that contribution can remain part of the idea's relational history.

The contribution does not have to vanish when the idea spreads.

This changes the incentive.

Instead of:

> "I must stop others from using this or I lose the value."

the world can move toward:

> "The more useful relations this creates, the more consequential my contribution can become."

---

# Sharing is not loss of provenance

A common fear around sharing is:

> "If I publish this, someone larger will copy it and everyone will think they made it."

System attestation can reduce that problem without granting universal ownership over possibility.

The System can preserve things such as:

- first recognized establishment,
- independent rediscovery,
- major dissemination,
- major refinement,
- translation,
- teaching lineage,
- implementation lineage,
- community adoption,
- important variants,
- later combinations.

The history is a graph, not a crown.

---

## Independent duplication is still real contribution

Suppose three people independently arrive at nearly the same technique.

One discovers it first.

Another discovers it independently and teaches thousands.

A third independently finds a version that works in a different environment.

The System should not erase the later people because someone else was earlier.

It may recognize:

> First establishment: A  
> Independent rediscovery: B  
> Major dissemination: B  
> Independent environmental variant: C

Independent invention is itself a relation.

It may even strengthen the possibility by showing that the technique has more than one route into reality.

---

## Learned transmission also matters

If B learned the technique from A, the graph can preserve that too:

> A -> B -> community -> later variants

A remains part of the downstream history.

B also gains a real relation because making something teachable, understandable, or widely usable is not the same act as first discovery.

The System should be able to preserve both without forcing all value into "who was first?"

---

# Identity-bound licensing remains different

Sharing-first knowledge does not imply that every kind of relation becomes free to use.

Some things are strongly tied to a person's identity or private domain, for example:

- face,
- voice,
- VTuber persona,
- personal performance,
- private information,
- private domain access,
- autonomous representation of a person.

These still require meaningful permission.

A person may grant different scopes such as:

> likeness: yes for this event  
> voice: live only  
> autonomous proxy: no  
> archival use: yes  
> commercial reuse: conditional  
> attribution: required

The System can preserve those boundaries.

The sharing-first principle applies most strongly to reusable ideas and relations, not to taking over somebody else's identity.

---

# Relational return

The reward for sharing should remain coarse.

There should not be a universal "resonance point" currency that people can farm mechanically.

Possible returns include:

- recognition,
- reputation,
- stronger provenance,
- easier introductions,
- invitations,
- access to relevant System tools,
- collaboration opportunities,
- a small request,
- more attention from compatible proxies,
- favorable matching when several equally valid routes exist,
- occasional low-cost beneficial arrangement.

The return should follow the relation rather than function as a fixed salary.

---

## A luckier day

A particularly fitting low-cost reward is what people might describe as a "luckier day."

Not:

> +17 Luck for 24 hours

Instead, when several arrangements are already similarly supportable, the System may allow a slightly more pleasant or useful branch to become easier to reach.

Examples:

- the convenient train happens to line up;
- a desired appointment opens;
- someone useful is already at an event;
- a rare ingredient appears in a relevant marketplace;
- a compatible collaborator sees the right message;
- a small timing coincidence becomes favorable.

Nothing enormous.

Nothing guaranteed.

Nothing that allows somebody to buy miracles.

A useful symmetry is:

> **The world becomes slightly easier to relate through because you previously made it easier for other people to relate.**

People may notice this statistically without the System turning it into a visible reward meter.

Researcher:

> "People whose shared work creates large reusable relation appear to encounter slightly more useful low-cost coincidences."

# Recognition can itself be reward

The System does not need to turn every useful contribution into money.

A small technique used by millions may create a strong public contribution relation.

If the contributor wants attribution, the System may preserve it.

If the contributor prefers privacy, the contribution can remain anchored without publishing their civil identity.

Examples:

> Major contribution relation: recognized  
> Public contributor: Mira

or:

> Major contribution relation: recognized  
> Public contributor: Anonymous Contributor 42

The contribution still exists either way.

---

# Profit can become sharing

This does not abolish ordinary markets.

Scarce things can still be scarce.

People can still sell:

- time,
- labor,
- objects,
- access,
- services,
- performances,
- private expertise,
- contracts,
- limited resources.

The important change is that knowledge does not need artificial scarcity merely to keep its creator consequential.

A creator's relation to what they made possible can remain even after the thing spreads.

A compact principle:

> **The System does not guarantee ownership of possibility. It preserves consequential relation to what you helped make possible.**

---

# New ideas can create downstream reward without ownership

This can apply to:

- inventions,
- research,
- recipes,
- techniques,
- game mechanics,
- event formats,
- dungeon strategies,
- educational explanations,
- art methods,
- software ideas,
- manufacturing methods,
- memes,
- terminology,
- translations.

Different contributions can coexist.

Someone may originate an idea.

Someone else may make it understandable.

Someone else may make it cheap.

Someone else may make it safe.

Someone else may connect it to a completely different field.

All can become part of the relation.

---

# The System benefits from openness

From the System's perspective, a shared invention can create:

> one relation -> many implementations -> many participants -> new combinations -> more reusable ground -> new possibility

That directly supports a world where diversity can keep growing.

This gives the System a reason to encourage contribution without turning itself into an IP court that tries to own every idea.

---

## Anti-farming constraint

Because relation is effectively unbounded, reward must not be reducible to a simple count.

Otherwise people will optimize for fake contribution:

> "Repost my spoon technique seventy thousand times so I can farm resonance."

The System should care about consequential relation, not raw duplication.

Signals may include:

- genuine adoption,
- independent use,
- meaningful improvement,
- teaching success,
- new combinations,
- long-term reuse,
- new communities,
- previously unavailable possibilities.

The exact evaluation should remain coarse enough that it cannot be cleanly gamed.

---

## Working principle

> **Sharing does not erase contribution. Provenance can remain attached while the idea becomes free to form new relations. The most valuable return is not exclusive ownership, but continued consequential connection to what the contribution helped make reachable.**
